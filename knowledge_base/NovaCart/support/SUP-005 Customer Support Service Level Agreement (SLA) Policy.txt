Purpose
This document establishes the binding Service Level Agreement (SLA) performance standards, operational timelines, and compliance requirements for NovaCart Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "NovaCart"). It defines mandatory First Response Times (FRT), Target Resolution Times (TRT), Maximum Resolution Times (MRT), pause/restart logic, and breach escalation pathways for all customer support operations.
As a core operational policy (SUP-005) in the NovaCart Enterprise Knowledge Base (NEKB), SUP-005 works in direct alignment with SUP-001, SUP-003, and SUP-004 to guarantee operational velocity, accountability, and consistent service delivery across all supported operating regions (India, Singapore, UAE).
Scope
This SLA policy applies universally to:
* All Level 1 (L1), Level 2 (L2), and Level 3 (L3) Customer Support Representatives (CSRs) and automated conversational agents.
* Cross-functional resolution units in Logistics, Warehouse Operations, Returns & Refunds, Finance, Information Security, Product Management, Legal & Compliance, and Engineering.
* All customer interaction channels integrated within the Omni-Channel Support Portal (crm.novacart.internal).
SLA Objectives
NovaCart enforces strict SLAs across customer service workflows to achieve four strategic objectives:
1. Predictable Velocity: Maintain binding performance thresholds so customers receive timely initial responses and definitive resolutions.
2. Operational Accountability: Track time-to-resolution metrics dynamically across every department and functional queue.
3. Proactive Breach Interception: Automate system alerts to escalate aging tickets before compliance breaches occur.
4. Data-Driven Optimization: Analyze SLA compliance metrics to eliminate operational bottlenecks in logistics, payments, software engineering, and partner carrier workflows.
Service Hours
Customer support channels operate under standardized regional operating windows:
* Live Chat & App Messaging (Digital): 24/7 / 365 across all operating regions (IN, SG, UAE).
* Voice Telephony: 08:00 to 22:00 Local Regional Time (IST, SGT, GST), Monday through Sunday.
* Web Ticket & Email Queue (Asynchronous): 24/7 / 365 intake; queue processing active 24/7 for P1/P2 incidents, and 08:00 to 20:00 Local Regional Time for P3/P4 tickets.

	Resolution Time Standards
Resolution SLA performance is governed by two distinct metrics:
* Target Resolution Time (TRT): The operational timeframe within which $90\%$ of tickets in a given category must be fully resolved under standard business conditions.
* Maximum Resolution Time (MRT): The absolute, non-negotiable upper limit for ticket closure before a mandatory SLA breach is logged and executive escalation is triggered.

	SLA Monitoring Process
SLA tracking is managed automatically by crm.novacart.internal via continuous real-time monitoring:
1. Timer Activation: The SLA clock starts immediately upon ticket creation and timestamp logging.
2. Visual SLA Indicator: Support agent dashboards display color-coded countdown indicators for active tickets:
   * Green: SLA consumption $< 50\%$ of target time.
   * Yellow: SLA consumption between $50\%$ and $75\%$ of target time (Warning threshold).
   * Orange: SLA consumption between $76\%$ and $99\%$ of target time (Imminent breach alert).
   * Red: SLA consumed $\ge 100\%$ (Active breach state).
3. Automated Interception Alerts: When a ticket reaches $75\%$ SLA consumption without resolution, the system issues an automated priority alert to the floor supervisor and domain squad lead.

	Customer Notification Standards
To preserve trust during extended investigations, CSRs must issue proactive status updates at defined intervals:
* P1 Incidents: Mandatory customer progress updates every 1 hour until resolution.
* P2 Incidents: Mandatory customer progress updates every 4 hours until resolution.
* P3/P4 Tickets: Mandatory customer progress updates every 24 hours until resolution.

	Performance Metrics
Customer Support operational performance is evaluated monthly against five Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):
1. First Response Time (FRT) Compliance Rate: Percentage of incoming contacts receiving an authorized first response within channel SLA limits (Target: $\ge 95\%$).
2. Target Resolution Time (TRT) Compliance Rate: Percentage of tickets fully resolved within applicable TRT targets (Target: $\ge 90\%$).
3. SLA Breach Rate: Percentage of total volume exceeding Maximum Resolution Times (Target: $< 2\%$).
4. First Contact Resolution (FCR) Rate: Percentage of tickets resolved during initial contact without secondary follow-up (Target: $\ge 75\%$).
5. Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT): Post-interaction survey rating across resolved tickets (Target: $\ge 88\%$).
Business Rules
Rule 1: SLA Pause Conditions
The SLA resolution timer within crm.novacart.internal may be placed in a PAUSED state only under the following verified conditions:
* Pending Customer Action (WAITING_ON_CUSTOMER): The agent has formally requested necessary evidence (e.g., photo of damaged goods, bank statement proof) and is awaiting customer response.
* Pending Third-Party Vendor / Courier Action (WAITING_ON_CARRIER): The ticket requires physical carrier trace logs from external 3PL logistics partners (Refer to SHIP-006).
* Pending Technical Diagnostic (WAITING_ON_TECHNICIAN): On-site technician inspection is scheduled for large home appliances (CAT-APP) (Refer to PROD-004).
Rule 2: SLA Restart Conditions
The SLA timer resumes immediately upon receipt of requested customer evidence, carrier update logging, or technician report submission. The system recalculates remaining resolution allowance automatically without resetting the initial creation timestamp.
Rule 3: Unilateral SLA Alteration Prohibited
Agents and squad leads are strictly forbidden from manually modifying ticket creation dates, altering SLA timers, or switching ticket status to PAUSED without logged evidence. Unauthorized timer manipulation constitutes a serious compliance violation audited by Quality Assurance.
Exception Handling
* Force Majeure & Major Outages: During severe weather disruptions, national logistics strikes, or major cloud infrastructure outages, the Chief Operating Officer (COO) may authorize a temporary "Global SLA Relaxation State." During this period, public system banners notify customers of extended response windows, and SLA breach penalties are suspended for affected regions.
* Customer Non-Responsiveness: If a ticket remains in WAITING_ON_CUSTOMER status for 72 consecutive hours despite three automated reminder notifications, the system automatically marks the ticket as RESOLVED_NO_RESPONSE. The SLA timer closes without logging a breach.
SLA Examples
The following practical scenarios illustrate SLA application and timer behavior:
Example 1: P1 Account Compromise
* Event: A customer reports an unauthorized login and altered delivery address via live chat at 10:00 AM.
* SLA Execution:
   * FRT Target: $< 30\text{ seconds}$ (Achieved at 10:00:20 AM).
   * TRT Target: $< 2\text{ hours}$ (Target: 12:00 PM).
   * Action: L1 CSR freezes account instantly and escalates to QUEUE_SECURITY_SOC. Security analyst revokes active tokens, cancels fraudulent shipment, and issues password reset by 11:15 AM (Resolved in 1 hour 15 minutes; TRT met).
Example 2: P2 Duplicate Payment (SLA Pause Event)
* Event: Customer submits an email ticket at 08:00 AM stating card was debited twice (₹15,000 each) for one order.
* SLA Execution:
   * FRT Target: $< 30\text{ minutes}$ (Achieved at 08:18 AM).
   * TRT Target: $< 12\text{ hours}$ (Target: 08:00 PM).
   * Action: Finance Specialist reviews gateway logs at 09:00 AM but requires customer's bank reference number. Status updated to WAITING_ON_CUSTOMER at 09:15 AM (Timer PAUSED at 1 hour 15 minutes consumption). Customer replies with bank reference at 01:15 PM (Timer RESUMES). Refund executed and ticket resolved at 04:15 PM (Total active SLA time consumed: 4 hours 15 minutes; TRT met).
Example 3: P2 Delivery Delay Breach Management
* Event: A customer submits a chat ticket regarding a shipment 48 hours past ETA.
* SLA Execution:
   * FRT Target: $< 30\text{ seconds}$ (Achieved).
   * TRT Target: $< 12\text{ hours}$; MRT: $24\text{ hours}$.
   * Execution Path: Ticket assigned to Logistics at 09:00 AM. Carrier trace encounters delays. At 06:00 PM (75% of TRT), Level 1 Alert alerts floor supervisor. At 09:00 PM (100% of TRT), Level 2 Alert auto-promotes ticket to Logistics Hub Manager. Manager issues immediate replacement order from local FC at 11:30 PM (Resolved in 14 hours 30 minutes; TRT breached, MRT met).
Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
Q1: Does the SLA clock reset if a ticket is transferred between departments?
Answer: No. The SLA clock relies strictly on the original ticket creation timestamp. Departmental transfers move ticket custody within crm.novacart.internal but do not reset or extend the overarching TRT/MRT countdown timers.
Q2: How do customer response delays affect SLA compliance reporting?
Answer: Placing a ticket in WAITING_ON_CUSTOMER status pauses the SLA clock. Time spent waiting for customer evidence is excluded from agent handling calculations, ensuring SLA compliance metrics reflect active operational work accurately.
Q3: What happens if an agent incorrectly pauses an SLA timer to prevent a breach?
Answer: Quality Assurance regularly audits paused tickets (WAITING_ON_CUSTOMER / WAITING_ON_CARRIER). If an audit reveals an agent paused a ticket without logging an outbound customer inquiry or carrier trace request, the pause is revoked, the SLA is logged as breached, and the event is recorded as a quality non-compliance finding.
