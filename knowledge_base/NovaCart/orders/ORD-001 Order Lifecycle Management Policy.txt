Purpose
This document establishes the official enterprise governance framework for managing the complete lifecycle of customer orders within NovaCart Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "NovaCart"). It defines the sequential stages, state transition logic, system interactions, departmental ownership boundaries, and operational rules governing orders from initial placement through payment validation, inventory reservation, warehouse picking and packing, shipping handover, delivery, post-delivery return window closure, and final archival.
As a foundational operational policy (ORD-001) in the NovaCart Enterprise Knowledge Base (NEKB), ORD-001 enforces deterministic order execution across human operators and backend microservices, ensuring full compatibility with COMP-001 through COMP-004, SUP-001 through SUP-005, PAY-001, SHIP-001, and REF-001.
Scope
This policy applies universally across all operating territories (India, Singapore, UAE), commercial channels (B2C Retail, B2B SMB, Corporate Enterprise Accounts, NovaCart Prime Memberships), and underlying software engines managing checkout and fulfillment pipelines.
Coverage Includes
* Order state transitions within the core Order Management System (api.novacart.internal/oms).
* Departmental handoffs across Sales, Finance, Warehouse Management, Logistics, Customer Support, and Returns.
* Event-driven notifications generated for customers and merchant sellers.
* Operational exception pathways including inventory stock-outs, payment gateway drops, delivery failures, split fulfillment, and post-delivery returns.
Order Lifecycle Overview
The NovaCart order lifecycle is governed by an automated, event-driven state machine managed by the core Order Management System (OMS). Every order moves through distinct processing phases designed to ensure transactional integrity, real-time inventory precision, and verifiable logistics execution.

Primary Lifecycle Phases
1. Ingress & Authorization: Order checkout initiation, price calculation, fraud screening, and payment authorization.
2. Inventory Allocation: Hard reservation of Stock Keeping Units (SKUs) in localized Fulfillment Center (FC) databases.
3. Fulfillment Execution: Warehouse pick, pack, weight audit, serial number logging, and staging.
4. Logistics & Transit: Carrier pickup, middle-mile linehaul, regional hub sorting, and last-mile out-for-delivery routing.
5. Final Settlement: Delivery confirmation via digital Proof of Delivery (POD), return window countdown, merchant settlement, and final archiving.

	Order Creation Process
1. Cart Submission: The customer submits a purchase request via web or mobile application interfaces.
2. Tax & Delivery Calculation: The platform calculates real-time regional taxes (GST in IN, VAT in UAE), shipping fees based on delivery speed (Standard, Express, Same-Day), and applicable promotions.
3. Master Order Generation: The OMS writes a Master Order ID (e.g., NC-IN-88201) to the primary transactional database in api.novacart.internal with an initial status of PENDING_PAYMENT.
Payment Verification
1. Gateway Handshake: For prepaid orders (Credit/Debit Card, UPI, Net Banking, EMI, Wallets), the payment gateway attempts financial clearing (Refer to PAY-001).
2. Fraud & Risk Screening: Parallel anti-fraud algorithms screen the transaction for velocity spikes, card-not-present risk, and IP mismatches (Refer to SEC-004).
3. State Transition:
   * Success: Payment gateway returns AUTHORIZATION_SUCCESS. The OMS updates status to PAYMENT_CONFIRMED.
   * Failure: Gateway returns AUTHORIZATION_FAILED. OMS retains status as PENDING_PAYMENT for 15 minutes to allow customer payment re-attempt. If uncompleted after 15 minutes, OMS transitions order to CANCELLED (Reason: PAYMENT_TIMEOUT) (Refer to PAY-002).
   * COD Validation: Cash on Delivery orders bypass electronic clearing but undergo automated phone OTP verification or customer historical COD acceptance audit before moving to PAYMENT_CONFIRMED.
Inventory Reservation
1. Sourcing Optimization: Upon reaching PAYMENT_CONFIRMED, the OMS sourcing logic evaluates customer delivery address against FC inventory levels (BLR-01, DEL-02, BOM-01, CCU-01, SIN-01, DXB-01, AUH-01).
2. Split Order Evaluation: If items within a multi-item order reside in separate FCs, the OMS partitions the Master Order into distinct Sub-Order IDs (e.g., NC-IN-88201-A and NC-IN-88201-B) (Refer to ORD-005).
3. Hard Lock Execution: The WMS locks specific item quantities in physical storage bins. Order status transitions to INVENTORY_RESERVED.
4. Stock Unavailability Trigger: If inventory audit reveals a stock mismatch (physical stock missing), WMS issues an immediate STOCK_UNAVAILABLE exception event to OMS.
Order Confirmation
1. Confirmation Messaging: Once inventory is successfully reserved, OMS generates an automated email, SMS, and in-app push notification to the customer containing order breakdown, sub-order tracking IDs, itemized tax invoice, and estimated delivery dates (ETA).
2. Seller / Warehouse Alert: The sub-order details populate on active picking workstations within designated FCs.
Warehouse Processing
1. Wave Picking: Warehouse personnel receive digital wave pick tasks on handheld terminals (hht.novacart.internal). Status transitions to PROCESSING.
2. Serial Number Registration: For high-value categories including Consumer Electronics (CAT-ELE) and Mobile Devices (CAT-MOB), picking staff scan unique item IMEI/serial numbers, permanently binding them to the Sub-Order ID in WMS (Refer to COMP-001, PROD-001).
3. Packaging & Weight Audit: Items pass to packing stations where tamper-evident packaging is applied. Automated inline scales verify package gross weight against catalog SKU expectations. Status updates to PACKED.
4. Labeling: Shipping labels containing destination barcodes, routing codes, and masked customer PII are affixed. Status transitions to READY_FOR_DISPATCH.
Shipment Handover
1. Carrier Manifesting: The Transportation Management System (TMS) assigns staged packages to scheduled linehaul transport or direct 3PL courier pickups (Refer to SHIP-001, SHIP-002).
2. Dock Scanning: Courier driver scans package barcodes at FC dispatch docks, transferring custody from Warehouse Management to Logistics.
3. State Transition: Status updates to SHIPPED. TMS activates public tracking milestones and calculates dynamic courier ETA.
4. Last-Mile Routing: Upon parcel arrival at regional destination hubs, TMS assigns packages to delivery riders. Status transitions to OUT_FOR_DELIVERY.
Delivery Completion
1. Verification at Doorstep: Delivery rider arrives at customer location and requests verification via time-stamped One-Time Password (OTP) sent to customer mobile device or recipient digital signature.
2. Delivery Confirmation: Successful verification writes a digital Proof of Delivery (POD) record to TMS.
3. State Transition: Status updates immediately to DELIVERED. OMS triggers customer delivery confirmation alerts.
Order Closure
1. Return Window Holding Period: Orders remain in DELIVERED status for the duration of the category-specific return window (7 days for CAT-ELE/CAT-MOB; 10 days for CAT-APP) (Refer to REF-001).
2. Transition to Completed: If no return request (RMA), defect report, or delivery dispute is initiated prior to return window expiration:
   * OMS updates order status to COMPLETED (Terminal State).
   * Financial ledger releases merchant escrow funds and triggers seller commission settlement.
   * Order record is archived for long-term audit and tax compliance.

	Rule 2: Non-Modifiability of Shipped Orders
Once an order state transitions to SHIPPED, customer-initiated cancellations or delivery address modifications via self-service applications are strictly locked. Address changes post-dispatch require L2 Logistics intervention (Refer to SHIP-007).
Rule 3: Immutable Terminal States
The statuses COMPLETED, CANCELLED, and REFUNDED represent absolute terminal states. Once an order reaches a terminal state, its status cannot be reopened or transitioned to an active state. Any secondary dispute requires creating a new administrative ticket ID.
Exception Handling
* Systemic OMS Outages: If the central OMS experiences API database locks, picking terminals fall back to local queue offline caching. Offline pick logs sync back to OMS upon connectivity restoration, preventing fulfillment halts.
* Lost Package Auto-Replacement: If TMS logs no courier tracking updates for 96 hours, OMS flags the parcel as lost, initiates an automatic replacement sub-order dispatching new inventory from the nearest FC, and files an internal insurance claim (Refer to SHIP-006).
Order Lifecycle Examples
Scenario 1: Successful Order Completion
1. Customer purchases a gaming mouse (₹3,499) via UPI checkout at 10:00 AM. Status: PENDING_PAYMENT.
2. UPI authorization clears at 10:00:15 AM. OMS updates status to PAYMENT_CONFIRMED.
3. OMS sources item at hub BLR-01 at 10:02 AM. Status updates to INVENTORY_RESERVED.
4. FC staff picks and packs item by 11:30 AM. Status transitions: PROCESSING $\rightarrow$ PACKED $\rightarrow$ READY_FOR_DISPATCH.
5. Courier scans parcel at 02:00 PM. Status updates to SHIPPED.
6. Delivery rider completes OTP doorstep verification next day at 01:30 PM. Status updates to DELIVERED.
7. Return window (7 days) expires without dispute. OMS transitions order to COMPLETED (Terminal State) and settles funds to merchant.
Scenario 2: Split Order Processing
1. Customer orders a laptop (CAT-ELE) and a refrigerator (CAT-APP) in Master Order NC-IN-99102. Status: PAYMENT_CONFIRMED.
2. OMS identifies laptop at BLR-01 and refrigerator at BOM-01. OMS creates Sub-Orders NC-IN-99102-A (Laptop) and NC-IN-99102-B (Refrigerator).
3. Laptop processes through express air freight (SHIPPED $\rightarrow$ DELIVERED in 24 hours).
4. Refrigerator processes through heavy surface freight (SHIPPED $\rightarrow$ DELIVERED in 72 hours).
5. Master Order reflects PARTIALLY_DELIVERED until Sub-Order B reaches DELIVERED, whereupon Master Order transitions to DELIVERED (Refer to ORD-005).
Scenario 3: Delivery Failure & Return to Origin (RTO)
1. Order dispatched to Dubai customer reaches OUT_FOR_DELIVERY status.
2. Courier attempts delivery; customer is unreachable. Rider logs Non-Delivery Report (NDR) attempt 1 (Refer to SHIP-005).
3. After 3 failed attempts over 3 consecutive days, courier marks parcel as DELIVERY_REFUSED_RTO.
4. Package returns to hub DXB-01. Returns team verifies unopened seal and updates status to RETURNED.
5. Finance processes full item refund minus outbound shipping charges, updating final status to REFUNDED (Terminal State).
Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
Q1: Can a customer cancel an order after it has reached PACKED status?
Answer: Yes. Customers may cancel orders self-service or via Customer Support while in PACKED or READY_FOR_DISPATCH status. WMS receives a recall command, prevents courier handover, unpacks the item back to inventory, and Finance issues a 100% refund (Refer to ORD-002, REF-001).
Q2: What happens to allocated inventory if a customer's payment remains pending?
Answer: Allocated stock is soft-locked for exactly 15 minutes during PENDING_PAYMENT status. If payment verification is not completed within 15 minutes, OMS releases the soft lock back to active platform catalog inventory and cancels the order (Refer to PAY-002).
Q3: Who holds operational authority to manually alter an order status?
Answer: Manual status overrides are restricted to authorized L2/L3 Customer Support, Operations, and Finance Specialists via crm.novacart.internal. Every manual status change requires a mandatory reason code entry and generates an indelible audit log entry linked to the employee's ID.
