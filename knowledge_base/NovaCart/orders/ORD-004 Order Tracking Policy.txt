Purpose
This document defines the official enterprise policy, operational standards, technical integration specifications, and notification rules governing end-to-end shipment tracking across NovaCart Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "NovaCart"). It establishes tracking identifier generation formats, tracking status milestone definitions, update frequency SLAs, automated customer notification workflows, support responsibilities, and exception management for all in-transit parcels.
As a core operational document (ORD-004) in the NovaCart Enterprise Knowledge Base (NEKB), ORD-004 enforces absolute transparency across customer-facing tracking portals, Customer Support Representative (CSR) dashboards, and backend Transportation Management System (api.novacart.internal/tms) engines, operating in strict alignment with ORD-001 through ORD-003, SHIP-001, SHIP-005, SHIP-006, and SUP-001.
Scope
This policy applies universally across all order fulfillment routes across NovaCart operating regions (India, Singapore, UAE), delivery speeds (Standard, Express, Same-Day), third-party logistics (3PL) carrier networks, and direct last-mile delivery fleets.
Coverage Includes
* Generation and assignment of Master Tracking IDs and Sub-Order Tracking IDs.
* Carrier webhook API telemetry and milestone status mapping in tms.novacart.internal.
* Real-time customer tracking interfaces across Web, Mobile Applications, SMS, WhatsApp, and Email channels.
* Tracking exception management including delayed milestones, failed delivery attempts, unlinked tracking numbers, and Return to Origin (RTO) tracking sequences.
Order Tracking Objectives
NovaCart manages order tracking under four primary operational objectives:
1. End-to-End Visibility: Provide clear, real-time tracking visibility from warehouse dispatch staging to physical doorstep delivery.
2. Proactive Customer Transparency: Alert customers automatically regarding transit milestones or carrier delays before the customer initiates a support enquiry.
3. Automated Status Mapping: Normalize disparate 3PL carrier status codes into standardized NovaCart tracking states within api.novacart.internal/tms.
4. Early Interception of Exceptions: Identify stalled shipments or repeated delivery failures early to trigger automated logistics investigations and support interventions.
Tracking Process Overview
Shipment tracking operates as an event-driven data pipeline integrated across Warehouse Management Systems (WMS), Transportation Management Systems (TMS), 3PL carrier APIs, and customer-facing notification portals.

Primary Tracking Pipeline Phases
1. Identifier Generation & Manifesting: Upon order packaging, WMS requests a carrier waybill number and generates a standardized NovaCart Tracking ID.
2. Carrier Handoff & Activation: Physical parcel scan at warehouse loading docks triggers transit tracking initialization.
3. In-Transit Telemetry Ingestion: 3PL carrier webhooks push real-time hub scan updates into tms.novacart.internal.
4. Normalized Milestone Mapping: TMS maps carrier raw data to standardized NovaCart tracking statuses and publishes event payloads to notification services.
5. Delivery Verification & Milestone Closure: Digital Proof of Delivery (POD) registration closes active tracking logs and archives the tracking record.
Tracking Number Generation
Tracking IDs are generated automatically by the OMS/TMS middleware during the warehouse PACKED state transition:
* Format Architecture: Standard Tracking IDs follow a structured 14-character alphanumeric schema: NC + [Region Code 2-Char] + [Julian Date 5-Char] + [Unique Sequence 5-Char] (e.g., NCIN2621088201).
* Sub-Order Mapping: In Split Order scenarios (ORD-005), each Sub-Order ID is assigned a distinct Tracking ID appended with sub-identifiers (e.g., NCIN2621088201-A and NCIN2621088201-B).
* Carrier Waybill Linking: The internal NovaCart Tracking ID is permanently mapped in tms.novacart.internal to the primary 3PL carrier waybill or air waybill (AWB) number (e.g., DHL/FedEx/Delhivery tracking code), allowing dual-lookup capabilities across internal and external portals.

	Customer Tracking Methods
NovaCart provides four primary self-service methods for customers to monitor active shipments:
1. Integrated Web & App Tracking Dashboard: Authenticated users view interactive tracking timelines, real-time rider location maps (for express/same-day orders), and estimated delivery time windows under My Orders.
2. Public Guest Tracking Portal: Unauthenticated users track shipments at [https://track.novacart.com](https://track.novacart.com) by entering their Master Order ID or Tracking ID combined with their registered phone number.
3. Omni-Channel Notification Links: Direct deep-links embedded in SMS, WhatsApp, and Email alerts open instant tracking views without login prompts.
4. Interactive Conversational AI Bot: Automated support bots across Web Chat and WhatsApp answer tracking inquiries instantly using api.novacart.internal/tms/track endpoints.
Carrier Tracking Integration
NovaCart integrates with 3PL carrier networks via an event-driven architecture managed by Engineering and Logistics:
* Webhook Telemetry (Primary): 3PL carriers push tracking events to [https://api.novacart.internal/v1/telemetry/carrier-events](https://api.novacart.internal/v1/telemetry/carrier-events). Event payloads are processed in real-time, normalized to standard tracking statuses, and written to the database within 5 seconds of receipt.
* API Polling Fallback (Secondary): For non-webhook carriers, TMS executes automated polling queries every 60 minutes to fetch active shipment statuses.
* Data Normalization Engine: The TMS contains explicit translation rules converting carrier-specific status strings (e.g., "Arrived at Facility DXB-02" or "Departed Sorting Office") into standardized NovaCart status tags (ARRIVED_AT_SORTING_FACILITY / IN_TRANSIT).

	Business Rules
Rule 1: Mandatory Real-Time Scanning
Every physical transfer of a parcel—warehouse dock handover, linehaul vessel loading, regional hub sorting, and last-mile rider pickup—must be accompanied by an immediate barcode scan. Manual status updates without barcode scan verification are strictly prohibited.
Rule 2: Tracking Information Availability SLA
Tracking information for every order in SHIPPED status must be accessible and active on public tracking portals within 15 minutes of courier dock pickup. If a tracking ID returns a "Not Found" error after 1 hour, TMS triggers an automated alert to Engineering.
Rule 3: Dynamic Delivery Time Window (ETA) Accuracy
Estimated Delivery Dates (ETAs) displayed on customer tracking dashboards must update dynamically based on live transit telemetry. If middle-mile transit delays occur, TMS must recalculate and update the customer-facing ETA automatically within 2 hours of detecting the delay.
Exception Handling
* Carrier Webhook Outage: If a 3PL carrier's webhook service experiences an outage, TMS automatically switches to high-frequency polling (api.novacart.internal/tms/poll) every 15 minutes until webhook service restoration is confirmed.
* Disputed Proof of Delivery (Fake POD): If a customer claims non-delivery for a shipment marked DELIVERED, Customer Support verifies rider geo-location logs and OTP verification records in tms.novacart.internal. If geo-location data reveals the rider was $> 200\text{ meters}$ from the delivery address during scan, the POD is invalidated, and a loss investigation is opened under SHIP-006.
Practical Examples
Scenario 1: Normal In-Transit Tracking Flow
1. Customer orders a laptop in Bangalore. FC packs item and generates Tracking ID NCIN2621088201. Status: LABEL_CREATED.
2. Courier scans parcel at FC dock at 05:00 PM. Status updates to PICKED_UP_BY_COURIER. Customer receives SMS with tracking link.
3. Parcel arrives at destination hub next morning at 06:00 AM. Status updates to ARRIVED_AT_SORTING_FACILITY.
4. Last-mile rider loads parcel at 08:30 AM. Status updates to OUT_FOR_DELIVERY. SMS dispatched with rider name and delivery OTP.
5. Customer provides OTP at doorstep at 11:15 AM. Rider verifies OTP in app. Status updates to DELIVERED (Terminal State).
Scenario 2: Failed Delivery Attempt (NDR Handling)
1. Rider attempts delivery for a package in Dubai at 02:00 PM; customer is at work. Rider logs NDR code CUSTOMER_UNREACHABLE in TMS App.
2. Status updates to DELIVERY_ATTEMPTED. System sends instant WhatsApp alert to customer containing a link to select a new delivery slot or confirm an alternate gate drop instructions.
3. Customer selects "Re-attempt Tomorrow After 05:00 PM" via WhatsApp link.
4. TMS re-manifests parcel for next-day evening delivery. Rider completes delivery next day at 05:30 PM (DELIVERED).
Scenario 3: Stalled In-Transit Shipment (Courier Delay)
1. Parcel shipped from Mumbai to Delhi shows no carrier scans for 30 hours due to highway transit flooding.
2. TMS automated SLA monitor detects missing milestone ($> 24\text{ hours}$). Status updates automatically to DELIVERY_DELAYED.
3. System recalculates ETA (+24 hours) and dispatches automated proactive email to customer explaining transit delay.
4. Logistics Hub Squad opens carrier trace request with 3PL. Carrier clears delay next day; tracking resumes normal milestones to delivery.
Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
Q1: Why does a tracking ID sometimes show "Label Created" for several hours without movement?
Answer: LABEL_CREATED indicates that warehouse staff have packed the order and generated the shipping waybill, but the physical parcel is staged at the FC loading bay awaiting scheduled courier pickup. Courier pickup occurs multiple times daily, and tracking activates upon dock scan (Refer to SHIP-001).
Q2: How can a customer track a Split Order with multiple items?
Answer: When an order is split into multiple sub-orders, each sub-order receives a distinct Tracking ID (e.g., NCIN2621088201-A and NCIN2621088201-B). The customer tracking dashboard displays separate timeline cards for each package, allowing independent tracking (Refer to ORD-005).
Q3: What should a customer do if their tracking status shows "Delivered" but they have not received the parcel?
Answer: The customer should contact Customer Support immediately. CSRs check OTP verification and rider GPS logs in tms.novacart.internal. If an incorrect delivery occurred, Support initiates an urgent driver retrieval or authorizes a replacement order under SHIP-006 and SUP-001.
