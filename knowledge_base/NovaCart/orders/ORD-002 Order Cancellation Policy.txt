Purpose
This document defines the official enterprise policy and operational rules governing order cancellations at NovaCart Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "NovaCart"). It establishes customer eligibility criteria, seller cancellation parameters, automated system cancellation triggers, pre-dispatch vs. post-dispatch handling, approval hierarchies, refund execution, and departmental responsibilities.
As an essential operational document (ORD-002) in the NovaCart Enterprise Knowledge Base (NEKB), ORD-002 ensures deterministic execution across customer self-service portals, Customer Support Representative (CSR) tooling, and automated backend Order Management System (api.novacart.internal/oms) services, working in strict alignment with ORD-001, PAY-001, REF-001, SHIP-001, and SUP-001.
Scope
This policy applies to all purchase cancellations across NovaCart operating regions (India, Singapore, UAE), customer tiers (B2C Retail, B2B SMB, Corporate Enterprise Accounts, NovaCart Prime Memberships), and product categories (CAT-ELE, CAT-MOB, CAT-GAM, CAT-APP, CAT-SMT, CAT-SFT, CAT-GFT, CAT-SUB).
Coverage Includes
* Customer-initiated cancellations via self-service web/app portals or Customer Support.
* Merchant/seller-initiated cancellations due to stock mismatches or fulfillment failures.
* System-initiated automatic cancellations driven by payment time-outs, fraud risk, or unverified Cash on Delivery (COD) orders.
* Partial cancellations for Split Orders and multi-item purchases.
* Approval authorization thresholds for high-value and non-standard cancellation claims.
Cancellation Objectives
NovaCart manages order cancellations under four primary operational objectives:
1. Customer Empowerment: Provide seamless, instant self-service cancellation options for pre-dispatch orders without mandatory CSR intervention.
2. Inventory Reclaim Velocity: Instantly release locked inventory back to active sales channels upon cancellation to maintain catalog precision.
3. Automated Refund Processing: Eliminate administrative delays by triggering automated payment reversals immediately upon cancellation verification.
4. Fulfillment Cost Containment: Intercept packaging and logistics operations prior to carrier handover to eliminate unrecoverable shipping expenses.

	Merchant Cancellation Policy
Third-party marketplace merchants and direct 1P fulfillment centers must adhere to strict cancellation guidelines to maintain catalog integrity and prevent buyer disappointment.
Merchant Cancellation Rules
1. Out-of-Stock Mandatory Cancellation: If a merchant cannot fulfill an order due to an inventory mismatch, they must initiate a merchant cancellation within 6 business hours of order placement.
2. Cancellation Penalty Fee: Merchants that cancel an order due to stock-outs or pricing errors are assessed a merchant penalty fee equal to 5% of the item GMV (minimum ₹200 / $5 / AED 15), automatically deducted from their next payout settlement.
3. Seller Reliability Index Impact: Frequent merchant-initiated cancellations ($> 1.5\%$ of monthly order volume) trigger an automated account review by Sales and may result in catalog suspension.

	Customer Notification Process
1. System Event Trigger: Upon state update to CANCELLED within OMS, an automated event payload is published to the notification service (api.novacart.internal/notify).
2. Multi-Channel Dispatch: The customer receives instant notifications across three channels:
   * In-App Push Notification: Real-time alert confirming cancellation and refund initiation.
   * SMS Alert: Concise transaction summary containing Master Order ID, Cancellation Reason, and Refund Reference Number.
   * Detailed Email: Formal credit memo detailing cancelled items, itemized refund breakdown, original payment instrument, and expected bank reflection window (Refer to PAY-001, REF-001).
Business Rules
Rule 1: Self-Service Pre-Dispatch Cancellation Authority
Customers possess absolute, non-discretionary authority to cancel any order self-service via web or mobile apps while the order status remains in PENDING_PAYMENT, PAYMENT_CONFIRMED, INVENTORY_RESERVED, PROCESSING, or PACKED, without requiring CSR intervention or managerial approval.
Rule 2: High-Value Cancellation Approval Threshold
Any customer cancellation request for an order exceeding ₹100,000 / $1,500 / AED 4,000 in READY_FOR_DISPATCH or SHIPPED status requires digital approval from an L2 Support Specialist or Operations Manager before a manual refund instruction can be submitted to ledger.novacart.internal (Refer to SUP-003).
Rule 3: Split Order Cancellation Isolation
In a Split Order scenario containing multiple Sub-Order IDs (e.g., NC-IN-88201-A and NC-IN-88201-B), cancelling one sub-order isolates the cancellation event strictly to that child record. Remaining sub-orders continue through fulfillment without interruption (Refer to ORD-005).
Exception Handling
* Carrier Intercept Failure: If an L2 Logistics agent issues a transit intercept command for a SHIPPED parcel, but the carrier fails to intercept and completes doorstep delivery, the customer is instructed to refuse delivery upon arrival. If accepted accidentally, NovaCart issues a free reverse pickup label under REF-005.
* System Outage Intercept Delay: If WMS offline caching prevents a real-time cancellation signal from reaching the packing station, and a cancelled item is handed to a courier, TMS flags the tracking barcode upon first carrier hub scan and auto-routes the parcel back to the FC (Return to Origin).
Practical Examples
Scenario 1: Immediate Pre-Dispatch Cancellation
* Details: Customer purchases a gaming monitor (₹28,999) at 02:00 PM. At 02:15 PM, the customer decides to cancel via the mobile application.
* Workflow: OMS verifies order status is INVENTORY_RESERVED. Cancellation executes instantly. WMS releases bin lock. Financial ledger initiates an automated 100% refund (₹28,999) to the customer's credit card. SMS/Email notifications are sent within 30 seconds.
Scenario 2: Merchant Inventory Stock-Out
* Details: Customer orders a smartphone (CAT-MOB). The seller identifies physical stock damage during wave picking.
* Workflow: Merchant logs inventory failure in wms.novacart.internal. System updates status to CANCELLED_NO_STOCK. OMS triggers an immediate 100% refund, issues an automated ₹200 goodwill credit to the customer's account wallet, and assesses a 5% penalty fee against the merchant's payout account.
Scenario 3: Cancellation Request After Dispatch (SHIPPED)
* Details: Customer contacts Live Chat requesting cancellation of a laptop shipped 12 hours prior, currently in transit via air freight.
* Workflow: CSR explains that direct cancellation is locked for SHIPPED status (Refer to SUP-002). CSR initiates a carrier transit intercept request in tms.novacart.internal. The carrier holds the parcel at the destination hub and routes it back to the FC (RTO). Once FC receives the parcel, Finance issues a refund minus outbound shipping charges.
Scenario 4: Partial Cancellation of a Split Order
* Details: Master Order NC-SG-50021 contains a mechanical keyboard (Sub-Order A) and a desktop monitor (Sub-Order B). Sub-Order A is SHIPPED, while Sub-Order B is PROCESSING. Customer requests cancellation of the monitor.
* Workflow: CSR cancels Sub-Order B in crm.novacart.internal. Sub-Order B status updates to CANCELLED, releasing its warehouse lock and triggering a full refund for the monitor's value. Sub-Order A remains SHIPPED and continues normal last-mile delivery (Refer to ORD-005).
Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
Q1: Can a customer cancel an order after it has reached READY_FOR_DISPATCH status?
Answer: Yes. Customers can cancel orders in READY_FOR_DISPATCH status via self-service or CSR. WMS revokes the shipping manifest, prevents courier pickup, unpacks the parcel back to warehouse storage, and Finance issues a 100% refund (Refer to ORD-001, REF-001).
Q2: What happens if a customer attempts to cancel a digital software key (CAT-SFT)?
Answer: Digital software activation keys cannot be cancelled or refunded once the digital code has been revealed or displayed in the customer's account portal. If the key remains unrevealed in system logs, Customer Support can process a cancellation (Refer to REF-006).
Q3: How long does it take for a cancellation refund to reflect in a customer's bank account?
Answer: Refunds issued to NovaCart Wallet reflect instantly. Refunds to Credit Cards, Debit Cards, Net Banking, or UPI instruments are processed by Finance within 24 hours and reflect in the customer's bank account within 3 to 5 business days, depending on issuing bank processing cycles (Refer to PAY-001, REF-002).
