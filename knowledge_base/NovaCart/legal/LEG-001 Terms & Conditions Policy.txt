Purpose
This document defines the official Terms & Conditions Policy of NovaCart Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "NovaCart"). It establishes standardized operational principles, customer eligibility criteria, account responsibilities, acceptable platform usage guidelines, prohibited activity definitions, service availability limitations, and business rules governing customer engagement across NovaCart’s products, services, e-commerce storefronts, mobile applications, and digital platforms.
As the foundational document (LEG-001) in the Legal category of the NovaCart Enterprise Knowledge Base (NEKB), LEG-001 governs customer engagement terms across e-commerce channels, operating in strict operational alignment with COMP-001 through COMP-004, SUP-001 through SUP-005, ORD-001 through ORD-005, SHIP-001 through SHIP-007, PAY-001 through PAY-006, REF-001 through REF-006, PROD-001 through PROD-006, SEC-001 through SEC-006, and LEG-001.
Scope
This policy applies universally to all end-users, registered B2C customers, NovaCart Prime VIP members, B2B merchant partners, corporate accounts, guest checkouts, and platform visitors accessing NovaCart's digital channels across India, Singapore, and the United Arab Emirates.
Coverage Includes
* Platform terms acceptance, electronic agreement binding, and policy update notification protocols.
* Customer eligibility parameters, age restrictions, and corporate account representation standards.
* Customer account ownership principles, credential security duties, and multi-user access restrictions.
* Acceptable use rules, acceptable content posting guidelines, and anti-scraping/anti-bot parameters.
* Prohibited platform activities, fraud indicators, security circumventions, and promotional abuse controls.
* Operational service limitations, inventory availability disclaimers, scheduled maintenance windows, and account suspension/termination workflows.
Out of Scope
* Detailed dispute resolution, court jurisdiction clauses, and litigation procedures (governed by internal legal department guidelines).
* Technical data protection, privacy consent mechanisms, and PII storage schedules (governed by SEC-003).

Core Operational Principles
1. Reciprocal Fair Use: Platform access is provided as a service conditioned upon customer compliance with acceptable use standards and honest commercial intent.
2. Deterministic Governance: All platform interactions—from account creation to checkout, delivery, and post-purchase support—are governed by consistent, transparent operational rules.
3. Proactive Risk Containment: NovaCart reserves the right to enforce immediate protective holds, account suspensions, or service restrictions to mitigate fraud, security threats, or systemic platform abuse (SEC-004, SEC-005).
4. Policy Harmony: Platform usage rules operate in complete synchronization with underlying security (SEC-001 to SEC-006), payment (PAY-001 to PAY-006), refund (REF-001 to REF-006), and product (PROD-001 to PROD-006) policies.
Acceptance of Terms
1. Binding Electronic Acceptance: Acceptance of this policy occurs upon creating a NovaCart account, checking the "I Agree to Terms & Conditions" prompt during checkout, or accessing digital platform services.
2. Policy Version Binding: Users are bound by the version of LEG-001 active at the time of account action or order placement.
3. Modification and Update Standards: NovaCart reserves the right to update this policy periodically to reflect operational, technical, or regulatory changes.
   * Minor Modifications: Operational updates, typographical corrections, or layout enhancements take effect immediately upon publication to the platform.
   * Material Modifications: Significant policy updates affecting customer rights, account fees, or core service conditions are notified to registered users 14 calendar days prior to the effective date via email, SMS, or in-app dashboard banners. Continued use of the platform after the 14-day notice period constitutes acceptance of updated terms.

	Orders, Products & Services
1. Order Offer and Acceptance: The display of products on NovaCart storefronts constitutes an "invitation to offer." An order placed by a customer represents a commercial offer to purchase. Acceptance occurs when NovaCart updates the order status to PAYMENT_CONFIRMED and issues a formal Order Confirmation email (ORD-001).
2. Catalog Description Accuracy: NovaCart strives for absolute product information accuracy (PROD-001). However, minor cosmetic variances in packaging or manufacturer design updates may occur.
3. Order Capping and Quantity Limits: To ensure fair distribution of high-demand merchandise, NovaCart reserves the right to cap maximum purchase quantities per account, per household, or per payment card (PROD-002).
4. Digital Products & Licensing: Purchase of digital software, gift cards, or subscriptions grants a non-exclusive, revocable usage license under PROD-006. Unmasked or revealed digital keys are non-refundable (REF-001).
Pricing & Payment Principles
* Price Currency & Display: All product prices are displayed in local regional currency (INR for India, SGD for Singapore, AED for UAE) inclusive of applicable local taxes (GST/VAT) as detailed in PAY-001.
* Pricing Error Corrections: In the event of an obvious technical or typographical pricing error where an item is listed at a fraction of its retail value due to a system bug, NovaCart reserves the right to cancel unfulfilled orders placed at the erroneous price, issue a 100% full refund via PAY-003, and notify the customer.
* Payment Method Authorization: Customers must utilize authorized, legally owned payment instruments. Transactions flagged for name mismatches or cross-account card sharing trigger automated security holds under SEC-004 and PAY-006.

	Business Rules
Rule 1: Single Identity Binding Rule
An individual customer may register and operate only one primary personal B2C account. Creating multiple synthetic or duplicate accounts to farm promotional discounts, bypass catalog purchase limits, or evade account suspensions constitutes a Tier 2 policy violation resulting in immediate account consolidation, credit revocation (REF-006), and account termination (SEC-004).
Rule 2: Unclaimed Orders and Abandoned Accounts
In-transit orders returned to NovaCart fulfillment centers due to multiple failed delivery attempts or customer refusal without valid justification are processed under REF-002 rules, incurring standard reverse logistics deductions from the final refund.
Rule 3: Record Retention for Policy Acknowledgments
All user policy acknowledgments, term acceptance timestamps, IP logs, and version consent records must be logged continuously in auth.novacart.internal and retained for a mandatory period of 7 years to support operational and legal compliance audits (SEC-003).
Exception Handling
* Corporate Bulk Purchase Pre-Approval: Commercial entities seeking to purchase products in quantities exceeding standard consumer catalog caps (PROD-001) must apply for a Corporate B2B Account. Upon tax ID verification, B2B accounts are granted customized order quantity limits and dedicated invoicing (ORD-001).
* Appeals for Suspended Accounts: A customer whose account has been suspended or terminated for suspected policy violations may file a formal appeal through Customer Support within 14 calendar days of suspension. Security Operations re-evaluates the case file; the decision upon appeal review is final (SUP-003, SEC-004).
Practical Examples
Scenario 1: Automated Scripting and Quantity Bypass Attempt
1. A customer utilizes an automated browser script to bypass catalog purchase limits (max 1 unit) on a limited-release gaming console (CAT-GAM).
2. The script attempts to place 6 consecutive orders across 4 synthetic accounts using the same credit card token (PAY-006).
3. shield.novacart.internal detects Rule 1 violation (multi-account farming) and Tier 1 security violation (automated script traffic).
4. System automatically cancels the unfulfilled orders, voids payment holds (PAY-003), revokes promotional credits, and sets all synthetic accounts to STATUS_TERMINATED.
Scenario 2: Handling Unacceptable User Support Behavior
1. A customer dissatisfied with a out-of-warranty replacement rejection (PROD-003) engages in repeated verbal abuse and profane threats toward Customer Support agents on live chat (SUP-001).
2. The CSR logs a Tier 3 policy violation and escalates the ticket to Level 3 Management via SUP-003.
3. Support Manager reviews chat transcripts, issues a formal written warning, and temporarily restricts the customer's live chat privileges for 30 days, re-routing future inquiries exclusively to formal email channels.
Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
Q1: How am I notified when NovaCart updates its Terms & Conditions Policy?
Answer: Minor operational updates take effect upon publication to our website. For material policy modifications that affect your customer rights or account rules, we provide an advance 14-day notice via email, SMS, or dashboard pop-up alerts before updated terms take effect.
Q2: Can I share my NovaCart Prime membership account with friends or family members?
Answer: NovaCart retail and Prime accounts are non-transferable and designed strictly for individual household or corporate use. Sharing login credentials across unlinked households violates account security policies (SEC-001, SEC-002) and may trigger automated step-up MFA challenges or security suspensions.
Q3: What happens if an item I ordered was listed at an incorrect price due to a system glitch?
Answer: In the rare event of a clear technical pricing error (where an item is displayed at a fraction of its true value due to a system bug), NovaCart reserves the right to cancel unfulfilled orders placed under the error, notify you immediately, and issue a 100% full refund to your original payment method (Refer to PAY-003).
