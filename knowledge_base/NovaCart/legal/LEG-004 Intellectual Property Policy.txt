Purpose
This document defines the official Intellectual Property Policy of NovaCart Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "NovaCart"). It establishes standardized operational procedures, ownership standards, asset protection guidelines, permitted usage rules, infringement complaint workflows, licensing principles, and business rules governing the creation, ownership, protection, licensing, authorized use, and infringement management of NovaCart's proprietary intellectual property and third-party intellectual property hosted across NovaCart platforms.
As a core operational policy (LEG-004) in the Legal module of the NovaCart Enterprise Knowledge Base (NEKB), LEG-004 governs brand assets, software IP, catalog content, and digital rights across all platform channels, operating in strict operational alignment with COMP-001 through COMP-004, SUP-001 through SUP-005, ORD-001 through ORD-005, SHIP-001 through SHIP-007, PAY-001 through PAY-006, REF-001 through REF-006, PROD-001 through PROD-006, SEC-001 through SEC-006, LEG-001, LEG-002, and LEG-003.
Scope
This policy applies universally across all proprietary technology platforms, mobile applications, software source code, digital storefronts, brand assets, product catalog listings (catalog.novacart.internal), digital software licenses (PROD-006), 3P merchant product listings, and user-generated content across NovaCart’s operating regions (India, Singapore, UAE).
Coverage Includes
* Ownership frameworks for NovaCart brand assets, logos, proprietary software, UI/UX designs, catalog data, and employee work products.
* Licensing and permitted usage rules for customers, 3P marketplace merchants, corporate partners, and employees.
* Governance of user-generated content (UGC), product reviews, Q&As, and uploaded profile media.
* Usage, display, and authorization standards for third-party manufacturer trademarks, brand logos, and copyrighted product imagery.
* Notice-and-action workflows for processing intellectual property infringement notices, copyright takedown requests, and counterfeit product complaints.
* Investigation procedures, content removal criteria, merchant counter-notice handling, repeat infringer enforcement, and audit record retention.
Out of Scope
* Detailed technical digital rights management (DRM) key encryption mechanics and token distribution architectures (governed by PROD-006).
* Litigation procedures, formal court injunctions, and jurisdiction-specific statutory lawsuit filings (governed by internal legal department protocols).
Intellectual Property Overview
Intellectual property (IP) is a vital commercial asset for NovaCart, its brand partners, marketplace merchants, and software vendors. Maintaining a trusted e-commerce ecosystem requires protecting NovaCart's proprietary assets while respecting the copyrights, trademarks, patents, and trade secrets of third-party rights holders.
Core IP Principles
1. Ownership Clarity & Protection: All intellectual property created by NovaCart, embedded in its technology platforms, or produced by employees within their scope of employment belongs exclusively to NovaCart.
2. Zero-Tolerance for Counterfeiting: NovaCart strictly prohibits the listing, promotion, or sale of counterfeit goods, unauthorized replicas, or pirated digital media across all 1P and 3P fulfillment channels.
3. Fair Notice and Takedown Framework: NovaCart operates an efficient, objective, and transparent notice-and-action process for rights holders to report suspected IP infringements, ensuring rapid investigation and appropriate content removal.
4. Balanced Rights Governance: Takedown mechanisms protect legitimate rights holders while providing safeguards against false, bad-faith, or anti-competitive infringement claims through counter-notice mechanisms.

	Customer-Generated Content
Customers frequently contribute text, ratings, and media to the platform:
1. Grant of License: By posting product reviews, ratings, Q&A responses, or uploading defect photographs (REF-004), the customer grants NovaCart a non-exclusive, royalty-free, worldwide, perpetual, irrevocable license to display, reproduce, format, translate, and distribute the content across NovaCart's digital storefronts, marketing channels, and mobile apps.
2. Content Standards & Originality: Customers warrant that uploaded content is original, accurate, and does not infringe upon any third-party copyright, trademark, privacy, or publicity rights (LEG-003).
3. Moderation and Removal: NovaCart reserves the right to edit, reformat, decline, or remove any user-generated content that violates community standards, contains copyrighted media owned by third parties without authorization, or engages in review manipulation (LEG-003).
Third-Party Intellectual Property
NovaCart respects the intellectual property rights of brand partners, original equipment manufacturers (OEMs), and digital content creators:
* Authorized Brand Assets: Manufacturer logos, brand names, and official product imagery hosted in catalog.novacart.internal are utilized strictly under authorized distribution, retail partner, or reseller agreements (PROD-001).
* Merchant Listing Warranties: 3P marketplace merchants listing merchandise on NovaCart warrant that all product titles, descriptions, and uploaded images are original or fully licensed from rights holders. Merchants are strictly prohibited from copying text or imagery from competing listings or external websites (LEG-003).
* Prohibition of Digital Piracy: Digital software products (CAT-SFT), e-books, e-learning content, and gaming keys sold via NovaCart (PROD-006) must be genuine, legally sourced activation keys provided under official publisher licensing models.
Intellectual Property Infringement Reporting
Rights holders or their authorized representatives who believe their copyright, trademark, or brand rights are being infringed on NovaCart may submit a formal complaint through the NovaCart IP Rights Portal (ip.novacart.internal):
Mandatory Takedown Notice Requirements
To be processed, an Infringement Takedown Notice must contain:
1. Identification of Rights Holder: Full legal name, organization, physical address, email address, and contact phone number of the rights holder or authorized agent.
2. Proof of Ownership: Registration certificates, trademark numbers, copyright registration details, or verifiable documentation demonstrating legal ownership of the asserted IP.
3. Identification of Infringing Material: Exact Product Title, Master SKU, Order ID, or direct URL leading to the alleged infringing product listing, image, or text on NovaCart.
4. Statement of Good Faith: An explicit statement that the complaining party believes in good faith that the disputed use is not authorized by the IP owner, its agent, or the law.
5. Statement of Accuracy: A statement under penalty of perjury that the information in the notice is accurate and that the submitter is authorized to act on behalf of the owner.

	Merchant Counter-Notice Workflow
If a merchant believes a takedown notice was issued in error or bad faith, the merchant may submit a formal Counter-Notice within 7 calendar days of notification, providing verified distribution agreements, brand authorization letters, or invoices proving legitimate sourcing.
Licensing Principles
* Software Access Licenses: Purchasing software or digital subscriptions (PROD-006) grants the customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable license to use the digital item under the publisher's End-User License Agreement (EULA).
* White-Label & Private Label IP: Products manufactured under NovaCart private-label brands (CAT-SMT, CAT-APP) are owned exclusively by NovaCart. Contract manufacturers receive a limited, time-bound manufacturing license restricted strictly to fulfilling NovaCart purchase orders.
Customer Responsibilities
Customers accessing NovaCart platforms must adhere to the following IP obligations:
1. Respect Brand Assets: Refrain from using NovaCart logos, brand names, or corporate graphics on personal websites, commercial media, or social channels without prior written authorization.
2. Originality in Contributions: Ensure product reviews, uploaded images, and Q&A submissions do not contain copyrighted music, watermarked stock images, or third-party proprietary text.
3. No Digital Product Redistribution: Refrain from attempting to copy, reverse-engineer, resell, or unauthorizedly redistribute digital software activation keys (PROD-006) or platform digital content.
NovaCart Responsibilities
NovaCart commits to fulfilling explicit operational duties regarding intellectual property governance:
1. Maintaining Platform Integrity: Actively audit catalog listings, investigate reported IP infringements promptly, and maintain robust anti-counterfeiting measures across fulfillment centers (wms.novacart.internal).
2. Fair Due Process: Provide marketplace merchants and content contributors with fair notice and structured counter-notice mechanisms when claims are filed against their listings.
3. Protecting Merchant & Brand Partner IP: Ensure third-party brand assets and manufacturer imagery uploaded to catalog.novacart.internal are protected against unauthorized copying or cross-merchant scraping.

	Business Rules
Rule 1: Instant Containment for Counterfeit Claims Rule
Any listing associated with a complete, verified counterfeit claim submitted by an established brand owner (IP-CAT-BRAND) must be placed on immediate temporary suspension (STAGE-SPD in catalog.novacart.internal) within 4 business hours, and physical inventory locked in wms.novacart.internal, pending investigation.
Rule 2: Three-Strikes Repeat Infringer Mandate
Any 3P marketplace merchant who incurs three (3) substantiated IP infringement violations (counterfeit goods, copyright theft, or trademark misuse) within a rolling 12-month period shall have their merchant account permanently terminated (STATUS_TERMINATED), active merchant listings purged, and escrow payout balances frozen pending legal audit (SEC-004, LEG-003).
Rule 3: Mandatory 7-Year Retention for IP Case Files
All infringement notices, counter-notices, brand authorization certificates, merchant communications, and takedown logs must be archived in secure WORM storage (audit.novacart.internal) for a mandatory period of 7 years to support legal compliance audits and dispute defense (SEC-003, SEC-006).
Exception Handling
* False or Bad-Faith Infringement Notices: If an investigation reveals that a party submitted a false, fraudulent, or bad-faith takedown notice (e.g., a competitor submitting fake claims to disrupt a seller's flash sale), NovaCart rejects the notice, restores the blocked listing immediately, and permanently blocks the submitter from using the IP Portal.
* Authorized Parallel Imports / Grey Market Goods: Parallel import disputes where genuine, authentic branded merchandise is imported legally without the local distributor's exclusive consent are evaluated under regional trade laws. NovaCart does not block genuine, authentic products solely based on exclusive territorial distribution agreements unless statutory local law explicitly prohibits parallel importation.
Practical Examples
Scenario 1: Merchant Copying Copyrighted Product Imagery
1. Brand Owner A discovers that Merchant B copied official, copyrighted studio photography from Brand A's website and uploaded it to a 3P listing on NovaCart (catalog.novacart.internal).
2. Brand A submits a complete Takedown Notice via ip.novacart.internal containing copyright registration certificates and direct listing URLs.
3. Legal IP Team verifies the notice within 6 hours and updates the listing to hide the copyrighted images.
4. Merchant B is notified and fails to provide license proof within 7 days.
5. The listing images are permanently removed, Merchant B receives a Tier 3 policy violation (LEG-003), and the merchant is required to upload original photography.
Scenario 2: Counterfeit Claim and Inventory Quarantine
1. A major luxury audio manufacturer files a counterfeit claim against a 3P seller offering high-end headphones at a $70\%$ discount.
2. Executing Rule 1, NovaCart immediately suspends the listing (STAGE-SPD) within 2 hours and locks 45 physical units in FC BLR-01 into STG-RECALL_VAULT (PROD-002, PROD-005).
3. The seller is requested to provide brand authorization letters and legitimate manufacturer invoices within 7 days.
4. The seller fails to respond. Executing Rule 2, NovaCart deletes the listing permanently, impounds the counterfeit stock for destruction, and terminates the seller's marketplace account.
Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
Q1: How do I report a counterfeit product or copyright violation on NovaCart?
Answer: Brand owners and authorized representatives can submit a formal infringement complaint through our online NovaCart IP Rights Portal (ip.novacart.internal). Your notice must include proof of ownership (trademark/copyright registration), the specific product URLs or SKUs, and a formal statement of accuracy. Our Legal IP team reviews complete notices within 12 business hours.
Q2: What happens if a customer uploads a copyrighted image in a product review?
Answer: Customers grant NovaCart a usage license for content they upload, but they must warrant that they own the media or have rights to use it (LEG-003). If a rights holder reports that a customer review contains copyrighted photography or video used without permission, NovaCart verifies the claim and removes the infringing media from the product page.
Q3: Can I use the NovaCart logo on my website or promotional flyers?
Answer: No. NovaCart brand names, logos, trade dress, and platform graphics are proprietary intellectual property (IP-CAT-NOVA). You may not use NovaCart logos on external websites, commercial flyers, or media without prior written approval from NovaCart Corporate Communications. Approved marketplace sellers may use official "Available on NovaCart" badges provided in the Merchant Portal.
