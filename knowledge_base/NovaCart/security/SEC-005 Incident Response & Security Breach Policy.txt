Purpose
This document defines the official Incident Response & Security Breach Policy of NovaCart Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "NovaCart"). It establishes standardized operational procedures, incident response principles, severity classification levels, containment protocols, investigation standards, customer communication standards, service restoration guidelines, and business rules governing the detection, reporting, containment, investigation, recovery, and post-incident review of security events and data breaches affecting NovaCart systems, customer accounts, products, or business operations.
As a core security policy (SEC-005) in the Security module of the NovaCart Enterprise Knowledge Base (NEKB), SEC-005 governs emergency threat management across the Incident Management Engine (incident.novacart.internal), Identity & Access Management (auth.novacart.internal), Threat Telemetry (shield.novacart.internal), and Customer Communications (crm.novacart.internal), operating in strict operational alignment with COMP-001 through COMP-004, SUP-001 through SUP-005, ORD-001 through ORD-005, SHIP-001 through SHIP-007, PAY-001 through PAY-006, REF-001 through REF-006, PROD-001 through PROD-006, SEC-001, SEC-002, SEC-003, and SEC-004.
Scope
This policy applies universally across all technology systems, cloud networks, database environments, customer support portals, payment microservices, mobile applications, employee devices, third-party vendor integrations, and physical facilities operated by NovaCart across India, Singapore, and the United Arab Emirates.
Coverage Includes
* Detection, triage, and handling of account compromises, unauthorized logins, insider misuse, malware/ransomware events, credential leaks, data exposures, third-party vendor incidents, DDoS attacks, and payment service disruptions resulting from security events.
* Computer Security Incident Response Team (CSIRT) mobilization, leadership, and operational escalation hierarchies.
* Immediate containment controls, system isolation, session revocations, and network perimeter filtering.
* Forensic evidence preservation, chain-of-custody logging, and root-cause analysis.
* Service restoration sign-offs, customer notification thresholds, regulatory filings, post-incident reviews, and 7-year audit retention.
Out of Scope
* Routine non-security technical glitches, hardware failures, or routine maintenance downtime not caused by security events (governed by IT Operations SOPs).
* Day-to-day individual transaction risk scoring and payment chargeback defense (governed by PAY-004 and SEC-004).
Incident Response Overview
Security incidents—ranging from isolated credential stuffing attacks to complex multi-node data exposures or third-party service compromises—pose severe operational, financial, and reputational risks. NovaCart maintains a structured, proactive incident response capability designed to minimize customer impact, contain threats rapidly, ensure operational continuity, and restore services securely.
Core Incident Response Principles
1. Safety & Customer Protection First: The primary goal during any security incident is isolating threats to prevent customer account compromise, financial loss, or personal data exposure (SEC-001, SEC-003).
2. Speed & Rapid Containment: Response teams execute immediate containment controls to isolate affected subnets, revoke compromised session tokens, and block malicious traffic before full root-cause analysis concludes.
3. Preservation of Evidence: All incident response actions must preserve system state telemetry, server logs, and memory dumps in a tamper-evident manner to support forensic audit, regulatory reporting, and legal proceedings.
4. Transparent Communication: Customer and stakeholder notifications regarding security breaches must be accurate, timely, and provided through verified channels without misleading or minimizing the event's scope.

	Incident Reporting Process
Every NovaCart employee, contractor, merchant partner, or customer support representative who discovers or suspects a security anomaly must report the event immediately:
1. Internal Incident Reporting Channels:
   * Emergency Portal: Submit a high-priority security ticket via incident.novacart.internal.
   * Emergency Hotline: Call the 24/7 CSIRT Incident Dispatch Line (+91-CSIRT-NOW).
   * SIEM Telemetry Ingestion: Automated alerts generated by shield.novacart.internal ingest directly into the CSIRT triage queue.
2. Mandatory Reporting Telemetry: Initial reports must include the reporter's contact details, timestamp of discovery, affected system or user IDs, observed anomalous behavior, and initial threat indicators.
3. No-Penalty Security Reporting Policy: Employees who report security incidents, accidental exposures, or suspected breaches in good faith are protected from disciplinary action, encouraging immediate transparency.

	Containment Procedures
Once an incident is declared (SEV-1 through SEV-3), CSIRT executes immediate containment to isolate the threat vector:
1. Short-Term Containment (Immediate Isolation):
   * Account & Session Revocation: Mass revocation of active JWT session tokens (LOGOUT_ALL_DEVICES) for affected user accounts or compromised employee credentials (SEC-001, SEC-002).
   * Network Edge Filtering: Blocking malicious IP subnets, user-agent strings, or geographic regions at the edge API gateway (api.novacart.internal).
   * Database & Vault Isolation: Placing sensitive data vaults (digital.novacart.internal, media-vault.novacart.internal) into read-only mode or temporary offline isolation (SEC-003).
   * Payment Microservice Pause: Temporarily pausing automated payment processing (PAY-001) or refund disbursements (PAY-003) if payment API integrations exhibit breach indicators.
2. Long-Term Containment (System Stabilization):
   * Applying temporary code hotfixes, re-routing traffic to uncompromised failover subnets, and isolating infected virtual instances or container nodes for offline forensic analysis.
Investigation Process
The investigation phase determines the root cause, scope of compromise, and extent of data exposure while preserving evidence integrity:
1. Forensic Evidence Preservation Standards:
   * Forensic snapshots of virtual machine memory, disk volumes, and container states must be captured before terminating or rebooting compromised nodes.
   * Server logs, SIEM telemetry, and network traffic captures must be hashed (SHA-256) and copied to an immutable, write-once-read-many (WORM) storage bucket (forensics-vault.novacart.internal).
2. Root-Cause Analysis (RCA): Forensics specialists analyze attack vectors, entry points (e.g., unpatched software vulnerability, compromised employee API key, phishing credential harvest), lateral movement paths, and data access logs (SEC-002, SEC-003).
3. Data Impact Assessment: Auditing database access logs to determine precisely which data classification tiers (Classes 1–4 under SEC-003) and specific customer accounts were accessed or exfiltrated.
Recovery & Service Restoration
Restoring affected systems to live operational production requires strict multi-stage verification to ensure the threat is completely eradicated:
1. Eradication Verification: Confirming that all malicious artifacts, unauthorized backdoors, rogue administrative accounts, and compromised API keys have been removed.
2. System Hardening & Patching: Applying permanent software patches, updating firewall rules, enforcing fresh credential rotations, and re-certifying RBAC permissions (SEC-002).
3. Staged Service Restoration: Systems are restored in controlled phases:
   * Phase 1: Core Database & Identity Services (auth.novacart.internal).
   * Phase 2: Internal Operational Tools & Warehouse Logistics (wms.novacart.internal).
   * Phase 3: Customer Storefront & Checkout APIs (api.novacart.internal/oms).
4. Formal Restoration Approval: Services cannot be restored to live customer traffic following a SEV-1 or SEV-2 incident without formal, documented sign-off from both the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) and VP of Infrastructure.

	Post-Incident Review & Continuous Improvement
Every SEV-1, SEV-2, and SEV-3 security incident must conclude with a formal Post-Incident Review (PIR) process:
1. Mandatory PIR Meeting: Held within five (5) business days of incident closure, attended by the CSIRT leads, CISO, Engineering Leads, and Customer Support Director.
2. Post-Mortem Analysis Scope: Reviewing the complete timeline, initial detection gaps, containment efficacy, communication delays, and root-cause vulnerabilities.
3. PIR Action Item Tracking: Every identified gap (e.g., missing SIEM alert rule, unpatched API endpoint, outdated playbook) is assigned a Jira tracking ticket with a strict resolution SLA:
   * Critical Security Action Items: Must be resolved within 7 calendar days.
   * Major Security Action Items: Must be resolved within 14 calendar days.
4. Lessons Learned Documentation: Final PIR reports are archived in incident.novacart.internal/pir and integrated into annual security awareness training.
Employee Responsibilities
All NovaCart employees, contractors, and third-party personnel maintain explicit duties during security events:
1. Immediate Reporting Duty: Report any suspected security anomaly, accidental data exposure, or phished credential to the CSIRT within 15 minutes of discovery.
2. Strict Media Silence: Employees are strictly prohibited from commenting on, posting about, or discussing active or past security incidents on social media, public forums, or with news media outlets.
3. Cooperation with Forensics: Fully assist CSIRT forensic investigators, surrender assigned corporate devices upon request, and provide accurate timelines without altering or deleting log files.

	Business Rules
Rule 1: Dual Sign-Off Service Restoration Mandate
No production system, checkout API, or customer portal isolated during a SEV-1 or SEV-2 security incident may be reconnected to live traffic without documented, dual digital sign-off from both the CISO and the VP of Infrastructure in incident.novacart.internal.
Rule 2: Mandatory 24-Hour Breach Notification Threshold
If an investigation confirms that Class 3 (Confidential) or Class 4 (Restricted) customer PII has been exfiltrated or accessed by an unauthorized entity (SEC-003), direct customer breach notifications must be dispatched within 24 hours of confirmation, regardless of brand sensitivity.
Rule 3: Automated Containment Override Block
System-automated containment actions (e.g., edge IP blocking, session revocations, account freezes executed by shield.novacart.internal) cannot be overridden or unblocked by Customer Support Representatives or IT Helpdesk agents. Overrides require explicit CSIRT Lead authorization.
Rule 4: Mandatory 7-Year Incident Record Retention
All incident ticket records, CSIRT chat logs, forensic disk images, telemetry snapshots, PIR reports, and regulatory correspondence must be retained in WORM secure storage for a mandatory period of 7 years (SEC-003).
Exception Handling
* False Positive Security Alert Containment Release: If automated system containment is triggered by a false positive alert (e.g., a legitimate high-traffic promotional campaign misidentified as a DDoS attack), the CSIRT On-Call Lead can issue a CONTAINMENT_OVERRIDE in incident.novacart.internal, restoring traffic flow within 5 minutes while logging the false positive for SIEM rule tuning.
* Simultaneous Multiple Security Incidents: If multiple independent security incidents occur concurrently, the CSIRT Lead invokes Emergency Multi-Triage Protocol. Resources are allocated strictly by Incident Priority Score (P1 through P4), and secondary engineering teams are mobilized from standby rotations.
Practical Examples
Scenario 1: Response to High-Volume Account Takeover (ATO) Campaign (SEV-2)
1. shield.novacart.internal detects a credential stuffing attack originating from a botnet targeting customer login endpoints. 600 accounts experience unauthorized logins from unfamiliar geographic subnets.
2. System triggers SEV-2 Incident INC-88201 and alerts the CSIRT On-Call Lead within 2 minutes.
3. CSIRT executes short-term containment: Mass revokes active JWT session tokens (LOGOUT_ALL_DEVICES) for all 600 accounts (SEC-001, SEC-002), blocks botnet IP ranges at the edge API gateway, and places temporary shipping holds on active pending orders (ORD-001).
4. CSIRT verifies zero PII exfiltration beyond account profile viewing.
5. Within 2 hours, affected customers receive SMS and Email alerts explaining the containment action and providing a direct link to re-verify identity and establish new credentials (SEC-001).
Scenario 2: Third-Party Payment Service Security Disruption (SEV-1)
1. A major third-party payment gateway integration exhibits anomalous API responses indicating a potential security breach at the vendor level.
2. CSIRT declares SEV-1 Incident INC-99102 and mobilizes full emergency response.
3. CSIRT executes Rule 1 containment: Instantly pauses the affected gateway payment route in api.novacart.internal/oms and shifts 100% of payment traffic to secondary failover gateways (PAY-001) within 3 minutes.
4. Storefront displays zero downtime for customers. CSIRT collaborates with vendor security leads to verify breach scope.
5. Once vendor submits an audited security remediation certificate, CISO and VP of Infrastructure sign off on restoring the primary gateway route.
Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
Q1: What should I do if I suspect my NovaCart customer account has been hacked or accessed without my permission?
Answer: If you notice unauthorized orders, unexpected password reset emails, or logins from unfamiliar locations, contact Customer Support immediately via Live Chat or phone. Our team will initiate emergency account containment, lock active sessions, place holds on pending orders, and guide you through secure identity re-verification and recovery (Refer to SEC-001).
Q2: How does NovaCart notify customers if a data breach affects their personal information?
Answer: If an investigation confirms that your personal information was involved in a security breach, NovaCart will notify you directly via registered Email, SMS, and an In-App Account Security Banner within 24 hours of confirmation. The notice will clearly explain what happened, what data was involved, what steps NovaCart has taken, and what protective measures you should take (Refer to SEC-003).
Q3: Who leads the response team during a major cyber attack or security emergency at NovaCart?
Answer: Major security incidents are directed by the Computer Security Incident Response Team (CSIRT), led by the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) and On-Call Security Lead. CSIRT coordinates technical containment with Engineering, legal reporting with Legal & Compliance, and customer messaging with Customer Experience (Refer to SUP-003).
