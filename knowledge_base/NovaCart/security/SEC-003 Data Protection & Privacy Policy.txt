Purpose
This document defines the official Data Protection & Privacy Policy of NovaCart Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "NovaCart"). It establishes standardized operational procedures, privacy governance principles, data classification standards, information handling controls, retention schedules, access boundaries, and business rules governing the secure collection, processing, storage, internal sharing, external transfer, and disposal of customer, employee, and business information.
As a core operational document (SEC-003) in the Security module of the NovaCart Enterprise Knowledge Base (NEKB), SEC-003 governs data management practices across e-commerce storefronts, mobile applications, customer support systems (crm.novacart.internal), payment ledgers (ledger.novacart.internal), order management microservices (api.novacart.internal/oms), and data vaults, operating in strict operational alignment with COMP-001 through COMP-004, SUP-001 through SUP-005, ORD-001 through ORD-005, SHIP-001 through SHIP-007, PAY-001 through PAY-006, REF-001 through REF-006, PROD-001 through PROD-006, SEC-001, and SEC-002.
Scope
This policy applies universally across all customer, employee, merchant, and operational data processed across NovaCart’s operating regions (India, Singapore, UAE) across all commercial sales channels and internal platforms.
Coverage Includes
* Enterprise data classification definitions, sensitivity tiers, and handling standards.
* Life-cycle management of Personally Identifiable Information (PII), financial records, order logs, support transcripts, and identity verification media.
* Technical storage baseline standards, encryption requirements, and data minimization controls.
* Employee role-based data access restrictions, internal query boundaries, and support agent masking.
* Data sharing rules for third-party service providers (logistics carriers, payment gateways, communication vendors).
* Standardized data retention schedules, automated archiving pipelines, and secure data disposal workflows.
* Operational workflows for customer privacy requests, information exports, data corrections, and account closure purges.
Out of Scope
* Technical authentication mechanisms, OAuth tokens, and session JWT architectures (governed by SEC-002).
* Detailed cryptographic tokenization algorithms for cardholder data (governed by PAY-006).
Data Protection & Privacy Overview
Data protection and privacy are fundamental operational commitments at NovaCart. In executing daily e-commerce operations—processing orders (ORD-001), delivering shipments (SHIP-001), clearing payments (PAY-001), managing returns (REF-002), and resolving support inquiries (SUP-001)—NovaCart collects and processes significant volumes of personal information. Protecting this information against unauthorized access, inappropriate exposure, misuse, and improper retention is critical to maintaining operational integrity and customer trust.
Core Privacy Principles
1. Purpose Limitation & Lawful Use: Information is collected exclusively for specific, explicit, and legitimate business purposes (order fulfillment, account maintenance, customer support, fraud prevention) and is never processed for secondary incompatible activities.
2. Data Minimization: NovaCart collects and retains only the minimum volume of personal data strictly necessary to execute authorized business operations.
3. Storage Limitation & Lifecycle Control: Information is retained only as long as required to fulfill operational purposes or satisfy statutory audit retention requirements, after which it is securely purged or anonymized.
4. Transparency & Individual Control: Customers maintain visibility into their stored data profiles and possess operational mechanisms to correct inaccurate information, manage communication preferences, or request account data deletion.
Privacy Principles
All systems, operational workflows, software microservices, and employee procedures within NovaCart must incorporate four baseline privacy controls:
* Privacy by Design & Default: New software features, microservice APIs, customer portals, and vendor integrations must embed privacy protections, field-level encryption, and strict access controls into their architecture prior to production deployment.
* Strict Need-to-Know Access Boundaries: Employees are granted access strictly to the specific data fields required for their assigned operational duties. Broad database access is prohibited (SEC-002).
* Prohibition of Data Selling: NovaCart strictly prohibits selling, renting, or leasing customer personal information, browsing behavior, or purchase histories to third-party advertisers or data brokers.
* Accountability & Continuous Audit: Data access events, query logs, bulk exports, and system purges are recorded continuously in immutable audit logs for regular compliance reviews.

	Data Collection & Usage Standards
Information collection adheres strictly to operational necessity and customer notification:
1. Lawful Business Purpose Mandate: Data collection occurs exclusively during specific transactional events: account registration (SEC-001), checkout placement (ORD-001), payment processing (PAY-001), return generation (REF-002), support ticketing (SUP-001), or warranty registration (PROD-003).
2. Explicit Consent & Notification: Customers are informed of data collection at the point of entry (e.g., checkout forms, registration screens). Opt-in consent is captured explicitly for promotional marketing, SMS updates, or third-party partner offers.
3. Collection Minimization in Forms: Customer-facing data capture forms are restricted to essential inputs. Optional fields (e.g., alternate delivery instructions) are explicitly labeled as optional.
Data Storage & Protection Standards
Information stored across NovaCart infrastructure must satisfy defined security baselines:
* Encryption in Transit: All data moving between client browsers, mobile applications, external APIs, and internal microservices must be encrypted using TLS 1.3 protocols. Plain-text HTTP connections are automatically redirected to HTTPS at edge API gateways.
* Encryption at Rest: All Class 3 (Confidential) and Class 4 (Restricted) data stored in databases, storage buckets, or backup media must be encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption.
* Storage Isolation: Sensitive Identity Verification Media (INF-IDN) uploaded during account recovery (SEC-001) or high-risk fraud investigations is stored in an isolated, encrypted document bucket (media-vault.novacart.internal) separated from general web server storage.

	Customer Privacy Rights
NovaCart provides clear, self-service, and support-assisted workflows for customers to exercise their privacy rights:






+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                        CUSTOMER PRIVACY REQUEST WORKFLOW                          |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 1. REQUEST LODGED   | Customer submits Privacy Request in portal or via Support.  |
| 2. IDENTITY VERIFY  | Level 2/3 Step-Up MFA verification completed (SEC-001).    |
| 3. TYPE EVALUATION  | System routes request: [A] Export Data, [B] Correct Data, [C] Delete Account. |
| 4. EXECUTION SLA    | Data Export / Correction < 48 hrs; Account Deletion < 30 days. |
| 5. CONFIRMATION     | Automated completion notice dispatched to customer.         |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

1. Right to Access & Data Portability
Customers may request a complete digital copy of their stored personal information. Upon identity verification (Level 2/3 under SEC-001), the portal generates a machine-readable, encrypted data export (.zip containing JSON/CSV files) covering profile data, order histories, wallet balances, and support tickets within 48 hours.
2. Right to Correction & Data Rectification
Customers may update incorrect names, delivery addresses, or contact preferences directly inside My Account. For primary email or phone number updates, dual-OTP step-up verification is enforced (SEC-001).
3. Right to Erasure & Account Deletion
A customer may request permanent account closure and data erasure via My Account $\rightarrow$ Privacy Settings $\rightarrow$ Delete My Account.
* Pre-Deletion Audit: The system validates that no active orders are in transit (ORD-001), no open return/refund requests exist (REF-001), and zero unexpired wallet balances remain (REF-006).
* Execution Workflow: Upon confirmation, the account transitions to STATUS_CLOSED_USER for a 30-day grace period. At Day 30, all Class 3 Confidential PII is permanently scrubbed or anonymized, and the customer receives confirmation.
Customer Responsibilities
Customers share responsibility for maintaining the privacy of their personal information:
1. Accurate Profile Information: Provide accurate profile information and promptly update delivery addresses or phone numbers upon changes.
2. Secure Communication Channels: Transmit sensitive documents or identity media exclusively through secure NovaCart upload portals (auth.novacart.internal/verify). Never email passwords, full credit card numbers, or government IDs via unencrypted public email.
3. Preference Management: Actively manage marketing preferences, SMS notifications, and browser cookie settings via portal account tools.
Employee Responsibilities
All NovaCart employees, contractors, and third-party personnel must adhere to strict data handling standards:
1. Zero Unauthorized Access: Never query, access, or attempt to view customer personal data out of curiosity or for non-operational personal reasons. Every customer data query is logged and monitored.
2. Clean Desk & Screen Protection: Lock physical computer screens (Win + L / Cmd + Ctrl + Q) when leaving workstations and ensure customer data is not visible to unauthorized onlookers.
3. Prohibition of Local Data Export: Downloading customer lists, spreadsheets containing PII, or support transcripts onto local personal devices or unauthorized cloud storage is strictly prohibited.

	Business Rules
Rule 1: Automated Identity Upload Purge Mandate
Government ID scans (INF-IDN) uploaded to media-vault.novacart.internal for Level 4 identity verification (SEC-001) or fraud audits (SEC-004) must be automatically and permanently shredded using cryptographic overwriting within 30 calendar days of audit completion.
Rule 2: Order Data Anonymization Standards
When an account is deleted or when the 7-year statutory financial retention window expires for an order record, financial line-item values are retained for corporate tax ledgers, but all customer PII fields (Name, Delivery Address, Email, Phone Number) are replaced permanently with the anonymized string [DATA_PURGED_PER_SEC-003].
Rule 3: Support Transcripts Screen Masking Standard
Customer Support tools (crm.novacart.internal) must dynamically mask primary contact details, credit card numbers, and delivery street addresses in live chat and ticket view screens. Unmasking a field requires an explicit click-to-view action by an authorized CSR, which logs an audit entry detailing Agent ID, Customer ID, and Reason Code.
Exception Handling
* Accidental Non-Sensitive Data Exposure: If an internal operational report containing non-sensitive, aggregated operational data (Class 2) is accidentally exposed to unauthorized internal staff, the discovering employee must notify Information Security within 1 hour. Security restricts access, audits view logs, and logs an internal incident ticket.
* Data Deletion Blocked by Legal / Dispute Hold: If a customer requests account deletion while an active chargeback dispute (PAY-004), fraud investigation (SEC-004), or legal notice is pending, the system pauses the 30-day erasure pipeline, notifies the customer of the specific operational hold, and resumes deletion immediately upon dispute resolution.
Practical Examples
Scenario 1: Customer Requesting Account Deletion and Data Purge
1. Customer opens My Account $\rightarrow$ Privacy Settings and clicks Request Account Deletion.
2. System prompts Level 2 SMS OTP verification (SEC-001). Customer verifies code.
3. System executes pre-deletion check: Confirms zero active deliveries in TMS, zero open RMA returns in rma.novacart.internal, and zero wallet balance.
4. Account status transitions to STATUS_CLOSED_USER. Customer receives an email confirmation detailing the 30-day grace period.
5. At Day 30, automated batch scripts in auth.novacart.internal permanently scrub all PII fields, scrub support logs, anonymize historical order records, and send a final completion notification.
Scenario 2: Secure Handling of Identity Documents Uploaded for Account Recovery
1. Customer losing both email and phone access submits a Level 4 Account Recovery request (SEC-001).
2. Customer uploads an Emirates ID scan to auth.novacart.internal/verify. File uploads directly to media-vault.novacart.internal with AES-256 encryption.
3. Security Specialist reviews the document in shield.novacart.internal via an ephemeral, watermarked viewer that prevents local downloading or screenshotting.
4. Identity is verified, account access is restored, and the specialist marks the audit complete.
5. 30 days post-audit, automated vault scripts cryptographically shred the Emirates ID file.
Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
Q1: How do I get a copy of all the personal information NovaCart has stored about me?
Answer: You can request a complete digital copy of your personal data directly through your account. Go to My Account $\rightarrow$ Privacy Settings $\rightarrow$ Download My Data. After completing a quick security verification, our system will generate an encrypted, downloadable file containing your profile details, order history, and support records within 48 hours (Refer to SUP-001).
Q2: Does NovaCart share my delivery address and phone number with third-party sellers or delivery drivers?
Answer: Yes, but strictly for fulfillment purposes. We share your delivery name, address, and contact number with contracted logistics carriers (SHIP-001) and field service technicians (PROD-004) solely to ensure your package is delivered or installed correctly. Our partners are contractually prohibited from using your contact details for any secondary purpose or marketing (Refer to SEC-002).
Q3: Can I delete my NovaCart account if I have a package currently in transit?
Answer: To ensure you receive your items and retain access to delivery tracking, return rights, and warranty invoices, account deletion requests cannot be finalized while an order is actively in transit. Once your package is marked DELIVERED and any open returns or refunds are completed, you can proceed with account deletion (Refer to ORD-001, REF-001).
