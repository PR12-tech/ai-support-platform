Purpose
This document defines the official enterprise policy, operational standards, fulfillment acceleration workflows, and business rules governing expedited shipping services at NovaCart Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "NovaCart"). It establishes eligibility criteria, order processing cut-off deadlines, priority warehouse picking/packing protocols, dedicated courier selection, delivery time commitments, and exception handling for Express and Priority shipments.
As a core operational document (SHIP-003) in the NovaCart Enterprise Knowledge Base (NEKB), SHIP-003 enforces fast-track execution across fulfillment centers (FCs), local dark store networks, contracted express courier fleets, and the Transportation Management System (api.novacart.internal/tms), maintaining complete consistency with COMP-001 through COMP-004, SUP-001 through SUP-005, ORD-001 through ORD-005, SHIP-001, and SHIP-002.
Scope
This policy applies to all expedited domestic and regional cross-border order shipments originating from or delivered to designated metropolitan and high-density operating zones across India (IN), Singapore (SG), and the United Arab Emirates (UAE).
Coverage Includes
* Accelerated fulfillment across Service Tiers: Express Delivery (SRV-EXP), Priority Same-Day Delivery (SRV-SDY), and Scheduled Time-Slot Delivery (SRV-SCH).
* High-priority processing for high-value merchandise (CAT-ELE, CAT-MOB, CAT-GAM) and time-sensitive commercial orders.
* Expedited member benefits for NovaCart Prime subscription tiers and Corporate Enterprise Accounts.
* Warehouse fast-track picking queues, dedicated courier air/surface linehaul allocations, and expedited last-mile fleet routing.
Express Shipping Overview
Express Shipping (SRV-EXP) is NovaCart's primary accelerated transit service designed for customers seeking rapid $24\text{-to-}48\text{ hour}$ door-to-door delivery across metropolitan and regional Tier 1/Tier 2 urban hubs.
Core Operational Principles
1. Accelerated Warehouse Processing: Orders placed prior to daily cutoff times bypass standard picking queues and are allocated to dedicated fast-track packing lines in local FCs.
2. Dedicated Courier Linehaul: Express parcels are assigned to premium express courier networks utilizing direct point-to-point air freight or priority surface express routes.
3. Proactive Progress Tracking: Automated real-time milestone telemetry with dynamic delivery window predictions updated every 2 hours in tms.novacart.internal.
Priority Shipping Overview
Priority Shipping (SRV-SDY / SRV-SCH) represents NovaCart's ultra-fast, premium fulfillment tier. It utilizes localized hyper-local dark store nodes and dedicated point-to-point last-mile courier riders to achieve same-day delivery within 4 to 12 hours or guaranteed customer-selected time-slots.
Core Operational Principles
1. Hyper-Local Inventory Sourcing: Sourced exclusively from local urban fulfillment nodes (BLR-01, BOM-01, SIN-01, DXB-01) located within a $25\text{ km}$ radius of the delivery location.
2. Immediate Lock & Pick: Warehouse workstations receive instant audio/visual pick alerts upon payment authorization, requiring item packaging within 30 minutes.
3. Dedicated Rider Allocation: Last-mile dispatch engines allocate single-parcel point-to-point delivery riders to eliminate transit consolidation delays.

	Carrier Selection Guidelines
Carrier assignment for expedited orders is controlled by the automated TMS Carrier Selection Engine (api.novacart.internal/tms/select-express-carrier):
1. Express Carrier Tiering: Assigned exclusively to Tier-1 air express or direct surface express carriers (e.g., DHL Express, FedEx Priority, BlueDart Apex, or NovaCart Express Direct).
2. Real-Time Carrier Performance Scoring: Carriers must maintain an active trailing 30-day On-Time Delivery (OTD) rating of $\ge 98.5\%$ on the target route to remain eligible for express order allocation.
3. Direct Point-to-Point Rider Dispatch: For Priority Same-Day (SRV-SDY), the system assigns the closest available internal or dedicated partner courier rider using real-time GPS proximity algorithms.

	Customer Communication
Expedited order processing triggers heightened customer communication to provide complete operational transparency:
1. Order Checkout Confirmation: Displays explicit delivery time commitment, guaranteed delivery date, and assigned fulfillment hub.
2. Dispatch & Tracking Activation: Instant SMS, WhatsApp, and Email alerts sent upon FC dock handover containing direct live tracking links and assigned express courier contact details.
3. Live Rider Map Tracking (Priority Same-Day): For SRV-SDY orders, the customer mobile application displays interactive real-time GPS tracking of the delivery rider once OUT_FOR_DELIVERY status is activated.
4. Pre-Arrival Notification: Delivery riders make an automated 30-minute pre-arrival phone call to confirm recipient availability at the doorstep.

	Business Rules
Rule 1: Cut-Off Enforcement Policy
Order placement timestamps in api.novacart.internal/oms strictly govern delivery commitments. An express order placed at 14:01 Local Time is classified as post-cutoff and automatically adopts the next business day dispatch schedule.
Rule 2: Express Delivery Guarantee & Fee Waiver
If an Express (SRV-EXP) or Priority (SRV-SDY) order fails to arrive within the committed delivery window due to an internal or carrier operational delay (and not due to customer unreachability or invalid address), NovaCart automatically refunds the express shipping fee in full and issues a ₹250 / $5 / AED 15 goodwill wallet credit (Refer to SUP-005).
Rule 3: High-Value Packaging Mandate
All Express and Priority shipments containing high-value electronics (CAT-ELE, CAT-MOB) exceeding ₹25,000 / $400 / AED 1,100 must be packaged in reinforced tamper-evident containers secured with serialized security tape. The security seal ID must be scanned into WMS and verified by the recipient upon doorstep delivery (Refer to SHIP-001).
Exception Handling
* Severe Weather & Force Majeure Suspensions: In the event of severe weather alerts, flooding, airport closures, or civic disruptions, the Logistics Operations Director may declare a temporary "Express Guarantee Suspension." Active customers are notified via SMS/Push notification, and late delivery financial guarantees are waived during the suspension period.
* Carrier Capacity Exhaustion: When daily express volume limits are reached for a specific postal zone during major shopping events, the platform automatically greys out Express/Priority options at checkout, defaulting to Standard Shipping (SRV-STD) to prevent unfulfillable commitments.
* Doorstep Unreachability (Express NDR): If a priority delivery rider arrives at the doorstep but the recipient is unreachable, the rider waits 10 minutes and attempts phone contact twice. If still unreachable, the rider logs NDR code EXPRESS_CUSTOMER_UNREACHABLE. Priority Same-Day orders are returned to the local hub, and a re-attempt is scheduled for the next morning (Refer to SHIP-001, SHIP-005).
Practical Examples
Scenario 1: Successful Priority Same-Day Fulfillment (SRV-SDY)
1. Customer in Bangalore places an order for a replacement smartphone (CAT-MOB) at 09:30 AM selecting Priority Same-Day Shipping (SRV-SDY).
2. Order is assigned to local urban hub BLR-01. WMS generates high-priority pick task at 09:31 AM.
3. Item is picked, serial number logged, tamper-evident security tape applied, and packaged by 09:55 AM (Status: READY_FOR_DISPATCH).
4. TMS allocates direct last-mile rider at 10:05 AM (SHIPPED). Customer receives WhatsApp message with live GPS map link.
5. Rider arrives at customer premises at 01:15 PM, verifies 4-digit mobile OTP, and completes delivery (DELIVERED) in under 4 hours from order placement.
Scenario 2: Express Delivery Cutoff & On-Time Guarantee Claim
1. Customer in Dubai places an express order (SRV-EXP) for a gaming headset at 01:45 PM (15 minutes prior to the 14:00 cutoff).
2. FC DXB-01 packs item and hands parcel to express courier dock run at 03:30 PM.
3. Due to a middle-mile express courier sorting hub failure, the parcel is delivered on the 2nd business day instead of the guaranteed next business day.
4. TMS flags the late delivery exception automatically. System issues a full refund of the AED 25 express shipping fee back to the customer's credit card and deposits an AED 15 goodwill credit into their NovaCart Wallet (Refer to SUP-005).
Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
Q1: Is Express Shipping available on weekends and public holidays?
Answer: Priority Same-Day (SRV-SDY) and Scheduled Delivery (SRV-SCH) operate 7 days a week (Monday through Sunday) in Tier 1 metropolitan zones. Express Delivery (SRV-EXP) operates Monday through Saturday, excluding regional statutory national holidays.
Q2: How do NovaCart Prime members qualify for free Express Shipping?
Answer: NovaCart Prime members automatically receive free Express Shipping (SRV-EXP) on all eligible items displaying the Prime badge at checkout, provided the delivery address falls within supported Tier 1 or Tier 2 metropolitan zones (Refer to COMP-001).
Q3: What happens if an express parcel arrives with a broken tamper-evident security seal?
Answer: Customers must refuse delivery immediately if the serialized tamper-evident security tape is broken, cut, or altered upon arrival. The customer should instruct the rider to log "Refused - Seal Tampered" in the TMS app and contact Customer Support immediately for a priority replacement dispatch (Refer to SHIP-001, REF-003).
