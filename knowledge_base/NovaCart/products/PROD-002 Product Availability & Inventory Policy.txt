Purpose
This document defines the official enterprise policy, inventory management principles, stock availability definitions, reservation rules, replenishment guidelines, and business rules governing product availability across all sales channels at NovaCart Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "NovaCart"). It establishes standardized procedures to maintain high inventory accuracy, prevent unhandled overselling, enforce reservation timeout policies, and ensure seamless synchronization between physical stock allocations and customer-facing availability displays.
As a core operational document (PROD-002) in the Products category of the NovaCart Enterprise Knowledge Base (NEKB), PROD-002 governs stock management within the central Inventory Management System (inventory.novacart.internal), operating in strict alignment with COMP-001 through COMP-004, SUP-001 through SUP-005, ORD-001 through ORD-005, SHIP-001 through SHIP-007, PAY-001 through PAY-006, REF-001 through REF-006, and PROD-001.
Scope
This policy applies universally across all product categories—Consumer Electronics (CAT-ELE), Mobile Devices (CAT-MOB), Gaming Hardware (CAT-GAM), Home Appliances (CAT-APP), Smart Home Hardware (CAT-SMT), Digital Software (CAT-SFT), Gift Cards (CAT-GFT), and Subscriptions (CAT-SUB)—managed across NovaCart’s operating regions (India, Singapore, UAE).
Coverage Includes
* Customer-facing product availability statuses, stock thresholds, and display rules.
* Real-time Available-to-Promise (ATP) calculations and inventory state management.
* Two-stage stock reservation workflows (checkout soft locks and order hard locks).
* Omnichannel inventory synchronization frequencies, event streams, and channel webhooks.
* Backorder and pre-order qualification parameters, stock allocation priority, and price guarantees.
* Inventory replenishment triggers, safety stock buffer formulas, cycle counts, manual inventory corrections, and audit adjustments.
Out of Scope
* Physical warehouse picking, packing, linehaul routing, and dock departure execution (governed by SHIP-001 and SHIP-003).
* Master catalog SKU creation, taxonomy mapping, and attribute enrichment (governed by PROD-001).
Product Availability & Inventory Overview
NovaCart operates a distributed fulfillment infrastructure comprising regional primary fulfillment centers (FCs)—Bangalore (BLR-01), Mumbai (BOM-01), Delhi (DEL-02), Singapore (SIN-01), and Dubai (DXB-01)—alongside urban dark store nodes and marketplace partner facilities. The Inventory Control Engine (inventory.novacart.internal) aggregates physical balances and calculates real-time customer availability.
Core Operational Principles
1. Zero Unhandled Overselling: Real-time event-driven stock reservation prevents order acceptance when sellable inventory is depleted, enforcing a maximum permissible oversell threshold of 0.00%.
2. Single Sellable View: Aggregating Available-to-Promise (ATP) stock across all FCs, dark stores, and verified marketplace merchant nodes into a unified, location-aware customer stream.
3. Deterministic State Locking: Explicit isolation between physical inventory on hand, soft-reserved cart stock, hard-reserved allocated order stock, damaged/quarantined stock, and in-transit replenishment stock.
4. Continuous Multi-Node Synchronization: High-frequency event streaming ensuring inventory delta updates propagate across storefront channels in under 1 second.
5. Inventory Ownership & Accountability: Physical and systemic inventory accountability rests strictly with the assigned Fulfillment Center Manager or Marketplace Merchant node until the physical dock departure scan transfers custody to logistics carriers.

	Master Available-to-Promise (ATP) Formula
To maintain complete consistency across all sales channels, sellable inventory is computed continuously using the master availability formula:


$$\text{ATP} = \text{On Hand Stock (OH)} - \text{Hard Reserved Stock (HR)} - \text{Soft Reserved Stock (SR)} - \text{Quarantined Stock (QDS)} - \text{Safety Stock Buffer (SSB)}$$

	Inventory Reservation Process
To prevent race conditions during high-concurrency shopping events or flash sales, NovaCart enforces a strict two-stage stock reservation workflow:
1. Soft Reservation Workflow (Checkout Stage)
When a customer initiates checkout by clicking "Proceed to Pay", api.novacart.internal/oms issues a soft reservation command to inventory.novacart.internal. The system decrements the ATP count and applies a 15-minute soft lock.
* If payment authorization succeeds (AUTHORIZATION_SUCCESS) within 15 minutes, the soft lock converts automatically to a Hard Lock.
* If payment fails or the session times out (15 minutes elapsed), the soft lock expires automatically, returning the reserved units to the active ATP pool (PAY-001, PAY-002).
2. Hard Reservation Workflow (Order Execution Stage)
Upon payment confirmation, the order transitions to PAYMENT_CONFIRMED, and the soft lock converts to an immutable Hard Lock. Stock remains hard-reserved until warehouse picking and FC dock departure scan (SHIPPED), at which point On Hand and Hard Reserved counts decrement simultaneously.

	Inventory Synchronization Process
To maintain absolute inventory accuracy across multiple storefronts, mobile applications, marketplace channels, and warehouse nodes, NovaCart operates an event-driven synchronization pipeline managed by api.novacart.internal/inventory/sync.
Synchronization Frequencies & Telemetry Standards
* Real-Time Event Streams: Every inventory state change publishes an immediate event payload to the central Apache Kafka message bus.
* Storefront Cache Refresh: Storefront edge caches and Product Display Pages (PDP) update within $< 1\text{ second}$ of a Kafka inventory delta event.
* 3P Marketplace Channel Sync: Inventory balances synced to external marketplace channels (e.g., Amazon, partner portals) execute via webhook updates every 5 minutes.
* Full Reconciliation Audit: Batch reconciliation jobs audit physical WMS counts against ATP logs daily at 03:00 AM local time.

	Backorder Execution Rules
1. Inbound PO Verification: An item is eligible for backordering only if a confirmed Vendor PO is registered in inventory.novacart.internal with a guaranteed warehouse delivery date within 14 calendar days.
2. Capped Backorder Quantity: Total backorder allocations cannot exceed 80% of the confirmed inbound PO quantity. The remaining 20% is reserved as a buffer for inbound transit discrepancies.
3. Explicit Delivery SLA Display: The product page and checkout screen must display an explicit backorder shipping commitment (e.g., "Backorder Item - Ships by August 12, 2026").
4. Auto-Fulfillment Priority: Arriving replenishment stock at the FC is hard-allocated automatically to backorders in strict chronological sequence (First-In, First-Out by order timestamp).
Pre-order Management
Pre-orders enable advance customer purchasing for newly announced or upcoming products prior to official commercial launch dates.
Pre-order Execution Rules
* Factory Allocation Guarantee: Pre-order listings require formal manufacturer allocation documentation confirming factory production quotas and guaranteed delivery dates to primary hubs.
* Launch Date Binding: Pre-orders cannot be dispatched prior to the official manufacturer embargo launch date. Fulfillment centers stage packed pre-orders 24 hours prior to launch date for synchronized launch-day delivery (SHIP-001, SHIP-003).
* Price Protection Guarantee: If the official retail price decreases between pre-order placement and launch dispatch, NovaCart automatically refunds the price difference to the customer's original payment method (PAY-003).

	Business Rules
Rule 1: Hard Floor Safety Buffer Rule
Every active product listing maintains a mandatory, system-enforced Hard Floor Safety Buffer ($\text{SSB} = 2 \text{ units}$ for standard products; $5 \text{ units}$ for high-velocity items). The central inventory engine sets $\text{ATP} = 0$ when physical On Hand stock reaches the SSB threshold, preventing final-unit overselling caused by concurrent local checkouts.
Rule 2: Absolute 15-Minute Reservation Expiry
Checkout soft locks expire strictly at 15 minutes (900 seconds) from initiation. No customer support agent or system routine may extend a soft reservation lock. Unpaid orders automatically transition to CANCELLED_TIMEOUT, and stock returns to the sellable ATP pool (ORD-001, PAY-002).
Rule 3: Quarantined Return Inventory Isolation
Items returned by customers (REF-002, REF-004) cannot be injected into active sellable ATP stock directly from FC intake docks. Returned items must enter the QUARANTINED state and pass a formal Quality Inspection (Grade A/B under REF-004) before WMS software transfers the units to pickable stock bins.
Rule 4: Manual Inventory Adjustments & Recounts
Manual inventory adjustments (e.g., correcting mislaid bin counts or recording warehouse damage) exceeding $5\text{ units}$ or $\text{INR } 10,000$ / $\text{SGD } 150$ / $\text{AED } 400$ require dual-control sign-off from the Inventory Control Manager in wms.novacart.internal. All physical recounts must update system ATP logs within 1 hour.
Rule 5: Conditions for Product Discontinuation
When a SKU is declared DISCONTINUED by Merchandising (PROD-001), replenishable PO creation is permanently disabled. Remaining stock is sold off until ATP $= 0$, at which point the status updates automatically to ARCHIVED.

	Practical Examples
Scenario 1: Concurrent Checkout during Flash Sale
1. Customer A and Customer B initiate checkout simultaneously for the final smartphone unit ($\text{ATP} = 1$) at 10:00:00 AM.
2. Customer A's checkout request reaches inventory.novacart.internal at 10:00:00.100 AM. System grants a 15-minute soft lock ($\text{Soft Reserved} = 1, \text{ATP} = 0$).
3. Customer B's request reaches the engine at 10:00:00.150 AM. System detects $\text{ATP} = 0$ and rejects the cart reservation, displaying "Item Sold Out" to Customer B.
4. Customer A completes payment authorization at 10:02:15 AM. Soft lock converts to Hard Allocated Stock, and order moves to PAYMENT_CONFIRMED (ORD-001).
Scenario 2: Automated Re-Sourcing for Warehouse Stock Mismatch
1. Order #NC-IN-88201 for a gaming laptop is placed and allocated to local hub BLR-01.
2. FC picker arrives at bin location and discovers the physical box is damaged (liquid leak from adjacent bin). Picker logs BIN_DAMAGE_FLAG in WMS.
3. WMS sets the local unit to QUARANTINED and triggers exception EXC-INV-01.
4. The central Sourcing Engine (api.novacart.internal/oms/source) detects the mismatch and automatically re-routes the order allocation to secondary FC DEL-02 within 12 seconds. Order fulfills without customer delivery delay (ORD-005, SHIP-001).
Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
Q1: How long is an item held in my shopping cart during checkout?
Answer: When you click "Proceed to Pay" and enter the checkout session, stock is soft-reserved for exactly 15 minutes. If payment is not completed within 15 minutes, the reservation expires automatically, the items return to active stock, and the unpaid checkout session cancels (Refer to ORD-001, PAY-002).
Q2: What happens if an item I ordered becomes out of stock due to a warehouse error?
Answer: If a physical stock discrepancy occurs at the assigned warehouse, our automated sourcing engine immediately attempts to fulfill your order from another regional fulfillment center at no extra charge. If stock is unavailable nationwide, you will receive an immediate notification, a 100% full refund to your original payment method, and a ₹250 goodwill store credit (Refer to PAY-003, REF-006).
Q3: Can I cancel a backorder or pre-order if the estimated shipping date changes?
Answer: Yes. You may cancel any backorder or pre-order penalty-free at any time prior to warehouse packaging dispatch by clicking "Cancel Order" under My Orders in the mobile app or website, triggering an immediate 100% full refund (Refer to ORD-002).
