Purpose
This document defines the official Product Recall & Safety Policy of NovaCart Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "NovaCart"). It establishes standardized enterprise procedures, governance structures, recall classification levels, risk assessment protocols, customer notification standards, inventory isolation controls, and regulatory compliance rules governing product safety incidents, manufacturer-initiated recalls, regulatory directives, and voluntary platform withdrawals.
As a core governance document (PROD-005) in the Products module of the NovaCart Enterprise Knowledge Base (NEKB), PROD-005 coordinates crisis execution across Legal, Product Management (catalog.novacart.internal), Warehouse Operations (wms.novacart.internal), Logistics (tms.novacart.internal), and Customer Support (crm.novacart.internal), operating in strict operational alignment with COMP-001 through COMP-004, SUP-001 through SUP-005, ORD-001 through ORD-005, SHIP-001 through SHIP-007, PAY-001 through PAY-006, REF-001 through REF-006, PROD-001, PROD-002, PROD-003, and PROD-004.
Scope
This policy applies universally to all physical merchandise categories—Consumer Electronics (CAT-ELE), Mobile Devices (CAT-MOB), Gaming Hardware (CAT-GAM), Home Appliances (CAT-APP), and Smart Home Hardware (CAT-SMT)—offered across NovaCart's operating regions (India, Singapore, UAE) across all sales channels and fulfillment models (1P Direct Distribution and 3P Marketplace Merchants).
Coverage Includes
* Identification, triage, and risk assessment of product safety hazards, electrical risks, thermal/fire risks, battery defects, chemical contamination, choking hazards, software failures, and false product labeling.
* Managing manufacturer-initiated recalls, regulatory authority mandates, and NovaCart voluntary safety withdrawals.
* Real-time catalog delisting, checkout blocking, and warehouse inventory quarantine.
* Customer identification, multi-channel direct communication, and public safety advisory publishing.
* Product recovery, reverse logistics execution, customer remediation (full refund, free replacement, or certified repair), and regulatory audit logging.
Out of Scope
* Standard non-safety-related customer returns, exchanges, or size/color variant swaps (governed by REF-001 and REF-002).
* Routine post-warranty component repairs or isolated manufacturing defects not posing systematic safety hazards (governed by PROD-003).
Product Recall & Safety Overview
NovaCart prioritizes absolute consumer safety, regulatory compliance, and brand integrity above commercial interest. When a product sold on the platform is identified as defective, unsafe, or non-compliant with statutory safety standards, NovaCart executes immediate, systematic containment to eliminate risk to life, health, and property.
Core Operational Principles
1. Precautionary Principle: In cases of severe suspected safety hazards, NovaCart executes immediate catalog holds and warehouse quarantine prior to formal risk assessment conclusion.
2. Zero-Delay Isolation: Upon safety alert validation, checkout is blocked in catalog.novacart.internal within 15 minutes and warehouse picking is locked in wms.novacart.internal within 30 minutes.
3. Comprehensive Customer Tracing: Utilizing immutable transaction logs in api.novacart.internal/oms and serial/IMEI tracking in wms.novacart.internal to identify 100% of affected end-users.
4. Full Frictionless Remediation: Affected customers receive complete financial or operational resolution (100% monetary refund, free upgraded replacement, or certified technician repair) without bearing logistics or shipping costs.

	Recall Initiation Process
A safety event or recall procedure is initiated through one of four operational triggers:






+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                        RECALL INITIATION & CONTAINMENT WORKFLOW                   |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 1. TRIGGER EVENT    | Safety alert received (OEM Notice, Regulatory Order, CS Audit)|
| 2. EMERGENCY BLOCK  | PIM executes catalog block; WMS locks warehouse inventory.    |
| 3. RISK ASSESSMENT  | Safety Committee assigns Recall Class (I, II, III) & SLA.     |
| 4. USER TRACING     | OMS extracts customer list matching affected Serial/IMEIs.    |
| 5. NOTIFICATION     | Multi-channel alert dispatched; reverse logistics initiated. |
| 6. REMEDIATION      | Customer receives 100% refund, replacement, or repair.        |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

1. Trigger Event Registration:
   * Trigger A (OEM Notice): Manufacturer formally notifies NovaCart Legal of a global or regional product recall.
   * Trigger B (Regulatory Mandate): Government authority issues a mandatory recall directive or sales prohibition.
   * Trigger C (Internal Quality Audit): FC Quality Inspection (REF-004) or Warranty Operations (PROD-003) detects a cluster of safety-related failures ($> 3\text{ reports}$ of fire, smoke, or shock within 7 days).
   * Trigger D (Customer Incident Report): Customer Support (SUP-001) logs a severe product injury or property damage claim.
2. Emergency Lock Execution: The Product Safety Response Team issues a system-wide safety freeze. catalog.novacart.internal transitions SKU status to RECALLED (PROD-001), blocking checkout instantly. wms.novacart.internal transfers all physical bin stock to QUARANTINED status (PROD-002), blocking warehouse picking.
3. Safety Committee Triage: The Executive Product Safety Committee convenes within 2 hours to evaluate evidence and assign formal Recall Classification (Class I, II, or III).

	Risk Assessment Methodology
* Severity Score: Evaluates potential harm (e.g., burn radius, electrical shock voltage, structural collapse).
* Probability Score: Evaluates failure rates based on customer incident logs, warranty claims (PROD-003), and FC return audits (REF-004).
* Contagion Scope: Determines whether the defect impacts a specific batch/lot number, a specific manufacturing date range, or the entire SKU production run.

Product Recovery Process
NovaCart provides friction-free, zero-cost recovery options for affected products in customer possession:
1. Prepaid Reverse Logistics: The customer clicks "Initiate Recall Return" in the alert notice. api.novacart.internal/tms generates a prepaid priority reverse waybill (SRV-EXP).
2. Doorstep Courier Collection: Reverse couriers collect the recalled item within 24 to 48 hours. Couriers issue a digital Proof of Pickup (POP) confirming serial number collection (REF-002).
3. Hazardous Cargo Handling: For Class I battery thermal risks (CAT-MOB/CAT-ELE), NovaCart dispatches specialized hazardous material packaging containers to the customer's residence to ensure safe transit.
4. Warehouse Quarantine Isolation: Inbound recalled items bypass standard return staging and are received directly into the FC Safety Vault (STG-RECALL_VAULT), physically segregated from sellable stock (PROD-002).

	Regulatory Compliance
NovaCart maintains strict compliance with national product safety regulatory bodies across its operating regions:
* India (Central Consumer Protection Authority - CCPA / BIS): Formal recall notice submitted to CCPA within 24 hours of Class I recall initiation under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.
* Singapore (Enterprise Singapore - ESG): Mandatory reporting of safety hazards and consumer injury incidents to Enterprise Singapore within 24 hours.
* UAE (Ministry of Industry and Advanced Technology - MoIAT / CPSC): Immediate notification to MoIAT and local municipal consumer protection divisions.
* Reporting Metrics: Weekly compliance status reports submitted to authorities detailing total units sold, total units recovered, customer notification reach percentages, and final disposal records.

	Business Rules
Rule 1: Instant Precautionary Delisting Rule
Any product listing associated with a verified Class I safety hazard report involving fire, smoke, explosion, or physical injury must be delisted instantly (STAGE-SPD / RECALLED in PROD-001) prior to formal committee review. Re-activation requires written sign-off from the Chief Legal Officer.
Rule 2: Zero-Cost Recovery Mandate
Customers shall never incur shipping, pickup, restocking, or administrative fees for participating in a product recall. All logistics and return costs are borne by NovaCart and subsequently billed to the manufacturer or 3P merchant (PAY-003).
Rule 3: Permanent Serial Number Blacklisting
Serial numbers, IMEIs, and MAC addresses associated with recalled units are permanently blacklisted in wms.novacart.internal. Blacklisted serial numbers can never be re-injected into sellable inventory, re-sold, or re-registered for warranty service (PROD-001, PROD-003).
Exception Handling
* Customer Refuses Recall Participation: If a customer declines to return a Class I recalled product after multiple contact attempts, NovaCart sends a final registered Legal Notice advising of severe safety risks, remotely deactivates digital/smart features where technically feasible (CAT-SMT/CAT-ELE), and logs the refusal for regulatory liability exemption.
* 3P Merchant Unresponsiveness: If a 3P marketplace merchant fails to cooperate during a manufacturer recall for products sold on NovaCart, NovaCart executes customer remediation directly, debiting the merchant's escrow payout balance and suspending merchant platform selling privileges (COMP-001, PAY-003).
Practical Examples
Scenario 1: Class I Recall for Thermal Battery Overheating (CAT-MOB)
1. Manufacturer notifies NovaCart Legal of a thermal runaway defect in a smartphone model battery (CAT-MOB).
2. Merchandising executes Rule 1: SKU delisted in PIM within 10 minutes (STAGE-SPD).
3. WMS locks 120 units in FC BLR-01 into STG-RECALL_VAULT within 20 minutes.
4. OMS extracts purchase records for 450 delivered units. Priority SMS, WhatsApp, and Email alerts dispatch within 2 hours.
5. Reverse couriers collect devices using specialized fire-retardant containers.
6. Customers receive an automated 100% monetary refund plus a 10% goodwill wallet credit (CRD-GDW) within 1 hour of courier pickup (REF-006, PAY-003).
Scenario 2: Regulatory Direct Recall for Uncertified Power Adapter (CAT-ELE)
1. BIS authority issues a sales prohibition notice for an uncertified laptop charger brick exhibiting electric shock risks.
2. Safety Committee classifies event as Class II Recall.
3. System alerts 1,200 affected customers via Email and In-App Portal.
4. Customers are provided prepaid return labels or the option to receive a certified, fully compliant replacement charger brick dispatched within 24 hours (REF-003).
Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
Q1: How will I know if a product I bought on NovaCart has been recalled?
Answer: If a product you purchased is subject to a recall, NovaCart will contact you directly via SMS, Email, WhatsApp, and In-App Push Notifications using the contact details on your account. For critical Class I recalls, our customer safety team will also attempt direct phone outreach (Refer to SUP-001).
Q2: Do I have to pay for shipping when returning a recalled item?
Answer: No. All recall returns and reverse courier pickups are 100% free of charge. NovaCart provides prepaid reverse shipping labels or schedules doorstep courier collection at zero cost to you (Refer to SHIP-001, REF-002).
Q3: What if I no longer have the original box for a recalled product?
Answer: Original packaging is not required to return a recalled item. You only need to hand over the physical product and its primary power components to our reverse courier (Refer to REF-004).
