Purpose
This document establishes the official enterprise policy, governance standards, product information specifications, catalog data quality requirements, approval workflows, and business rules governing product catalog management at NovaCart Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "NovaCart"). It defines the operational framework for creating, updating, enriching, categorizing, maintaining, and retiring physical and digital products within the enterprise Product Information Management system (catalog.novacart.internal).
As the foundational document (PROD-001) in the Products category of the NovaCart Enterprise Knowledge Base (NEKB), PROD-001 governs master catalog data integrity across e-commerce storefronts, mobile applications, warehouse management systems (wms.novacart.internal), order management systems (api.novacart.internal/oms), and third-party logistics integrations, maintaining complete consistency with COMP-001 through COMP-004, SUP-001 through SUP-005, ORD-001 through ORD-005, SHIP-001 through SHIP-007, PAY-001 through PAY-006, and REF-001 through REF-006.
Scope
This policy applies universally to all physical merchandise categories (CAT-ELE, CAT-MOB, CAT-GAM, CAT-APP, CAT-SMT), digital software products (CAT-SFT), digital gift cards (CAT-GFT), and subscriptions (CAT-SUB) offered across NovaCart's operating territories: India (IN), Singapore (SG), and the United Arab Emirates (UAE).
Coverage Includes
* Master product structure, Stock Keeping Unit (SKU) assignment logic, and parent-child variant grouping rules.
* Mandatory product attribute specifications, naming conventions, and 8-digit Harmonized System (HS) tariff code mappings.
* Product information enrichment, digital asset management (DAM), and high-resolution imagery standards.
* Catalog lifecycle transitions from initial vendor onboarding through active listing, seasonal suspension, and final product retirement.
* Product taxonomy classification, category reassignment procedures, and duplicate listing prevention algorithms.
Out of Scope
* Real-time stock level synchronization, warehouse safety stock calculations, and reorder point thresholds (governed by PROD-002).
* Final checkout pricing calculations, promotional coupon discounting, and regional tax Engine execution (governed by PAY-001 and COMP-001).
Product Catalog Overview
The enterprise product catalog serves as the single source of truth for all sellable items and bundle configurations within NovaCart. Product information governance ensures that product listings are legally compliant, taxonomically accurate, search-optimized, and free of misleading specifications, thereby minimizing customer return rates (REF-001, REF-002) and operational fulfillment errors.

Core Operational Principles
1. Single Source of Truth: All customer-facing and system-level product data must originate from catalog.novacart.internal. Ad-hoc modifications directly in storefront presentation layers are strictly prohibited.
2. Deterministic SKU Uniqueness: Every sellable variant (combination of model, size, color, storage capacity, power plug type) must possess an immutable, unique Stock Keeping Unit (SKU) identifier.
3. Mandatory Statutory Compliance: Product listings must include compulsory legal and regulatory metadata, including manufacturer details, country of origin, 8-digit HS Code, local tax classification, and safety compliance certifications prior to storefront publishing.
4. Data Completeness Gatekeeping: A product listing cannot transition to ACTIVE status without satisfying $100\%$ of mandatory category attributes and digital asset requirements.

	Product Creation Process
New product listings—whether initiated by 1P Merchandising teams or 3P Marketplace Merchants—follow a strict, multi-tier onboarding workflow:






+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                        PRODUCT CREATION & PUBLISHING WORKFLOW                      |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 1. DATA ENTRY     | Input parent/child attributes, EAN/UPC, brand, and HS code in PIM.   |
| 2. ASSET UPLOAD   | Upload minimum 3 studio photos and user manual to DAM server.    |
| 3. DUPLICATE CHECK| System scans GTIN/UPC and MPN for existing catalog matches.        |
| 4. QA & COMPLIANCE| Catalog QA audits formatting, grammar, and legal safety claims.  |
| 5. PUBLISH LOCK   | PIM transitions status to ACTIVE; pushes data to OMS and WMS.     |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

1. Vendor / Merchandising Draft Creation: The creator inputs core metadata into catalog.novacart.internal, specifying Global Trade Item Number (GTIN/UPC/EAN), Manufacturer Part Number (MPN), Brand Name, Product Title, Category ID, and Country of Origin.
2. Taxonomy & Attribute Mapping: The system enforces category-specific mandatory fields (e.g., screen size and battery capacity for CAT-MOB; wattage and energy rating for CAT-APP).
3. Automated Duplicate Screening: The PIM engine executes a real-time duplicate check matching GTIN, MPN, and standardized Title. If a duplicate match is identified ($> 95\%$ similarity score), the request is rejected, and the creator is instructed to link to the existing Master SKU.
4. Digital Asset Attachment: High-resolution product images (minimum 3 angles) meeting enterprise photography guidelines are uploaded to the Digital Asset Management (DAM) repository.
5. Quality Assurance & Regulatory Sign-Off: Catalog QA specialists review attribute completeness, text formatting, and regulatory disclosures. Upon sign-off, the record transitions to STAGE-ACT.

	Category Reassignment Process
Reassigning an existing active product to a new leaf category requires:
1. Formal request in catalog.novacart.internal submitted by Category Lead.
2. Automated check ensuring all mandatory target category attributes are fully populated.
3. Approval by Merchandising Lead and Supply Chain Systems Admin.
4. Background re-indexing of storefront search filters and WMS staging attributes within 2 business hours.
Product Update Process
Updates to active products are classified into two operational tiers:
* Minor Updates (Instant Publish): Correcting spelling errors in descriptions, adding secondary marketing images, or adding supplementary attribute tags. These updates execute immediately in PIM and propagate to storefront feeds within 15 minutes.
* Major Updates (Re-Review Required): Altering Product Title, GTIN/EAN, Brand, Physical Dimensions/Weight, Packaging Specifications, or HS Tariff Codes. Major updates trigger a temporary administrative hold on listing modifications, requiring Catalog QA sign-off within 12 business hours.
Product Retirement Process

	Retirement Execution Rules
1. Inventory Exhaustion: A product declared DISCONTINUED remains visible on the storefront marked "Discontinued - Limited Stock" until total national physical inventory reaches zero ($0$ units across all FCs).
2. Transition to Archived: Once physical inventory reaches zero and all outstanding customer returns/replacements are resolved (REF-001, REF-003), PIM automatically transitions status from DISCONTINUED to ARCHIVED.
3. SKU Permanence: Retired SKU strings are permanently locked and never reused for future product introductions.
Product Data Quality Standards
Catalog integrity is audited continuously by the PIM Data Quality Engine (api.novacart.internal/pim/audit). Product listings must maintain a Minimum Data Quality Score of $95\%$ to retain ACTIVE status.
Data Quality Scoring Dimensions
* Completeness ($40\%$ Weight): All mandatory fields, dimensional attributes, and statutory metadata populated.
* Asset Compliance ($30\%$ Weight): Primary image meets white-background, high-resolution specifications; no promotional watermarks or text overlays.
* Taxonomic Accuracy ($15\%$ Weight): Item correctly placed in Level 4 Leaf Category with valid HS Code.
* Text Standardization ($15\%$ Weight): Title adheres to corporate naming formulas; description free of HTML styling tags, competitor mentions, or phone numbers.
Customer-Facing Product Information
To protect consumers and prevent post-purchase disputes, customer-facing listings must provide complete operational clarity:
* In-Box Contents List: Explicit enumeration of every item, cable, adapter, and manual included in the retail packaging.
* Regional Compatibility Disclaimers: Clear labeling of regional electrical plug types (e.g., Type G for UK/UAE/SG, Type C/M for India), operating voltage ($110\text{V}$ vs. $220\text{--}240\text{V}$), and localized radio/telecom regulatory approvals.
* Dimension and Space Requirements: Large appliances (CAT-APP) must display mandatory minimum doorway clearance dimensions to prevent installation access blocking (Refer to SHIP-004, PROD-004).

	Business Rules
Rule 1: Master GTIN / UPC Uniqueness Mandate
No product listing may be published to ACTIVE status without a verified, valid GTIN, UPC, EAN, or ISBN barcode number registered with GS1. Custom internal GTIN ranges are permitted exclusively for private-label white-label items and custom multi-item bundles.
Rule 2: Parent-Child Variant Integrity Rule
Product variants (e.g., size, color, storage capacity) must be grouped under a single Parent Master Item record. Child variant SKUs must inherit core parent attributes (Brand, Category, Parent Description) while maintaining individual child attributes (Color Code, EAN, Individual Weight, Child SKU). Creating separate standalone product pages for simple color/size variants is strictly prohibited.
Rule 3: Anti-Watermark Asset Policy
Product images displaying promotional text overlays (e.g., "Best Seller", "Free Shipping"), third-party seller logos, phone numbers, or low-resolution pixelated graphics are auto-rejected by the DAM intake engine.
Exception Handling
* Emergency Product Safety Recall: Upon receiving a manufacturer or government regulatory safety recall notice, Legal & Compliance triggers an EMERGENCY_RECALL command in PIM. Within 15 minutes, affected SKUs transition to RECALLED status, storefront checkout is blocked, and WMS quarantines physical stock (Refer to ORD-002, SHIP-001).
* Temporary Manufacturer Image Shortage: If a newly launched product lacks studio photography meeting pure white background standards, the Category Manager may apply for a temporary 14-day "Asset Waiver." The PIM Engine permits listing activation using high-resolution manufacturer render assets, provided compliant imagery is uploaded within 14 calendar days.
Practical Examples
Scenario 1: Onboarding a New Smartphone Variant (CAT-MOB)
1. Merchandising receives onboarding files for a new smartphone in 2 colors (Black, Silver) and 2 storage tiers (128GB, 256GB).
2. PIM specialist creates Parent Master Record NC-MOB-APL-IP15 and generates 4 child variant SKUs:
   * NC-MOB-APL-IP15-128-BLK
   * NC-MOB-APL-IP15-128-SLV
   * NC-MOB-APL-IP15-256-BLK
   * NC-MOB-APL-IP15-256-SLV
3. Specialist inputs GTINs, HS Code 8517.13.00, dimensions, 5 white-background studio images, and in-box contents (USB-C cable).
4. Catalog QA verifies data completeness score ($100\%$) and approves publication. Parent listing goes live with interactive color/storage selectors.
Scenario 2: Handling Discontinued Electronics SKU
1. Manufacturer issues EOL notice for a gaming laptop model (NC-ELE-ASU-G14-2024).
2. Category Manager updates product status in PIM to DISCONTINUED.
3. Storefront listing updates automatically to display "Discontinued Model - Limited Quantities Available."
4. Once the final 3 units in FC BLR-01 are sold and delivered, PIM transitions SKU status to ARCHIVED, locking the catalog record permanently.
Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
Q1: Why was my product creation request rejected for "Duplicate GTIN"?
Answer: NovaCart enforces strict GTIN/UPC uniqueness to prevent catalog fragmentation. If the GTIN you entered is already assigned to an existing Master SKU in catalog.novacart.internal, your request will be rejected. You must link your offer to the existing Master SKU instead of creating a duplicate listing.
Q2: How long does it take for a new product listing to go live on the storefront?
Answer: Once all mandatory attributes, HS codes, and compliant images are submitted, Catalog QA completes review within 12 business hours. Upon approval, the listing propagates to storefront search engines and mobile apps within 15 minutes.
Q3: Can a retired or archived SKU string be reused for a newer model version?
Answer: No. SKU identifiers are immutable historical records used across financial ledgers, logistics manifests, and customer order histories. Even when a product is permanently archived, its SKU string is locked and cannot be reassigned to any future product (Refer to COMP-004).
