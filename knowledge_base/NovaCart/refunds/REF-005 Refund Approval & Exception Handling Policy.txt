Purpose
This document establishes the official enterprise policy, decision-making workflows, administrative authorization levels, exception management protocols, and governance standards for approving, overriding, or rejecting customer refund requests at NovaCart Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "NovaCart"). It defines the operational framework governing refund decisions after initial eligibility verification (REF-001) and physical Quality Inspection (REF-004) have been completed.
As a core governance document (REF-005) in the Refunds module of the NovaCart Enterprise Knowledge Base (NEKB), REF-005 enforces financial control, operational accountability, and audit compliance across Customer Support, Fraud Operations, and Financial Systems (crm.novacart.internal, rma.novacart.internal, ledger.novacart.internal), maintaining complete consistency with COMP-001 through COMP-004, SUP-001 through SUP-005, ORD-001 through ORD-005, SHIP-001 through SHIP-007, PAY-001 through PAY-006, and REF-001 through REF-004.
Scope
This policy applies universally to all monetary refund decisions, partial refund disbursements, policy overrides, and discretionary goodwill adjustments executed across NovaCart's operating regions (India, Singapore, UAE) across all commercial channels (B2C Retail, B2B SMB, Corporate Accounts, NovaCart Prime Memberships).
Coverage Includes
* Automated vs. manual refund decision thresholds.
* Financial approval authority tiers and escalation workflows.
* Partial refund calculations for missing accessories or unreturned items.
* Discretionary policy exceptions for VIP customers, logistics failures, and goodwill gestures.
* Fraud containment holds, disputed inspection reviews, and legal/regulatory compliance refunds.
* Audit logging, decision documentation, and refund case closure criteria.
Out of Scope
* Defining general refund eligibility limits or product return windows (governed by REF-001).
* Physical receiving, unboxing, and Quality Inspection (QI) grading procedures (governed by REF-004).
* Technical clearing, bank gateway reversals, and electronic fund transfers (governed by PAY-003).
Refund Approval Overview
Refund approval represents the formal administrative authorization that commits enterprise funds to a customer prior to electronic clearing. NovaCart balances automated, frictionless customer resolutions for low-risk transactions with rigorous multi-tier managerial oversight for high-value, ambiguous, or exception-based claims.

Core Operational Principles
1. Segregation of Duties & Financial Control: Financial approval thresholds are strictly bound to user roles in crm.novacart.internal to prevent single-agent fraud or unauthorized disbursements.
2. Automated-First Authorization: System algorithms approve $100\%$ compliant, low-risk refund transactions instantly upon trigger events (pre-dispatch cancellations, lost shipments, passed QI inspections).
3. Evidence-Backed Exception Overrides: Discretionary policy overrides require documented operational justification, risk assessment co-signoff, and mandatory supervisory approval.
4. Immutable Audit Governance: Every approval, override, rejection, and partial deduction is recorded permanently in system logs for financial control audits.
Approval Workflow
Refund decision-making follows a structured, three-tier operational pipeline:






+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                        REFUND DECISION PIPELINE                                   |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PATH A: AUTOMATIC APPROVAL | System validates QI Pass / Pre-Dispatch Cancel $\rightarrow$ Instant.|
| PATH B: STANDARD REVIEW    | L1/L2 CSR audits photos/logs $\rightarrow$ Approval within limits.|
| PATH C: EXCEPTION / OVERRIDE| L3 Manager / Fraud / Legal co-signoff $\rightarrow$ Manual Commit. |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

1. Trigger Ingress: OMS or RMA portal emits a refund authorization request following a pre-dispatch cancellation (ORD-002), transit loss declaration (SHIP-006), or warehouse Quality Inspection pass (REF-004).
2. System Rule Evaluation: The decision engine (api.novacart.internal/rma/evaluate) checks transaction value, risk score, claim history, and inspection grade.
3. Route A - Automatic Approval: If all automated criteria are satisfied, the system updates claim status to REFUND_APPROVED and transmits a clearing payload to ledger.novacart.internal (PAY-003).
4. Route B - Manual Review Queue: If the claim involves ambiguous evidence, missing accessories, or value exceeding auto-thresholds, the file routes to crm.novacart.internal for L1/L2 CSR review.
5. Route C - Supervisory Escalation: Claims exceeding agent approval limits, flagged for fraud, or seeking out-of-policy overrides escalate to L3 Management, Fraud Operations, or Legal Counsel.

	Manual Review Process
When a claim flags for manual review, the assigned agent executes a standardized three-step evaluation:
1. Cross-System Data Verification: Agent verifies Order ID in OMS (ORD-001), courier tracking logs in TMS (ORD-004), and physical unboxing photos/scale weights in rma.novacart.internal (REF-004).
2. Account Risk & Claim Frequency Check: Agent reviews customer account history in shield.novacart.internal. Accounts displaying a high refund frequency ($> 30\%$ of total orders refunded) or linked fraud flags require L2 co-signoff (SEC-004).
3. Decision Execution: Agent logs the decision code, enters mandatory justification notes, attaches supporting documents, and sets status to REFUND_APPROVED, PARTIAL_REFUND_APPROVED, or REFUND_REJECTED.

	Customer Communication
Customers receive transparent, professional notifications detailing approval outcomes and financial timelines:
* Full Approval Alert: Email/SMS confirming 100% refund approval, specifying the original payment method, approved amount, and banking reflection window ($3\text{ to }5\text{ business days}$) (PAY-003).
* Partial Approval Alert: Email/SMS detailing line-item breakdowns, deductions for missing accessories or re-stocking fees, attached inspection photos, and net approved refund value.
* Rejection Alert: Formal written communication detailing specific rejection reasons (e.g., serial mismatch, physical damage, window expiry), attaching inspection photo evidence, and providing formal appeal instructions (SUP-001).
Decision Documentation Requirements
Every refund decision file must maintain an immutable audit trail in crm.novacart.internal:
1. Decision Record Parameters: Linked Master Order ID, Sub-Order ID, RMA ID, Agent User ID, Timestamp, and Financial Transaction Reference (RFT-XXXXX).
2. Mandatory Justification String: Free-text narrative explaining the operational justification for manual approvals or policy overrides (minimum 25 words).
3. Evidence Attachments: Copies of customer photos, technician reports, CCTV footage screen captures, or carrier weight audit receipts.
4. Co-Signoff Digital Stamp: Dual-token approval stamp for decisions requiring L2/L3 or Fraud co-signoff.

	Business Rules
Rule 1: Automated Approval Triggers
The system decision engine must automatically approve a 100% full refund without manual human intervention when any of the following deterministic events occur:
* Order cancelled prior to FC dispatch (PACKED or earlier) under ORD-002.
* Shipment declared lost in transit by Logistics (CANCELLED_LOST_IN_TRANSIT) under SHIP-006.
* Inbound return receives a Grade A or Grade B Quality Inspection pass (INSPECTION_PASSED) under REF-004.
Rule 2: Dual-Control Managerial Override Mandate
No individual agent or manager may approve a refund exceeding ₹25,000 / $400 / AED 1,100 that violates standard policy rules (e.g., claim lodged past return window or missing original packaging) without explicit, system-logged co-signoff from two authorized Tier 3 or Tier 4 managers.
Rule 3: Partial Refund Deduction Rules
When an item is returned with missing non-essential accessories or light packaging wear (Grade D under REF-004), the approving agent must apply standardized deduction formulas:
* Missing minor accessory (cables, manuals): Deduct fixed retail accessory value ($\text{INR } 500\text{--}1,500$).
* Missing primary component (remote, power brick): Deduct $20\%$ of line-item gross value.
* Damaged/missing original display box: Deduct $10\%$ re-stocking fee.

	Practical Examples
Scenario 1: Automated Refund Approval (Lost Shipment)
1. Laptop order #NC-IN-88201 (₹55,000) shows no carrier tracking updates for 96 hours in Delhi transit.
2. Logistics declares the shipment lost and sets status to CANCELLED_LOST_IN_TRANSIT (SHIP-006).
3. System decision engine evaluates Rule 1, approves a 100% full refund automatically (REFUND_APPROVED), and transmits clearing payload to ledger.novacart.internal (PAY-003).
4. Customer receives automated Email/SMS confirmation containing original payment clearing details within 60 seconds.
Scenario 2: Managerial Override for VIP Customer Exception
1. NovaCart Prime VIP member (lifetime spend > ₹300,000) requests a return for a smartwatch (₹18,000) 9 days after delivery (2 days past the 7-day REF-001 claim window).
2. L1 agent flags claim as out-of-window and routes file to L3 Support Manager via Escalation Level 3.
3. Manager reviews account history in shield.novacart.internal, confirms high lifetime value and zero prior return abuse flags, and executes an EXC-VIP policy override.
4. Manager inputs mandatory narrative justification in crm.novacart.internal, applies dual-token co-signoff, and sets status to REFUND_APPROVED.
Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
Q1: How long does it take for NovaCart to approve a refund after an item is returned?
Answer: Returns received at our fulfillment center undergo physical Quality Inspection (QI) within 24 hours. If the item passes inspection, your refund is approved automatically within 1 hour of inspection completion (Refer to REF-002, REF-004).
Q2: Why was a partial deduction made from my approved refund amount?
Answer: Partial deductions occur if a returned item is missing original inbox accessories (e.g., power adapters, remote controls) or if the original manufacturer packaging is severely damaged or missing. The exact deduction reflects the standard replacement value of the missing component (Refer to REF-004).
Q3: Can a customer appeal a rejected refund decision?
Answer: Yes. If your refund request is rejected, you may file a formal appeal within 7 calendar days through Customer Support by providing additional evidence (e.g., clear unboxing video or official brand service technician report). A Senior QA Lead will review the file for a final ruling (Refer to SUP-001).
