Purpose
This document establishes the official enterprise policy, operational standards, inventory reservation workflows, replacement fulfillment procedures, and business rules governing product replacement requests at NovaCart Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "NovaCart"). It defines the end-to-end lifecycle of a replacement transaction after initial eligibility is confirmed under REF-001.
As a core operational document (REF-003) in the Refunds category of the NovaCart Enterprise Knowledge Base (NEKB), REF-003 enforces standardized replacement execution across Customer Support tools, reverse logistics networks, and warehouse fulfillment engines (api.novacart.internal/oms), maintaining complete consistency with COMP-001 through COMP-004, SUP-001 through SUP-005, ORD-001 through ORD-005, SHIP-001 through SHIP-007, PAY-001 through PAY-006, REF-001, and REF-002.
Scope
This policy applies universally to all physical merchandise replacement requests across NovaCart operating regions (India, Singapore, UAE), commercial channels (B2C Retail, B2B SMB, Corporate Accounts, NovaCart Prime Memberships), and product categories (CAT-ELE, CAT-MOB, CAT-GAM, CAT-APP, CAT-SMT).
Coverage Includes
* Replacement authorization for Dead on Arrival (DOA) units, manufacturing defects, transit damage, incomplete shipments, and wrong SKUs.
* Inventory availability verification, soft-locking, and automated re-sourcing algorithms.
* Replacement dispatch pipelines (Standard Replacement, Simultaneous Swap, Advance Replacement).
* Inbound Quality Inspection (QI) of defective/damaged original units and serial number matching.
* Resolution pathways for depleted replacement inventory, repeated replacements, and reverse transit loss.
Out of Scope
* Direct monetary refund processing (governed by PAY-003 and REF-001).
* General preference-based size/color variant exchanges (governed by REF-002).
* Post-window manufacturer warranty claims managed directly by brand service centers (governed by PROD-001).
Replacement Policy Overview
NovaCart prioritizes product replacements over order cancellations to restore customer utility rapidly when orders arrive damaged, defective, incomplete, or incorrect.

Core Operational Principles
1. Fulfillment Priority: Replacement orders receive priority warehouse picking and fast-track courier dispatch (SRV-EXP) to minimize customer resolution time.
2. Serial & Part Integrity: Replacement units must be identical factory-sealed SKUs possessing full manufacturer warranties, accessories, and documentation.
3. Inventory Soft-Reservation: Automated soft-locking of replacement stock during the request authorization phase to guarantee availability before customer handover.
4. Validation-Driven Resolution: Physical or digital evidence verification (technical logs, damage photos, or technician reports) is mandatory prior to replacement dispatch.

	Replacement Request Process
When an eligible issue occurs, the replacement request lifecycle operates through structured system stages:
1. Request Submission: The customer logs into My Orders, selects the affected Sub-Order ID, chooses Request Replacement, selects the defect/damage reason code, and uploads required media proof.
2. Automated Evidence Verification: The Returns Engine (rma.novacart.internal) validates image metadata, delivery timestamps in api.novacart.internal/tms, and account history.
3. Technical Assessment (where required):
   * For CAT-SMT / CAT-ELE, Support executes a 5-minute remote diagnostic call (SUP-001).
   * For CAT-APP (Large Appliances), an on-site technician visit is scheduled within 24 hours (PROD-004).
4. Approval & Order Creation: Upon claim validation, OMS creates a child Replacement Sub-Order (e.g., NC-IN-88201-R1) linked permanently to the Parent Master Order (ORD-001, ORD-005).
5. Reverse Collection & Dispatch Synchronization: Reverse courier pickup and replacement dispatch execute according to the assigned replacement model (Standard, Simultaneous Swap, or Advance Dispatch).

	Replacement Approval Workflow
System authorization rules govern approval permissions based on item valuation:
* Level 1 (Auto-Approval): Items valued $\le \text{INR } 2,500$ / $\text{SGD } 40$ / $\text{AED } 110$ with valid photo proof are approved automatically by rma.novacart.internal without human intervention.
* Level 2 (CSR Approval): Items valued up to $\text{INR } 25,000$ / $\text{SGD } 400$ / $\text{AED } 1,100$ require L1 Customer Support Representative (CSR) verification of damage photos or diagnostic logs (SUP-001).
* Level 3 (Management Approval): High-value electronics (CAT-ELE, CAT-MOB, CAT-APP) exceeding $\text{INR } 25,000$ / $\text{SGD } 400$ / $\text{AED } 1,100$ require L2 Support Specialist co-signoff and technician diagnostic verification (SUP-003, PROD-004).
Replacement Shipment Process
NovaCart operates three distinct replacement shipping models tailored to item category, customer tier, and risk level:
1. Standard Replacement (Default): Reverse courier collects the defective/damaged original item. Upon warehouse receipt and Quality Inspection (QI) pass at the FC (INSPECTION_PASSED), the replacement unit dispatches automatically via Express Shipping (SRV-EXP).
2. Simultaneous "Swap & Collect" (Express Delivery): The courier rider arrives with the replacement package, verifies the original item's physical condition and serial number at the doorstep, hands over the replacement box, and collects the original unit in a single visit.
3. Advance Replacement (NovaCart Prime VIPs & Corporate Accounts): The replacement unit dispatches immediately prior to return collection. The customer agrees to hand over the original defective unit within 5 business days, failing which the customer's payment method is charged for the replacement unit value (PAY-001).

	Customer Responsibilities
Customers requesting a product replacement must comply with the following operational mandates:
1. Accurate Defect Disclosures: Provide truthful descriptions and clear photo/video evidence of the defect or damage. False claims trigger account warning flags (SEC-004).
2. Data & Lock Removal: Completely erase personal data, accounts (iCloud, Google, Samsung Account), and security PINs/passwords from mobile devices (CAT-MOB) and laptops (CAT-ELE) prior to return collection.
3. Inclusion of Original Accessories: Hand over the defective item complete with its original manufacturer box, power adapters, cables, remote controls, manuals, and free promotional items.
4. Doorstep Presence: Ensure an authorized adult is present during the scheduled pickup/swap window with the original item ready for courier inspection.
Merchant Responsibilities
Marketplace merchants (3P sellers) fulfilling replacement requests must adhere to strict service levels:
1. Inventory Reservation SLA: Reserve replacement stock within 6 business hours of receiving an automated replacement notification from rma.novacart.internal.
2. Dispatch SLA: Pack and dispatch approved replacement units within 24 hours of reverse item dock arrival.
3. Depletion Notification: If replacement stock is depleted, the merchant must notify NovaCart Support within 6 hours to enable immediate customer conversion to a full refund (REF-001, PAY-003).

	Business Rules
Rule 1: Serial Number Matching Mandate
No replacement order file shall be closed, nor shall an advance replacement hold be released, if the serial number or IMEI of the returned unit fails to match the outbound dispatch log stored in wms.novacart.internal. Mismatched units are rejected and re-routed back to the customer (REF-001, REF-005).
Rule 2: Maximum Replacement Cap
An individual order item may be replaced a maximum of two (2) times for consecutive defects. If the second replacement unit also exhibits a manufacturing defect, the system automatically blocks further replacements and converts the resolution to a 100% Full Refund including all shipping fees (REF-001, PAY-003).

Returned original units must satisfy strict condition parameters during Quality Inspection (QI) at the FC:

	Exception Handling
* Replacement Inventory Depletion: If a replacement request is approved but inventory depletes across all FCs and merchants before dispatch, OMS halts fulfillment automatically. Support contacts the customer within 2 hours to offer: (1) an upgraded model at no additional charge, (2) an alternate color variant, or (3) an immediate 100% full refund via PAY-003.
* Replacement Order Lost in Transit: If a replacement shipment is declared lost in transit by carrier trace under SHIP-006, OMS automatically dispatches a second replacement unit via Express Shipping (SRV-EXP) without awaiting carrier insurance claims.
* Inspection Rejection (User-Induced Damage): If a returned original unit fails Quality Inspection due to liquid damage or serial mismatch, the replacement order is cancelled (or charged to the customer if advance dispatched). The original item is shipped back to the customer with an inspection report attached.
Practical Examples
Scenario 1: Dead on Arrival (DOA) Smartphone Replacement
1. Customer in Bangalore receives a smartphone (CAT-MOB) at 11:00 AM. The device fails to power on or take a charge.
2. Customer submits a replacement request via the app at 01:00 PM, attaching a video demonstrating power failure.
3. Support verifies evidence, confirms order is within 7 days, and approves a "Swap & Collect" replacement (NC-IN-88201-R1).
4. Next day at 02:00 PM, express rider arrives with new sealed smartphone box, verifies original device IMEI, hands over new box, and collects original unit. Total resolution time: 25 hours.
Scenario 2: Large Appliance On-Site Technician Replacement
1. Customer in Dubai receives a washing machine (CAT-APP) that displays a drum motor error upon first cycle.
2. Customer logs a replacement request. OMS schedules an on-site technician visit for next morning.
3. Brand technician visits, inspects unit, confirms factory motor fault, and issues digital DOA Certificate DOA-DXB-99201.
4. System verifies report in crm.novacart.internal, approves replacement, and dispatches a new washing machine from FC DXB-01 along with free removal of the defective unit (PROD-004).
Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
Q1: Will I be charged extra shipping fees for a product replacement?
Answer: No. All approved product replacements for damaged, defective, wrong, or incomplete items are processed completely free of charge, including reverse collection and express outbound delivery of the replacement unit (Refer to SHIP-001, SHIP-003).
Q2: What happens if the item I need replaced is out of stock?
Answer: If replacement stock is depleted nationwide, Customer Support will contact you to offer an upgraded model at no extra charge, an alternate color variant, or an immediate 100% full refund to your original payment method (Refer to REF-001, PAY-003).
Q3: Can I request a refund instead of a replacement if my item arrives defective?
Answer: Yes. If your item arrives Dead on Arrival (DOA), damaged, or defective within the valid claim window, you have the absolute right to choose between a free product replacement or a 100% full refund (Refer to REF-001).
