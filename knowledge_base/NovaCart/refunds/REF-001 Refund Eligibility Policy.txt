Purpose
This document defines the official enterprise policy, business criteria, category-specific rules, evidence requirements, and decision-making framework governing refund eligibility at NovaCart Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "NovaCart"). It establishes clear operational boundaries to evaluate customer, merchant, and system requests for monetary refunds prior to financial clearing.
As the primary policy document (REF-001) in the Refunds module of the NovaCart Enterprise Knowledge Base (NEKB), REF-001 provides the business logic for customer service agents, automated RMA portals, and marketplace merchants, ensuring strict policy alignment with COMP-001 through COMP-004, SUP-001 through SUP-005, ORD-001 through ORD-005, SHIP-001 through SHIP-007, and PAY-001 through PAY-006.
Scope
This policy applies universally across all commercial transactions, customer tiers (B2C Retail, B2B SMB, Corporate Accounts, NovaCart Prime Memberships), and operating regions (India, Singapore, UAE) serviced by NovaCart.
Coverage Includes
* Defining business qualification parameters for full, partial, and goodwill refunds.
* Category-level return and refund restrictions across all catalog verticals.
* Binding claim timeframes, required customer evidence, and merchant obligations.
* Administrative approval authority thresholds and escalation rules.
Out of Scope
* Financial clearing, bank gateway reversals, settlement mechanics, or payment instrument clearing (governed exclusively by PAY-003).
* Reverse logistics courier routing and warehouse physical inspection workflows (governed by REF-002 and REF-005).
Refund Eligibility Overview
NovaCart strives to maintain complete customer trust by offering fair, policy-driven refund qualifications while protecting the platform against fraudulent claims, return abuse, and unverified merchant losses.

Core Operational Principles
1. Evidence-Based Qualification: Claims involving physical defects, transit damage, or missing items require verifiable photographic/video proof or courier inspection reports before approval.
2. Category-Specific Governance: Product categories with hygiene, digital activation, or perishable constraints operate under distinct eligibility restrictions.
3. Fulfillment Status Independence: Orders cancelled pre-dispatch qualify for automatic refunds; delivered items require Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) and physical inspection unless explicitly waived (Refer to ORD-002, REF-005).
4. Non-Discretionary Compliance: Support agents and marketplace merchants must evaluate claims strictly using the decision matrix defined in this document.
Eligible Refund Scenarios
A transaction qualifies for a monetary refund under the following verified operational conditions:
* Pre-Dispatch Order Cancellation: Cancellation executed before warehouse dispatch (PENDING_PAYMENT to READY_FOR_DISPATCH) by the customer, merchant, or automated system (Refer to ORD-002).
* Physical Transit Loss: Shipment officially declared lost in transit by Logistics after exceeding trace SLA limits (Refer to SHIP-006).
* Unresolvable Delivery Delay: Domestic shipment delayed by $> 72\text{ hours}$ past committed ETA (or $> 14\text{ days}$ for international shipments) where the customer elects to cancel (Refer to ORD-002, SHIP-002, SHIP-005).
* Damaged or Defective Goods Delivered: Items arriving with physical transit damage, structural cracks, or manufacturing defects verified via photo evidence or technician inspection (Refer to REF-003, PROD-004).
* Incorrect Item or Missing Quantity: Package containing an incorrect SKU, wrong product variant, or missing units verified against warehouse dispatch weight logs (Refer to ORD-001, REF-005).
* Duplicate Order / Unlinked Payment Debit: System error resulting in multiple charges for a single order or an unlinked debit where stock is depleted (Refer to PAY-002, PAY-003).
Non-Eligible Refund Scenarios
Refund requests are strictly rejected under the following conditions:
* Expiration of Claim Window: Requests submitted after the mandatory category claim window has closed.
* Buyer’s Remorse on Non-Returnable Categories: Preference-based return requests for digital software keys (CAT-SFT), digital gift cards (CAT-GFT), or unsealed hygiene-sensitive items.
* Customer-Induced Damage: Physical damage, liquid exposure, or serial number tampering caused by customer misuse or improper installation (Refer to PROD-001).
* Unverified Non-Receipt Claims: Claims marked DELIVERED with valid, verified One-Time Password (OTP) or GPS-matched Proof of Delivery (POD) where Security audit confirms valid delivery (Refer to SHIP-001, SHIP-006).
* Customs Abandonment (DAP/DDU): International shipments abandoned or destroyed by destination customs due to customer refusal to pay statutory import duties or provide identity documents (Refer to SHIP-002).

	Customer Responsibilities
Customers initiating a refund request must comply with the following obligations:
1. Timely Lodgment: Submit claims within the mandated time window specified in the Refund Time Window Matrix.
2. Evidence Submission: Provide clear, unedited photographic or video evidence showing outer packaging, courier shipping label, and specific item damage/defect.
3. Item Care & Integrity: Retain original manufacturer packaging, user manuals, accessories, warranty cards, and free promotional bundles.
4. Device Sanitization: Remove all personal accounts, passwords, and security locks (e.g., iCloud, Google Account, PINs) from mobile devices (CAT-MOB) and laptops (CAT-ELE) prior to RMA pickup.
5. RMA Return Cooperation: Hand over the approved return package to NovaCart's designated reverse courier within 5 business days of RMA approval (Refer to REF-005).
Merchant Responsibilities
Marketplace merchants (3P sellers) operating on NovaCart must adhere to enterprise refund standards:
1. Mandatory Acceptance of Valid Claims: Merchants must honor refund approvals granted by NovaCart Support for verified defects, transit damage, or wrong items dispatched from their inventory.
2. Response SLA: Merchants must review and respond to flagged defect claims within 24 business hours. Failure to respond within 24 hours results in automatic claim approval in favor of the customer, with costs debited from the merchant's payout account.
3. Stock Accuracy: Merchants must maintain real-time inventory counts to prevent pre-dispatch stock-out cancellations (Refer to ORD-002).

	Business Rules
Rule 1: Master Serial Number Matching
No refund or replacement shall be approved for Consumer Electronics (CAT-ELE) or Mobile Devices (CAT-MOB) if the serial number/IMEI of the returned item does not match the outbound warehouse dispatch log recorded in wms.novacart.internal. Mismatched items are flagged as fraudulent and rejected (Refer to COMP-001, REF-005).
Rule 2: Automatic Approval for Lost Shipments
When a shipment is officially declared lost by Logistics (CANCELLED_LOST_IN_TRANSIT) under SHIP-006, the refund eligibility status transitions automatically to APPROVED. Customer Support must offer the customer an immediate 100% refund or free priority replacement without requiring further documentation.
Rule 3: Single Refund Disbursement Limit
An order or individual sub-order line item may be refunded only once. ledger.novacart.internal blocks any secondary refund request exceeding the original captured transaction value (Refer to PAY-003).
Exception Handling
* Out-of-Policy Escalation for VIP Accounts: NovaCart Prime VIP members or B2B Enterprise Accounts requesting out-of-policy refunds (e.g., claim lodged 2 days past window) may receive discretionary approval upon L3 Customer Support Manager co-signoff (Refer to SUP-003).
* Unresolvable On-Site Technician Delay: If an appliance defect claim (CAT-APP) requires an on-site technician inspection, but the brand fails to dispatch a technician within 5 business days, Customer Support is authorized to override the report requirement and approve an immediate refund or replacement (Refer to PROD-004).
Practical Examples
Scenario 1: Approved Transit Damage Claim (48-Hour Window)
1. Customer receives a television screen (CAT-APP) delivered at 02:00 PM on Tuesday.
2. Upon unboxing at 05:00 PM, the customer discovers a cracked display panel.
3. The customer submits photos of the damaged box and cracked screen via the portal at 06:30 PM (within 48 hours).
4. Customer Support verifies outer packaging damage notes in TMS, approves RMA for immediate reverse pickup, and authorizes a 100% full refund upon FC dock scan (Refer to REF-003, REF-005).
Scenario 2: Rejected Claim for Unsealed Digital Software
1. Customer purchases an antivirus software license key (CAT-SFT).
2. System delivers activation key to customer's secure portal account at 10:00 AM (DELIVERED).
3. Customer views key at 10:05 AM and submits a refund request at 11:00 AM stating "No longer needed."
4. System audit log confirms digital key was revealed. Support rejects claim under REF-006 rules for Non-Refundable Categories.
Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
Q1: How long do I have to request a refund after receiving my order?
Answer: Claim windows depend on the issue type and product category: transit damage or missing items must be reported within 48 hours of delivery. Product defects or wrong items must be reported within 7 days (10 days for large appliances). Claims submitted past these windows cannot be approved for standard refunds (Refer to SUP-001).
Q2: Will I get a full refund if I cancel my order before it ships?
Answer: Yes. Orders cancelled prior to warehouse dispatch (PACKED or earlier) receive an immediate 100% full refund, including item price, taxes, and shipping fees (Refer to ORD-002, PAY-003).
Q3: What happens if I return an item without its original box or accessories?
Answer: Returned items must include all original packaging, user manuals, and accessories. If an item is returned incomplete or with missing accessories, NovaCart may reject the refund or deduct a re-stocking fee from the total refund amount (Refer to REF-005).
