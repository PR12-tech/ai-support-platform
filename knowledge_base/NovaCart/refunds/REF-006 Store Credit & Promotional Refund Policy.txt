Purpose
This document establishes the official enterprise policy, issuance workflows, validity standards, customer communication protocols, and business rules governing non-cash refunds, platform store credits, promotional concessions, and goodwill compensations at NovaCart Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "NovaCart"). It defines the conditions under which refunds and customer resolutions are issued as digital wallet balances, promotional credits, gift card adjustments, or loyalty point reinstatements instead of direct monetary reversals to original payment instruments.
As a core operational document (REF-006) in the Refunds category of the NovaCart Enterprise Knowledge Base (NEKB), REF-006 governs non-cash financial clearing within ledger.novacart.internal and auth.novacart.internal, operating in strict alignment with COMP-001 through COMP-004, SUP-001 through SUP-005, ORD-001 through ORD-005, SHIP-001 through SHIP-007, PAY-001 through PAY-006, and REF-001 through REF-005.
Scope
This policy applies to all non-monetary refund instruments, platform store credits, customer service goodwill vouchers, promotional coupon adjustments, and digital wallet credits issued across all operating regions (India, Singapore, UAE) and commercial channels (B2C Retail, B2B SMB, Corporate Accounts, NovaCart Prime Memberships).
Coverage Includes
* Issuance and lifecycle management of NovaCart Wallet balances and platform store credits.
* Goodwill compensation vouchers issued for service level agreement (SLA) breaches (e.g., delivery delays, damaged packaging).
* Reinstatement and recalculation of promotional discounts, coupon codes, and loyalty reward points on returned or cancelled orders.
* Digital Gift Card refund processing and voucher re-issuance.
* Non-cash refund rules regarding validity horizons, transferability, account closures, and anti-abuse security controls.
Out of Scope
* Direct electronic monetary refunds routed to external credit cards, debit cards, UPI, or bank accounts (governed by PAY-003).
* General refund eligibility qualifications and claim time limits (governed by REF-001).
Store Credit & Promotional Refund Overview
NovaCart provides flexible, non-cash refund mechanisms designed to deliver instant customer resolution, eliminate banking processing delays, and incentivize platform retention. Store credit and promotional refunds allow customers to receive immediate credit for cancelled, returned, or delayed orders without awaiting external bank clearing cycles.

Core Operational Principles
1. Customer Choice & Instant Settlement: Customers may elect to receive approved refunds as instant NovaCart Wallet credits, bypassing the 3-to-5 business day banking delay associated with external card reversals (Refer to PAY-003).
2. Promotional Integrity Protection: Promotional credits and goodwill vouchers issued for customer concessions carry explicit expiration dates, non-transferability rules, and usage restrictions to prevent promotional gaming.
3. Pro-Rated Promo Recalculation: Returns or cancellations on multi-item orders with cart-level promotional discounts automatically trigger pro-rated discount re-allocations to prevent unearned discount retention.
4. Immutable Ledger Audit: Every store credit deposit, wallet deduction, promotional voucher issue, or expiry write-off is logged as a distinct cryptographic event in ledger.novacart.internal.

	Eligibility Criteria
Non-cash refunds and promotional compensation qualify under specific operational parameters:
* Customer Opt-In for Instant Refund: Customer explicitly chooses NovaCart Wallet Credit during order cancellation (ORD-002) or return request creation (REF-002) to receive funds instantly upon approval.
* Cash on Delivery (COD) Refunds: COD returns where the customer prefers instant platform wallet credit over providing external NEFT/RTGS bank details (Refer to PAY-001, PAY-003).
* Service SLA Breaches: Delivery delays exceeding policy commitments (SHIP-005), missed scheduled delivery time-slots (SHIP-004), or minor transit packaging blemishes where the customer retains the item in exchange for a goodwill concession.
* Promotional Order Returns: Returns of orders purchased using promotional discount codes, bundle pricing, or loyalty reward points.
Store Credit Issuance Process
When store credit is authorized by system automation or Customer Support, the issuance lifecycle operates within ledger.novacart.internal:
1. Authorization Registration: OMS or crm.novacart.internal submits a credit deposit payload (POST /v1/wallet/credit) specifying Customer ID, Amount, Currency, Reason Code, and Expiration Rule.
2. Ledger Commit: ledger.novacart.internal creates an atomic balance entry, updating the customer's active wallet balance and logging the transaction reference (WLT-XXXXX).
3. Account Linkage: The credit links to the customer's authenticated profile in auth.novacart.internal.
4. Customer Alert: System dispatches an instant SMS, WhatsApp, and Email notification detailing the credited amount, updated total balance, and validity period.
Promotional Credit Management
Promotional credits and coupon codes applied during order checkout operate under strict recalculation math during partial cancellations or partial returns:
Pro-Rated Promotional Recalculation Formula
When a customer purchases multiple items using a threshold discount (e.g., "Save ₹1,000 on orders over ₹5,000") and subsequently returns one item, the system recalculates eligibility:
* If the retained items still satisfy the original promotional threshold, the refund matches the full line-item price.
* If the retained items fall below the promotional threshold, the promotional discount is revoked or pro-rated proportionally across retained items, deducting the discount variance from the refund value.

	Gift Card Refund Process
In accordance with master payment processing rules (PAY-001, PAY-003):
* Single Instrument Fidelity: Refunds for items purchased using a NovaCart Digital Gift Card (PAY-GFT) cannot be issued as physical cash or transferred to external bank cards.
* Voucher Re-Issuance: Upon RMA approval (REF-005), ledger.novacart.internal generates a new, single-use Digital Gift Voucher code for the exact refunded value and dispatches it securely to the customer's registered email address.
* Split Payment Reversal Order: For orders paid using a combination of Gift Card and Credit Card/UPI, refunds clear first to the external Credit Card/UPI instrument up to the original charged value, with any remaining balance credited to the NovaCart Digital Gift Voucher (Refer to PAY-001).
Loyalty Reward Compensation
NovaCart Prime members and loyalty tier subscribers earn platform reward points on qualified purchases:
* Point Clawback on Returns: When an order is returned for a refund, any loyalty reward points earned on the returned items are automatically deducted from the customer's active loyalty point balance in auth.novacart.internal.
* Point Reinstatement on Cancellations: If an order purchased using redeemed loyalty reward points is cancelled or returned, the spent loyalty points are reinstated to the customer's balance within 24 hours with a refreshed 180-day validity window.
Customer Communication
Customers receive transparent, automated updates regarding non-cash credits and promotional adjustments:
Customer Communication Standards
* Deposit Notification: Dispatched within 60 seconds of credit commit, displaying the exact credit amount, credit type (Wallet / Goodwill / Gift Voucher), updated account balance, and clear expiration date.
* Pre-Expiration Reminder: Automated SMS and Email notification sent 14 days and 3 days prior to the expiration of any wallet credit or promotional voucher, urging utilization.
* Transaction History Visibility: Complete transaction ledger visible under My Account $\rightarrow$ NovaCart Wallet & Credits, displaying itemized deposits, order usage, and expiration timestamps.

	Business Rules
Rule 1: Non-Transferability & Anti-Encashment Mandate
Platform store credits, goodwill vouchers, and promotional credits are non-transferable between customer accounts and cannot be sold, auctioned, or converted into physical cash, except for verified COD refund deposits where statutory encashment applies via electronic bank transfer (Refer to PAY-001, PAY-003).

	Exception Handling
* Account Closure with Active Store Wallet Balance: If a customer requests permanent account closure under SEC-003 and holds an unexpired NovaCart Store Wallet balance (CRD-WAL) derived from returned goods or COD refunds, Finance Operations verifies identity and executes a direct electronic bank transfer (NEFT/RTGS/IMPS) to the customer's verified bank account prior to closing the account file (Refer to PAY-003).
* Fraudulent Credit Generation Attempts: If Security detects systematic creation of fake accounts to exploit goodwill concessions or promotional referral credits, shield.novacart.internal freezes all associated accounts, revokes active wallet balances, and logs an abuse report (Refer to SEC-001, SEC-004).
Practical Examples
Scenario 1: Customer Selecting Instant Wallet Refund for COD Return
1. Customer returns an apparel item (₹2,499) originally paid via Cash on Delivery (COD).
2. Upon initiating the return request in the app, the customer selects Instant NovaCart Wallet Refund instead of entering bank details.
3. Item is collected, arrives at FC BLR-01, and passes Quality Inspection (INSPECTION_PASSED) under REF-004.
4. OMS issues a credit payload to ledger.novacart.internal. ₹2,499 is deposited to the customer's NovaCart Wallet within 30 seconds.
5. Customer receives an SMS notification and uses the ₹2,499 wallet balance immediately to purchase a replacement item.
Scenario 2: Goodwill Compensation for Express Delivery Delay
1. Customer in Dubai pays AED 25 for Priority Same-Day Delivery (SRV-SDY) on a replacement monitor.
2. Due to a courier vehicle breakdown, the delivery arrives the following morning (12 hours late) (Refer to SHIP-003, SHIP-005).
3. System exception engine flags the late delivery SLA breach automatically.
4. System executes Rule 2: AED 25 shipping fee is refunded to the customer's credit card via PAY-003, and an AED 15 Goodwill Wallet Credit (CRD-GDW) valid for 90 days is deposited to the customer's NovaCart Wallet.
Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
Q1: Why is a NovaCart Wallet refund faster than a refund to my credit card?
Answer: NovaCart Wallet credits execute directly within our internal financial ledger (ledger.novacart.internal) in under 1 minute. Credit or debit card refunds require processing through external acquiring banks, payment gateways, card networks (Visa/Mastercard), and your issuing bank, which takes 3 to 5 business days (Refer to PAY-003).
Q2: What happens to my store credit if I don't use it before it expires?
Answer: Store credit balances resulting from returned items (CRD-WAL) remain active for 365 days. We send automated reminders 14 days and 3 days before expiration. If you are unable to use your store credit before it expires, you may contact Customer Support prior to the expiration date to request a one-time 90-day validity extension (Refer to SUP-001).
Q3: Can I combine my store credit balance with a promotional discount code during checkout?
Answer: Yes. Your NovaCart Store Wallet balance represents real monetary credit on your account and can be applied alongside promotional coupon codes, promotional discounts, and free shipping offers during checkout (Refer to PAY-001).
