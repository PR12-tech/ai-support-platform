Purpose
This document establishes the official enterprise policy, operational standards, data ingestion criteria, settlement verification workflows, variance resolution protocols, and financial reporting standards governing payment reconciliation at NovaCart Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "NovaCart"). It defines the multi-tier matching processes required to reconcile order ledgers, payment gateway settlement files, acquiring bank statements, refund debits, and chargeback adjustments.
As a core financial governance document (PAY-005) in the NovaCart Enterprise Knowledge Base (NEKB), PAY-005 ensures zero financial variance across ledger.novacart.internal, merchant escrow accounts, and corporate bank accounts, operating in strict alignment with COMP-001 through COMP-004, SUP-001 through SUP-005, ORD-001 through ORD-005, SHIP-001 through SHIP-007, and PAY-001 through PAY-004.
Scope
This policy applies universally to all post-transaction financial reconciliation activities conducted across NovaCart’s operating regions (India, Singapore, UAE), supported payment channels (PAY-CARD, PAY-UPI, PAY-NB, PAY-WAL, PAY-BNPL, PAY-GFT, PAY-COD), and commercial models (1P Direct Sales and 3P Marketplace Merchants).
Coverage Includes
* Automated daily automated reconciliation ($T+1 / T+2$) between Order Management System (api.novacart.internal/oms) records, Payment Gateway (PG) settlement reports, and corporate bank statements.
* Settlement verification, fee deduction audits, and cross-currency exchange variance checks.
* Matching refund debits (PAY-003) and chargeback provisional adjustments (PAY-004) against acquiring settlement files.
* Variance identification, exception classification, manual ledger adjustments, and audit logging.
* Financial month-end close procedures, merchant escrow split verification, and executive reporting.

Payment reconciliation is the post-transaction accounting process that verifies whether every customer payment authorized on the platform successfully completes its financial journey through payment gateways, acquiring networks, and corporate bank accounts.

Core Operational Principles
1. Three-Way Matching Architecture: Continuous verification matching Order Records (OMS) $\longleftrightarrow$ Gateway Settlement Logs (PG) $\longleftrightarrow$ Bank Account Deposits (Bank Statements).
2. Zero Unreconciled Variance Standard: Every monetary discrepancy, unlinked credit, or fee mismatch must be classified, investigated, and cleared within mandatory SLA targets.
3. Automated Ingestion First: Utilizing automated batch processing scripts in ledger.novacart.internal to execute 99%+ of routine daily transaction matching without manual intervention.
4. Immutable Audit Trails: Systematically logging all manual ledger adjustments, reason codes, and supervisory approvals for internal and statutory financial audits.
Reconciliation Objectives
NovaCart enforces daily payment reconciliation to achieve four strategic financial objectives:
1. Financial Integrity & Loss Prevention: Detect uncaptured transactions, unlinked debits, duplicate payouts, and unauthorized fee deductions before they impact financial statements.
2. Accurate Merchant Settlements: Ensure 3P marketplace sellers receive precise net payouts after deducting correct platform commissions and processing fees (Refer to COMP-001).
3. Statutory Tax Compliance: Reconcile gross order values against regional tax collection accounts (GST in IN, VAT in UAE) to guarantee accurate statutory tax filings.
4. Rapid Customer Issue Resolution: Identify failed refunds (PAY-003) or pending payment drops (PAY-002) proactively to enable immediate support remediation.

Daily Processing Schedule
* 01:00 AM Local Time: Automated API ingestion of trailing day ($T-1$) PG settlement files from Razorpay, Stripe, PayU, Telr, and Tabby.
* 02:00 AM Local Time: Automated batch execution of the Three-Way Matching Engine (api.novacart.internal/pay/reconcile).
* 04:00 AM Local Time: System generates the Daily Exception Summary Report and populates manual review queues in ledger.novacart.internal.
* 09:00 AM Local Time: Finance Operations specialists begin manual investigation of open reconciliation exceptions.
Data Sources for Reconciliation
Reconciliation algorithms consume structured financial feeds from three independent operational data sources:
1. Order Management System (OMS) Records: Primary transactional record generated at checkout (api.novacart.internal/oms), containing Master Order ID, Sub-Order IDs, Gross Order Value, Tax Breakdown, Shipping Fees, and Payment Method Token (Refer to ORD-001).
2. Payment Gateway (PG) Settlement Files: Electronic settlement reports provided by payment processors, detailing Gateway Transaction ID, Authorized Amount, Captured Amount, Processing Fee Charged, Tax Deducted at Source (TDS/TCS), Net Settlement Amount, and Settlement Batch ID.
3. Acquiring Bank Statement Feeds: Direct MT940 / CAMT.053 electronic bank feeds or daily statement downloads from corporate acquiring banks (e.g., HDFC, ICICI, DBS, FAB), detailing actual net credit deposits received in NovaCart accounts.

	Refund Reconciliation
Refund reconciliation verifies that every internal refund instruction authorized under REF-001 and processed under PAY-003 successfully debits the original payment gateway account and adjusts corporate accounting ledgers:
* Matching Logic: RFT-ID (Internal Refund ID) $\longleftrightarrow$ RF-ID (PG Refund ID) $\longleftrightarrow$ PG Settlement Debit.
* Partial & Split Refund Verification: Reconciles pro-rated refund debits generated by split-order returns (ORD-005) or item cancellations (ORD-002) against remaining original order capture balances.
* Failed Refund Interception: Detects cases where a refund was marked REFUND_SUBMITTED in OMS, but the payment gateway returned a REFUND_FAILED settlement debit status, triggering automated re-routing to NEFT bank transfer (Refer to PAY-003).
Chargeback Reconciliation
Chargeback reconciliation tracks provisional financial debits and representment credits resulting from bank payment disputes managed under PAY-004:
* Provisional Debit Matching: Reconciles provisional chargeback debits and non-refundable dispute processing fees ($\text{INR } 500$ / $\text{SGD } 25$ / $\text{AED } 50$) imposed by acquiring banks against chargeback.novacart.internal case files.
* Representment Win Reversal Matching: Matches dispute reversal credits (DISPUTE_WON) received from acquiring banks, restoring provisionally debited funds to active corporate ledgers.
* Dispute Loss Write-Off: Formally registers permanent financial losses (DISPUTE_LOST) into corporate bad debt accounts (Refer to PAY-004).

	Business Rules
Rule 1: Daily Three-Way Matching Mandate
All transaction records generated on day $T$ must undergo three-way matching by 04:00 AM on day $T+1$. Un-reconciled transactions older than 48 hours trigger an automated alert to the Head of Payment Operations.
Rule 2: Dual-Control Manual Ledger Adjustment Rule
No single Finance Specialist may manually adjust a reconciliation ledger entry or create a manual balancing credit exceeding ₹10,000 / $150 / AED 400 without digital co-signoff from the Head of Payment Operations in ledger.novacart.internal.
Rule 3: Merchant Payout Hold Condition
If an order record exhibits an open reconciliation exception (EXC-001 through EXC-005), the associated merchant net payout is automatically placed on PAYOUT_HOLD status until the exception is resolved and closed by Finance Operations.
Exception Handling
* Unresolvable Bank Discrepancies ($> 30\text{ Days}$): If a bank or gateway settlement discrepancy remains unresolved after 30 days of active investigation and formal PG dispute lodging, the variance is escalated to the Chief Financial Officer for write-off authorization to corporate loss accounts.
* Gateway Systemic API Failures: If a payment gateway's reporting API experiences an outage, ledger.novacart.internal falls back to automated parsing of daily SFTP settlement CSV files, ensuring reconciliation processing continues without operational interruption.
Practical Examples
Scenario 1: Successful Three-Way Payment Reconciliation
1. Order #NC-IN-88201 (Value: ₹10,000) is placed at 10:00 AM. OMS records gross charge ₹10,000.
2. Razorpay PG captures ₹10,000, applies $1.5\%$ contracted processing fee (₹150) plus GST on fee (₹27), and generates net settlement value ₹9,823.
3. Next morning at 01:00 AM, Razorpay settles ₹9,823 into NovaCart’s HDFC corporate bank account.
4. At 02:00 AM, ledger.novacart.internal matches OMS Order ID NC-IN-88201 = PG Ref PG-99201 = HDFC Bank Credit ₹9,823.
5. System status updates to RECONCILED_SUCCESSFULLY. Merchant payout queue is approved for processing.
Scenario 2: Gateway Fee Discrepancy Resolution (EXC-003)
1. Order #NC-SG-40012 (Value: SGD $500) is processed via Stripe PG. Contracted fee rate is $1.8\%$.
2. Stripe settlement report lists fee deducted as $2.5\%$ (SGD $12.50$) instead of $1.8\%$ (SGD $9.00$), resulting in an SGD $3.50$ fee overcharge.
3. Automated matching engine flags variance and creates exception EXC-003 (Fee Overcharge).
4. Finance Specialist reviews contracted rate matrix, confirms overcharge, and submits an automated dispute ticket to Stripe account management. Stripe credits SGD $3.50$ back on the next settlement batch.
Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
Q1: Why does NovaCart perform daily payment reconciliation?
Answer: Daily reconciliation ensures that every customer payment is accurately captured, PG fees are billed correctly, bank deposits match accounting records, and marketplace sellers receive exact payouts without financial drift or loss (Refer to PAY-001).
Q2: What happens if my bank account was debited, but NovaCart's system initially showed no order?
Answer: Our automated 3-way reconciliation engine detects unlinked debits (EXC-001) during daily processing. If a valid payment is confirmed, the system either automatically confirms your order or initiates an immediate full refund back to your bank account (Refer to PAY-002, PAY-003).
Q3: Who audits NovaCart's payment reconciliation records?
Answer: Reconciliation records, manual ledger adjustments, and variance logs are audited continuously by internal risk teams, external financial auditors during annual audits, and regulatory tax compliance authorities (Refer to COMP-001).
