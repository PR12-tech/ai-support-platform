Purpose
This document establishes the official enterprise policy, investigation workflows, evidence representment criteria, banking response timelines, and legal dispute parameters governing payment chargebacks and payment service provider disputes at NovaCart Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "NovaCart"). It defines operational protocols for identifying, contesting, managing, and resolving payment disputes initiated by cardholders through issuing banks, acquiring banks, or payment gateways.
As a core risk and financial governance document (PAY-004) in the NovaCart Enterprise Knowledge Base (NEKB), PAY-004 enforces systematic defense against unauthorized claims and friendly fraud across ledger.novacart.internal, chargeback.novacart.internal, and shield.novacart.internal, operating in strict alignment with COMP-001 through COMP-004, SUP-001 through SUP-005, ORD-001 through ORD-005, SHIP-001 through SHIP-007, and PAY-001 through PAY-003.
Scope
This policy applies universally to all financial payment disputes, credit card chargebacks, debit card reversals, UPI dispute notices, and payment gateway claims raised across all NovaCart operating entities (India, Singapore, UAE), customer categories (B2C Retail, B2B SMB, Corporate Enterprise Accounts), and payment instruments (PAY-CARD, PAY-UPI, PAY-WAL, PAY-BNPL).
Coverage Includes
* Managing card brand chargebacks (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, RuPay).
* Managing payment gateway disputes and claims (Razorpay, Stripe, PayU, Telr, Tabby).
* Compelling evidence collection, representment file preparation, and submission workflows.
* Handling "Friendly Fraud" (legitimate purchases falsely claimed as unauthorized or unreceived).
* Managing pre-arbitration, card brand arbitration, and final financial adjustments.
* Account containment actions for high-risk accounts generating fraudulent disputes.
Out of Scope
* Direct customer refund processing for valid order cancellations or authorized returns managed via merchant channels (governed by PAY-003, REF-001, and REF-005).
Chargeback & Payment Dispute Overview
A Chargeback is a formal financial dispute initiated by an account holder directly through their card issuing bank, payment gateway, or financial institution, bypassing merchant customer support channels. The issuing bank executes a provisional reversal of transaction funds from NovaCart’s acquiring account while the claim undergoes formal investigation.

	Chargeback Lifecycle
Every chargeback notice passes through an explicit six-stage financial lifecycle managed within chargeback.novacart.internal:
1. Dispute Ingress & Provisional Debit: Issuing bank notifies acquiring gateway; funds are provisionally debited from NovaCart’s escrow account along with a non-refundable dispute processing fee ($\text{INR } 500$ / $\text{SGD } 25$ / $\text{AED } 50$).
2. System Alert & Intake: Gateway transmits webhook alert to chargeback.novacart.internal. Status updates to DISPUTE_RECEIVED.
3. Internal Case Investigation: Finance Operations & Fraud Specialists evaluate transaction logs, delivery PODs, and IP logs to decide whether to Accept or Contest (Representment).
4. Representment File Submission: If contested, Finance compiles a compelling evidence package and submits it to the acquiring bank prior to the network deadline. Status updates to REPRESENTMENT_SUBMITTED.
5. Bank Re-Evaluation: Issuing bank reviews evidence file ($30\text{ to }60\text{ days}$).
6. Final Adjudication:
   * Dispute Won (Reversal): Issuing bank rules in favor of NovaCart; provisionally debited funds are returned to NovaCart’s bank account. Status updates to DISPUTE_WON.
   * Dispute Lost: Issuing bank rules in favor of cardholder; provisional debit becomes permanent. Status updates to DISPUTE_LOST.

When a dispute notification is received, Finance Operations executes a structured investigation workflow within 48 hours:

Decision Criteria: Accept vs. Contest
* Accept Dispute: Executed if internal logs show clear merchant error (e.g., wrong item sent, confirmed lost parcel without replacement, or duplicate debit un-refunded). Provisional debit accepted; case closed as DISPUTE_ACCEPTED.
* Contest Dispute (Representment): Executed if logs show valid delivery POD (OTP verified), 3DS/MFA authorization, active user account usage, or unreturned merchandise. Representment file compiled.

	Customer Communication Process
While formal disputes are arbitrated through banking networks, Customer Support maintains communication rules to resolve legitimate customer confusion:
1. Information Verification Contact: If a dispute appears to be "Friendly Fraud" (e.g., customer forgot a purchase or family member used card), Support contacts the account holder to clarify order details.
2. Withdrawal Request: If the customer acknowledges the purchase, Support provides instructions for the customer to issue a formal "Dispute Withdrawal Request" to their issuing bank.
3. Professional Neutrality: CSRs must adhere to SUP-002 guidelines, avoiding hostile accusations while explaining that banking disputes freeze account privileges pending resolution.
Payment Gateway & Banking Coordination
NovaCart coordinates with acquiring partners to ensure representment files meet card network formatting standards:
* Acquirer Response SLAs: Representment evidence files must be transmitted to acquiring gateways within strict business day deadlines.
* Electronic File Transmission: Evidence packages are uploaded as secure, unified PDF documents via gateway portal APIs ([https://chargeback.novacart.internal/api/v1/submit-evidence](https://chargeback.novacart.internal/api/v1/submit-evidence)).

	Business Rules
Rule 1: Automatic High-Risk Account Containment
Any customer account associated with a payment chargeback initiated under reason code "Fraudulent Transaction" (FAIL-FRD) is automatically placed on temporary administrative hold by shield.novacart.internal. Active orders in PROCESSING or PACKED status are placed on shipping hold, and future Cash on Delivery (COD) options are restricted pending dispute resolution (Refer to SEC-001, SEC-004).
Rule 2: Non-Contesting Threshold
To optimize operational efficiency, disputes with a total value less than ₹500 / $10 / AED 30 where representment costs exceed potential recovery value may be auto-accepted by Finance Operations, unless part of a broader pattern of friendly fraud.
Rule 3: Single Recovery Rule
NovaCart will not pursue representment for a chargeback if internal logs confirm that a 100% refund was already executed for the transaction via PAY-003 prior to dispute filing. In such cases, Finance submits proof of the prior refund to the gateway for immediate dispute dismissal.
Exception Handling
* Card Brand Pre-Arbitration: If an issuing bank rejects initial representment evidence and files for Pre-Arbitration, Finance Operations conducts a cost-benefit review with Legal Counsel. If the transaction value exceeds $500 USD and evidence is conclusive (valid OTP + signed delivery), Legal authorizes filing for formal Card Brand Arbitration.
* Multiple Disputes Across Accounts: If Security identifies an organized "Friendly Fraud" syndicate generating multiple chargebacks across linked accounts, the case escalates to Level 3 Legal Review. Evidence is compiled for formal referral to law enforcement authorities and cybercrime divisions (Refer to SEC-005).
Practical Examples
Scenario 1: Representment Won for Friendly Fraud (Merchandise Not Received)
1. Customer purchases a laptop (₹65,000) delivered to Bangalore. Delivery rider verifies 4-digit OTP at doorstep (DELIVERED).
2. Two weeks later, customer files a chargeback with their issuing bank claiming "Merchandise Not Received" (Visa 13.1).
3. Gateway provisionally debits ₹65,000 from NovaCart. chargeback.novacart.internal flags dispute ingress.
4. Finance compiles evidence package: OMS order confirmation, 3DS authentication logs, TMS rider GPS map match, and time-stamped OTP verification log (Refer to SHIP-001).
5. Representment file submitted. Issuing bank reviews evidence, rules in favor of NovaCart (DISPUTE_WON), and returns ₹65,000 to NovaCart's bank account.
Scenario 2: Acceptance of Valid Dispute (Merchant Error)
1. Customer orders a TV (AED 2,500) in Dubai. Package is lost during middle-mile transit (Refer to SHIP-006).
2. Customer contacts bank and files a chargeback before Customer Support completes processing an internal refund.
3. Finance receives chargeback alert. Verification confirms parcel was declared lost by Logistics.
4. Finance auto-accepts the dispute (DISPUTE_ACCEPTED) to avoid unnecessary representment fees, updating ledger logs.
Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
Q1: Why was my NovaCart account suspended after I filed a chargeback with my bank?
Answer: When a bank chargeback is filed under a fraudulent transaction claim, our security systems automatically lock the account to prevent further unauthorized activity while the dispute is investigated. Once the dispute is resolved or a formal withdrawal letter is provided, account standing is evaluated (Refer to SEC-001).
Q2: What is "Friendly Fraud"?
Answer: Friendly fraud occurs when a legitimate customer or someone in their household makes an authorized purchase, receives the items, but subsequently files a chargeback with their bank claiming the transaction was unauthorized or unreceived. NovaCart aggressively defends against friendly fraud using digital Proof of Delivery (OTP) and device logs.
Q3: How long does a chargeback investigation take to resolve?
Answer: Banking chargeback rules allow card issuing banks 30 to 60 calendar days to evaluate representment evidence and issue a final ruling. You will receive email notifications when major milestones or final decisions occur (Refer to PAY-001).
