Purpose
This document defines the official enterprise policy, operational standards, automated recovery mechanisms, failure categorization frameworks, and customer communication protocols for managing failed and unconfirmed payment transactions at NovaCart Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "NovaCart"). It establishes clear rules for handling bank declines, gateway time-outs, network drops, unlinked debits, and pending payment status reconciliations.
As an essential operational document (PAY-002) in the NovaCart Enterprise Knowledge Base (NEKB), PAY-002 ensures deterministic execution across payment gateway integrations, financial ledgers (ledger.novacart.internal), Customer Support workflows, and the Order Management System (api.novacart.internal/oms), maintaining full compatibility with COMP-001 through COMP-004, SUP-001 through SUP-005, ORD-001 through ORD-005, SHIP-001 through SHIP-007, and PAY-001.
Scope
This policy applies to all failed, dropped, timed-out, or pending financial transactions across all operating regions (India, Singapore, UAE), supported payment methods (Credit/Debit Cards, UPI, Net Banking, Digital Wallets, BNPL, Gift Cards), commercial channels, and product categories.
Coverage Includes
* Categorization of payment failures based on origin root cause (User, Issuer Bank, Gateway, Network).
* Automated payment retry mechanisms and manual recovery link generation.
* Handling of "Unlinked Debits" (Customer debited, but order not generated in OMS).
* Handling of "Pending Payments" (Order created, but payment status unconfirmed).
* Multi-channel customer alerting, anti-fraud intervention, and duplicate payment prevention.
Payment Failure Overview
Payment failures represent a critical friction point in e-commerce fulfillment. NovaCart treats payment recovery with high operational priority to maximize checkout conversion while ensuring zero financial loss for the customer.
Core Operational Principles
1. Zero Customer Loss: Any payment debited from a customer's account without generating a confirmed order must be automatically recovered via order generation or fully refunded without requiring manual customer claims.
2. Deterministic Retry Logic: Programmatic, rule-based retry protocols that distinguish between hard declines (e.g., fraudulent card) and soft failures (e.g., temporary bank network drop).
3. 15-Minute Cart Holding Guarantee: Temporary reservation of inventory for 15 minutes following a soft payment failure to allow seamless customer payment re-attempts without cart loss.
4. Transparent Communication: Immediate multi-channel alerting informing customers of failure root causes, exact debit statuses, and secure re-payment options.

1. Immediate Gateway Response Ingress: The Payment Gateway (PG) returns an explicit failure payload (e.g., AUTHORIZATION_DECLINED, EXPIRED_CARD, INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS) via API response.
2. Webhook Timeout Listener: If no authorization payload or failure response is received within 180 seconds of checkout initiation, the payment state machine flags the transaction as PENDING_RECONCILIATION.
3. Automated Gateway Polling: For PENDING_RECONCILIATION transactions, background worker routines query the PG status endpoint (GET /v1/payments/{pg_id}) at 3-minute, 6-minute, and 12-minute intervals to obtain definitive transaction status.

	Manual Retry Process
When an automatic retry cannot resolve a payment failure, the customer is guided through a secure manual recovery workflow:
1. Failure Overlay Display: The checkout interface displays a clear, empathetic failure message explaining the specific root cause (e.g., "Your card was declined due to insufficient funds. Please try an alternate card or UPI.").
2. 15-Minute Cart Holding Guarantee: OMS holds the shopping cart items and allocated inventory in PENDING_PAYMENT status for 15 minutes.
3. Express Recovery Link: An automated SMS and WhatsApp message is sent containing a secure, single-use payment recovery link ([https://pay.novacart.com/recover/](https://pay.novacart.com/recover/){token}).
4. Instrument Switching: The customer clicks the recovery link, selects a different payment instrument (e.g., switching from Credit Card to UPI or Cash on Delivery), and completes payment.
5. Auto-Cancellation on Expiry: If no successful payment is completed within 15 minutes, OMS transitions the order status to CANCELLED_TIMEOUT and releases allocated inventory (Refer to ORD-001, ORD-002).
Pending Transaction Management
Pending transactions occur when funds are debited from a customer's bank account, but network drops prevent the gateway from notifying NovaCart in real time:
* Unlinked Debit Recovery (Order Auto-Creation): If background gateway polling confirms that funds were successfully debited within the 15-minute reservation window, ledger.novacart.internal automatically validates payment, creates the order in OMS, and updates order status to PAYMENT_CONFIRMED (Refer to ORD-001).
* Stock Depletion During Verification: If the gateway confirms payment after the 15-minute window expires and inventory has been purchased by another customer, OMS cannot generate the order. The system automatically triggers an Immediate Full Refund back to the customer's payment source and dispatches an apology notification with a ₹200 goodwill credit.

	Merchant & Payment Gateway Responsibilities
* Payment Gateway (PG) SLAs: Partner gateways must maintain an API authorization uptime of $\ge 99.95\%$ and process status polling queries within $< 500\text{ milliseconds}$.
* Merchant Non-Interference: Marketplace merchants are strictly prohibited from cancelling orders or altering prices while an order remains in PENDING_PAYMENT or PENDING_RECONCILIATION status (Refer to ORD-002).

	Business Rules
Rule 1: Maximum Manual Retry Attempts
A customer may attempt manual payment retries up to a maximum of five (5) attempts per order within the 15-minute window. If 5 consecutive payment attempts fail, the checkout session is locked for 1 hour to prevent carding fraud (Refer to SEC-004).
Rule 2: Absolute Prevention of Duplicate Debits
If a customer completes a successful payment retry using an alternate instrument, but the initial "failed" attempt is subsequently cleared by the bank, ledger.novacart.internal detects the duplicate debit within 2 hours and issues an Automatic 100% Refund for the secondary transaction back to the original payment source (Refer to PAY-003).
Rule 3: Single Recovery Link Policy
Payment recovery links ([https://pay.novacart.com/recover/](https://pay.novacart.com/recover/){token}) are single-use, cryptographically signed, and expire strictly after 15 minutes. Links automatically deactivate upon successful payment confirmation.
Exception Handling
* Bank Server Outages During Peak Events: During major shopping sales, if a major issuing bank (e.g., HDFC or State Bank of India) experiences a total core banking outage, api.novacart.internal/pay dynamically disables the affected bank option on checkout screens and highlights stable alternative payment methods (e.g., UPI or Digital Wallets).
* Unverified Pending Debits Exceeding 24 Hours: If a payment gateway maintains a transaction in PENDING status for $> 24\text{ hours}$ without definitive confirmation, NovaCart proactively issues a conditional store wallet credit to the customer, enabling order fulfillment while Finance pursues bank reconciliation.
Practical Examples
Scenario 1: Soft Failure & Success via Manual Recovery Link
1. Customer in Mumbai attempts credit card checkout for a smartphone (₹24,999). Issuing bank returns 3DS_TIMEOUT.
2. OMS holds items in PENDING_PAYMENT and sends an automated SMS/WhatsApp message containing a secure recovery link.
3. Customer clicks the link 4 minutes later, selects UPI payment (PAY-UPI), and enters valid UPI PIN.
4. Razorpay gateway returns AUTHORIZATION_SUCCESS. OMS updates status to PAYMENT_CONFIRMED and releases shipping manifest (Refer to ORD-001).
Scenario 2: Unlinked Debit Auto-Recovery
1. Customer in Dubai authorizes a credit card payment (AED 450) for a smart home hub. Network drops during 3DS redirection.
2. Customer sees a browser timeout screen and assumes payment failed. However, the bank debited AED 450.
3. Background polling worker queries Telr PG API 6 minutes later. Telr returns CAPTURED.
4. ledger.novacart.internal matches the PG transaction ID, creates the order in OMS, updates status to PAYMENT_CONFIRMED, and sends a WhatsApp confirmation alert to the customer.
Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
Q1: What should I do if my bank account was debited, but I received a "Payment Failed" notification?
Answer: Do not worry. Your money is completely safe. Our automated reconciliation system checks pending bank transactions every 3 minutes. If the payment was debited, your order will be automatically confirmed within 15 minutes. If stock is no longer available, a 100% full refund will be processed back to your bank account automatically (Refer to PAY-001, REF-001).
Q2: How long does NovaCart hold items in my shopping cart after a payment fails?
Answer: NovaCart reserves your selected items and holds active inventory for exactly 15 minutes after a soft payment failure, allowing you time to retry using an alternate payment method without losing your items (Refer to ORD-001).
Q3: Why was I prevented from retrying my payment after multiple failed attempts?
Answer: To protect your account from fraud and comply with security rules, our system locks checkout payment retries for 1 hour if 5 consecutive payment attempts fail on a single order. You can try again after 1 hour or contact Customer Support for assistance (Refer to SEC-004).
