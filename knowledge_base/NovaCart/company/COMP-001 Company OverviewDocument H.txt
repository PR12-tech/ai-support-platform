The average monetary amount spent by a customer per transaction on the platform.BNPL: Buy Now, Pay Later. A financial arrangement allowing installment-based customer purchases.COD: Cash on Delivery. A payment collection method where fulfillment triggers payment collection at the time of physical receipt.CSR: Customer Support Representative. An agent operating within the customer service hierarchy responsible for resolving user inquiries.ETA: Estimated Time of Arrival. The projected date and time window for shipment delivery or task completion.FC: Fulfillment Center. A centralized physical warehouse facility used for product storage, picking, packing, and dispatch.GMV: Gross Merchandise Value. The total monetary value of merchandise sold over a specified accounting timeframe.KBA: Knowledge Base Article. A modular, structured documentation unit indexed for operational reference and RAG retrieval systems.L1 Support: Level 1 Support. Front-line automated support interfaces and general customer service representatives handling basic queries.L2 Support: Level 2 Support. Specialized functional representatives handling complex, domain-specific escalations (e.g., payment investigations, shipping losses).L3 Support: Level 3 Support. Senior technical, operational, legal, or engineering leads handling systemic failures, security breaches, or high-value disputes.NDR: Non-Delivery Report. A formal system record generated when a courier attempts and fails to complete a shipment delivery.NCP: NovaCart Policy. The standardized naming convention and prefix for all corporate governance documents.NEKB: NovaCart Enterprise Knowledge Base. The centralized repository housing all formal corporate, technical, and operational documentation.POD: Proof of Delivery. Verification evidence (digital signature, OTP confirmation, or geo-tagged photograph) confirming physical delivery.RAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation. The technical framework used by AI agents to retrieve factual knowledge chunks from the NEKB to respond to queries.RMA: Return Merchandise Authorization. An official system-generated tracking code required before returning any physical product.SLA: Service Level Agreement. A defined, legally binding or operationally enforced metric governing response and resolution times.Master TerminologyMerchant of Record (MoR): The legal entity authorized and held liable for processing financial transactions and managing customer billing.NovaCart Platform: The proprietary e-commerce ecosystem consisting of customer interfaces (Web, Mobile Apps), enterprise resource planning (ERP) backends, order management systems (OMS), and merchant portals.Split Order: A single customer purchase order containing multiple items that are fulfilled from separate fulfillment centers or vendor locations, resulting in multiple distinct sub-orders and tracking IDs.Chargeback: A payment reversal enforced by a bank or payment gateway following a dispute raised by the account holder.Policy OverviewNovaCart Technologies Pvt. Ltd. operates as a high-velocity, customer-centric enterprise e-commerce platform. To maintain operational alignment across thousands of employees and technical automation platforms, NovaCart enforces a standardized documentation structure.All corporate policies must originate from approved master frameworks, maintain absolute internal consistency, avoid conflicting operational instructions, and strictly utilize the terminology established in COMP-001 and COMP-004.Detailed Policy1. Corporate Identity and HistoryFounded in 2018 in Bangalore, India, NovaCart Technologies Pvt. Ltd. has grown from a regional electronics vendor into an international multi-category enterprise marketplace. The company provides scalable digital commerce infrastructure, consumer electronics retail, enterprise hardware provisioning, software licensing, and subscription management across South Asia and the Middle East.2. Strategic Mission, Vision, and Core ValuesMission StatementTo deliver a secure, frictionless, and transparent e-commerce ecosystem that empowers retail consumers and enterprise clients through high-velocity fulfillment, deterministic operational policies, and exceptional customer support.Vision StatementTo establish the benchmark for digital marketplace reliability across emerging global markets by combining intelligent logistics, absolute policy clarity, and human-centered technology.Core ValuesCustomer Obsession: Decisions prioritize long-term customer trust over short-term operational convenience.Operational Precision: Strict adherence to standardized procedures ensures repeatable quality and eliminates ambiguity.Data Integrity and Security: Customer and enterprise data are protected with zero-trust architecture and strict privacy compliance.Algorithmic Transparency: Automated systems operate under explicit, predictable business rules governed by published policies.Continuous Improvement: Policies and workflows undergo systematic operational reviews to adapt to market demands and customer feedback.3. Business Model and Commercial ArchitectureNovaCart operates a hybrid e-commerce architecture combining direct inventory fulfillment (1P model) with a curated multi-vendor marketplace (3P model). The platform serves diverse buyer categories under strict commercial guidelines:Customer SegmentsRetail Customers (B2C): Individual consumers purchasing personal electronics, home appliances, and lifestyle technology.Business Customers (B2B - SMB): Small-to-medium businesses utilizing business accounts for tax-compliant invoicing (e.g., GSTIN in India, VAT in UAE) and procurement.Corporate Clients (Enterprise B2B): Large enterprise accounts operating under contractual credit terms, customized procurement catalogs, and dedicated account management.Premium Members (NovaCart Prime): Subscription-based accounts receiving benefits such as free express shipping, priority customer support routing, and extended return windows.4. Strategic Operational PhilosophiesCustomer-First PrinciplesFirst-Contact Resolution: Customer service teams must aim to resolve inquiries on the initial interaction whenever possible.Absolute Transparency: Operational terms, shipping deadlines, and refund eligibility rules must be clearly displayed prior to order confirmation.Fair Customer Presumption: In shipment disputes lacking conclusive carrier Proof of Delivery (POD), customer claims receive immediate operational priority pending internal carrier audits (Refer to SHIP-006).Technology PhilosophyDeterministic Workflows: System functions, automated messaging, and back-end updates must run on validated, rule-based logic to ensure consistency.Knowledge-Driven RAG Alignment: Automated agents and human representatives draw operational guidance from the same indexed Knowledge Base Articles (KBAs).Data Minimization: Admin and customer interfaces display only the data fields required for the active operational task.Security PhilosophyZero Trust Architecture: Every system connection, administrative function, and data access attempt requires explicit authentication, authorization, and logging.Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Access privileges match employee job responsibilities; unmasked payment details and sensitive customer data are restricted across all support tiers (Refer to SEC-001).Operational WorkflowThe following sequential workflows govern platform administration, documentation management, and core customer lifecycle execution across NovaCart operations.Workflow 1: active, published policies within the NEKB. Unofficial documentation or verbal instructions are strictly invalid for operational enforcement.Rule 2: Non-Discretionary Financial RefundsRefunds must be processed exclusively back to the original payment instrument used during checkout. Direct cash refunds are strictly prohibited across all operating territories. Cash on Delivery (COD) refunds must be issued via verified electronic bank transfers (NEFT/RTGS/IMPS) or platform wallet balances (Refer to REF-001, PAY-001).Rule 3: Split Order IsolationSub-orders generated from a split order transaction operate as independent entity records. The cancellation, delay, return, or refund of one sub-order must not automatically disrupt, invalidate, or alter the processing schedules of remaining sub-orders within the master transaction (Refer to ORD-005).Rule 4: Mandatory Serial VerificationAll physical items within CAT-ELE (Consumer Electronics) and CAT-MOB (Mobile & Accessories) must have their unique serial numbers recorded by warehouse staff prior to shipment packaging. Return authorizations (RMAs) require serial verification against out-bound warehouse records before refund approval (Refer to REF-005, PROD-001).Decision TablesThe following decision matrices instrument within 24 hours without requesting additional documentation from the customer.Compliance NotesNovaCart operations must continuously adhere to regional legal requirements across all jurisdictions:Data Privacy: Personal Identifiable Information (PII) handling must comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP) in India, the Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) in Singapore, and the Federal Decree-Law on Personal Data Protection in the UAE. Support interfaces must mask sensitive fields by default (Refer to SEC-001).Payment Security: Platform payment infrastructure must maintain annual PCI-DSS (Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard) Level 1 certification. Direct storage of raw Primary Account Numbers (PAN) or CVV codes on NovaCart servers is strictly prohibited (Refer to PAY-001).Tax and E-Invoicing Compliance: Orders generated in India must issue GST-compliant electronic tax invoices displaying the merchant GSTIN. Orders in the UAE must feature explicit VAT tax breakdowns in accordance with Federal Tax Authority standards.Security ConsiderationsAccess control to operational backend systems must enforce Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) and follow least-privilege RBAC standards (Refer to SEC-001, SEC-003).Customer interaction logs, agent notes, and support session transcripts must be encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3).Any identified or suspected account compromise, unauthorized administrative access, or systemic data leak must be reported to the Security Operations Center (SOC) within 15 minutes of detection (Refer to SEC-005).ExamplesScenario A: Enterprise B2B Procurement and Split FulfillmentAn enterprise account client in Singapore purchases 50 business laptops (CAT-ELE) and 50 digital productivity software licenses (CAT-SFT) for immediate staff deployment.Operational Breakdown:The platform generates Master Order ID NC-SG-88291.Tax invoice automation registers the corporate tax account ID and applies contractual B2B corporate pricing.The OMS executes a split order operation:Sub-Order NC-SG-88291-A encompasses the 50 digital software licenses, which are delivered instantly to the corporate administrator's account dashboard along with digital activation keys (Refer to CAT-SFT rules in REF-006).Sub-Order NC-SG-88291-B encompasses the 50 physical laptops, routed for pallet packing and dispatch at fulfillment hub SIN-01 (Refer to SHIP-001).Laptop serial numbers are scanned, logged, and linked to Sub-Order NC-SG-88291-B prior to courier handover.Proof of Delivery (POD) digital signatures are collected upon physical receipt at the client's corporate office, concluding the physical fulfillment lifecycle.Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)Q1: What is the official role of COMP-001 within the NovaCart documentation system?Answer: COMP-001 is the master company overview and governance framework policy. It establishes corporate identity, operational standards, core terminology, and baseline rules that govern all other domain-specific policies across the organization.Q2: How does NovaCart manage customer support escalations across different regions?Answer: Escalations move systematically from automated self-service / L1 CSRs to regional L2 domain specialists (e.g., Finance, Shipping), and finally to L3 managerial or legal teams based on defined priority matrices and financial authorization thresholds outlined in SUP-003 and SUP-004.Q3: What payment methods are supported for customers operating in the UAE?Answer: Supported payment options in the UAE include Credit/Debit Cards, Tabby (Buy Now, Pay Later), Careem Pay, and Cash on Delivery (COD), managed under payment security protocols detailed in PAY-001 and PAY-005.Q4: Are digital software licenses refundable if the customer changes their mind?Answer: No. Under CAT-SFT policies detailed in COMP-001 and REF-006, digital software activation keys and SaaS subscriptions are non-refundable once the digital key has been revealed or accessed by the customer.Q5: How are split orders tracked by customers and support personnel?Answer: Master orders containing items originating from different fulfillment hubs or product verticals are automatically separated into sub-order IDs. Each sub-order maintains independent shipping tracking numbers, ETAs, and fulfillment logs accessible via customer dashboards or support agent tooling (Refer to ORD-005).Revision HistoryVersionPublication DateAuthor / Primary ContributorSummary of Administrative Changes1.0.0January 1, 2026Enterprise Knowledge Architecture TeamInitial release of the master NovaCart Company Overview & Governance Policy framework.
