Purpose
This document serves as the official enterprise department directory and operational capabilities registry for NovaCart Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "NovaCart"). It establishes an exhaustive reference model for every operational division within the organization, defining their overarching purpose, core responsibilities, operational scope, software systems owned, policy documentation owned, internal customer networks, external vendor/regulatory interfaces, key performance indicators (KPIs), and escalation parameters.
As a primary core document (COMP-003) in the NovaCart Enterprise Knowledge Base (NEKB), this directory works in direct alignment with COMP-001 (Company Overview) and COMP-002 (Organization Structure) to eliminate departmental friction, standardize cross-functional communication, clarify operational ownership boundaries, and optimize vector retrieval engines utilized by automated AI support and knowledge platforms.
Scope
This directory applies globally across all operating territories, corporate offices, fulfillment facilities, customer engagement hubs, and technical deployment zones of NovaCart Technologies Pvt. Ltd.
Coverage Includes
* All fourteen (14) official enterprise departments operating across global hubs in India (Bangalore, Mumbai, Delhi, Kolkata), Singapore (Marina Bay), and the United Arab Emirates (Dubai, Abu Dhabi).
* Full mapping of primary department mandates, operational boundaries, owned technological systems, and governed documentation.
* Enterprise-wide responsibility assignment frameworks, system ownership matrices, and escalation matrices.
* Standardized inter-departmental communication protocols and operational collaboration workflows.
Definitions
To ensure standardized interpretation across human personnel and automated Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) agent systems, the following terms and abbreviations apply throughout this document:
Acronyms & Abbreviations
* API: Application Programming Interface. Software intermediary allowing systems to communicate.
* B2B: Business-to-Business. Commercial transactions conducted between NovaCart and corporate/business clients.
* B2C: Business-to-Consumer. Direct retail commercial transactions with individual consumers.
* BAU: Business As Usual. Standard routine operational activities.
* CISO: Chief Information Security Officer. Executive lead for corporate cybersecurity and platform risk.
* CRM: Customer Relationship Management. Software platform tracking customer interactions and support records.
* ERP: Enterprise Resource Planning. Integrated software suite managing core financial, inventory, and human resource processes.
* FC: Fulfillment Center. Physical logistics facility handling inventory receipt, packaging, and dispatch.
* KPI: Key Performance Indicator. Quantifiable metric measuring performance against operational goals.
* NEKB: NovaCart Enterprise Knowledge Base. The central repository of structured enterprise knowledge.
* OMS: Order Management System. Core platform module governing order validation, routing, and state transitions.
* RACI: Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed. Framework clarifying cross-functional roles.
* RMA: Return Merchandise Authorization. Tracking authorization code required for product returns.
* SLA: Service Level Agreement. Enforceable operational timeline commitment for task execution.
* SOC: Security Operations Center. Centralized team monitoring and securing enterprise infrastructure.
* TMS: Transportation Management System. Logistics software optimizing shipment routing and fleet dispatch.
* WMS: Warehouse Management System. Operational software managing inventory storage, picking, and packing.
Department Classification Standards
Enterprise departments within NovaCart are classified into three primary operational tiers based on their primary touchpoints, technical access levels, and organizational functions:
1. Direct Operations & Customer-Facing Tiers
* Departments: Customer Support, Sales, Returns & Refunds.
* Characteristics: Direct interaction with retail/business customers and active merchant accounts; high-volume real-time transactional interfaces; strict adherence to customer-facing SLAs.
2. Supply Chain & Physical Execution Tiers
* Departments: Operations, Logistics, Warehouse Management.
* Characteristics: Physical asset management, inventory movement, middle-mile/last-mile routing, hub facility management, and fulfillment safety protocols.
3. Strategy, Platform & Governance Tiers
* Departments: Executive Office, Engineering, Product Management, Finance, Information Security, Human Resources, Legal & Compliance, Enterprise Knowledge Management.
* Characteristics: Enterprise strategy, software development, financial reconciliation, regulatory adherence, risk mitigation, talent acquisition, and information taxonomy management.

Primary Responsibilities

	Communication Guidelines
To maintain operational velocity and prevent information fragmentation, all internal departmental communications must follow structured channels:
1. Urgent Operational Incidents (P1/P2): Communicated via centralized incident management channels on the enterprise chat platform and automated SMS callouts to on-call leads.
2. Standard Inter-Department Tasks (P3/P4): Ticketed and tracked exclusively through official enterprise workflow portals (jira.novacart.internal or crm.novacart.internal). Direct un-ticketed messaging for operational requests is prohibited.
3. Policy Modifications & KBA Announcements: Published exclusively through the Enterprise Knowledge Base (knowledge.novacart.internal) with automated notification blasts sent to impacted department managers.
Business Rules
Rule 1: System Administrative Custody
Each software application listed in the System Ownership Matrix must have exactly one primary Owning Department responsible for user privilege approvals, and one Technical Maintainer squad within Engineering responsible for code maintenance and uptime.
Rule 2: Non-Interference in Security Locks
Decisions made by Information Security to suspend an account, halt a transaction, or lock down a facility module due to active fraud or security threats take precedence over commercial considerations. Locks may only be lifted by the CISO or designated Fraud Operations Leads (Refer to SEC-004, SEC-005).
Rule 3: Mandatory Cross-Departmental Ticket Trails
No department may execute a financial refund, inventory write-off, address change, or shipment re-routing without creating a traceable, system-logged ticket containing the unique Order ID, employee ID, and business justification (Refer to SUP-001, PAY-001, SHIP-007).
Operational Examples
The following practical workflows illustrate cross-departmental collaboration in standard enterprise scenarios.
Example 1: Customer Refund Involving Support, Returns, and Finance
1. Initiation (Customer Support): The customer contacts live chat stating a received smartphone (CAT-MOB) is defective. The CSR initiates an RMA request in crm.novacart.internal adhering to SUP-001 and REF-005.
2. Reverse Pickup (Logistics): The TMS dispatches a reverse courier agent to collect the smartphone from the customer and transport it to hub BLR-01.
3. Physical Inspection (Returns & Refunds): Returns specialists unbox the item, scan the barcode into inspect.novacart.internal, and verify that the hardware serial number matches dispatch records. The inspection passes, and the return is marked "Approved."
4. Financial Settlement (Finance): The approved RMA automatically triggers a refund instruction to ledger.novacart.internal. Finance executes an electronic credit card reversal, updating the transaction status to "Refunded" and notifying the customer via email (Refer to REF-002).
Example 2: Lost Shipment Investigation Involving Logistics and Warehouse
1. Trigger (Logistics): An outbound package containing a laptop (CAT-ELE) shipped to Dubai misses its ETA window by 72 hours. The TMS automatically flags the tracking ID as "In-Transit Delay" (Refer to SHIP-005).
2. Warehouse Audit (Warehouse Management): Logistics contacts hub DXB-01 to verify if the parcel was loaded onto the line-haul trailer. Warehouse staff review video manifest logs and confirm the package left the loading dock.
3. Loss Declaration (Logistics): After a 48-hour carrier trace fails to locate the parcel, Logistics marks the tracking ID as "Lost in Transit" in tms.novacart.internal (Refer to SHIP-006).
4. Replacement & Claim (Customer Support & Finance): Customer Support is alerted to dispatch an expedited replacement order to the customer. Concurrently, Logistics submits a formal insurance loss claim to the 3PL carrier.
Example 3: Fraud Investigation Involving Security and Finance
1. Alert (Information Security): The Anti-Fraud Shield (shield.novacart.internal) flags three rapid high-value transactions originating from a single IP address using different international credit cards.
2. Account Containment (Security): The SOC analyst freezes the user account and places an immediate shipping hold on the orders in the OMS (Refer to SEC-004).
3. Gateway Verification (Finance): Security alerts Finance to check payment gateway authorization logs. Finance confirms two of the three cards were flagged for chargebacks by the issuing bank.
4. Resolution (Security & Finance): Security cancels the fraudulent orders, preventing inventory dispatch. Finance initiates pre-emptive transaction reversals to avoid bank chargeback processing fees.
Example 4: Warranty Claim Involving Product Management and Support
1. Claim Submission (Customer Support): A customer reports a smart home appliance hub (CAT-SMT) failed 4 months post-purchase. The CSR registers a warranty validation request in crm.novacart.internal (Refer to PROD-001).
2. Validation (Product Management): Product Management's warranty rules engine verifies that the product is within the 1-year coverage window and that the issue code matches covered hardware failures.
3. RMA Authorization (Support & Returns): The customer receives a prepaid shipping label to send the defective unit to an authorized repair center for replacement under PROD-002.
Example 5: Knowledge Base Update Involving Knowledge Management and Engineering
1. Policy Update (Engineering & Payments): Engineering releases a new payment feature supporting regional Buy-Now-Pay-Later (Tabby in UAE).
2. Drafting (Enterprise Knowledge Management): Knowledge Architects collaborate with Finance and Engineering to draft updated documentation for PAY-001 and KB-002.
3. RAG Indexing (Enterprise Knowledge Management): Upon approval, Knowledge Management publishes the updated documents to knowledge.novacart.internal, triggering automated vector re-indexing (rag-index.novacart.internal) to ensure AI support bots answer customer queries accurately.
Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
Q1: How do I identify which department owns a specific software system or portal?
Answer: Refer to the System Ownership Matrix in COMP-003. Each software tool domain is explicitly mapped to its Owning Department (for business privileges) and Technical Maintainer squad in Engineering (for system bugs).
Q2: What is the process for updating a department's operational scope or primary responsibilities?
Answer: Any modification to departmental scope must be submitted as a formal revision request to Enterprise Knowledge Management. Changes require approval from the Department Owner and Chief Operating Officer before COMP-003 is updated and re-indexed.
Q3: Which department handles customer disputes regarding damaged goods received in transit?
Answer: Customer Support manages initial customer contact (SUP-001), Returns & Refunds inspects returned items (REF-003), and Logistics handles carrier damage investigations and insurance claims (SHIP-006).
Q4: How are cross-departmental SLA disagreements or operational impasses resolved?
Answer: Operational disagreements follow the Escalation Ownership Matrix in COMP-003. If L2 domain managers cannot reach resolution within 24 hours, the issue escalates to L3 Department Heads or the Chief Operating Officer for a binding decision.
