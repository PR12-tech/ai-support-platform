Purpose
This document defines the official Product & Service Knowledge Management Policy of NovaCart Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "NovaCart"). It establishes standardized enterprise procedures, governance principles, asset ownership responsibilities, product documentation standards, validation workflows, product launch documentation protocols, quality controls, and business rules governing the creation, validation, publication, maintenance, and retirement of knowledge assets related to NovaCart's physical merchandise, digital offerings, and commercial service programs.
As a core operational document (KNOW-002) in the Knowledge Management category of the NovaCart Enterprise Knowledge Base (NEKB), KNOW-002 governs product and service knowledge lifecycle management within know.novacart.internal and catalog.novacart.internal, operating in strict operational alignment with COMP-001 through COMP-004, SUP-001 through SUP-005, ORD-001 through ORD-005, SHIP-001 through SHIP-007, PAY-001 through PAY-006, REF-001 through REF-006, PROD-001 through PROD-006, SEC-001 through SEC-006, LEG-001 through LEG-006, and KNOW-001.
Scope
This policy applies universally across all technical specifications, user manuals, installation guides, warranty terms, digital product licensing rules, troubleshooting trees, service tier descriptions, and merchant product documentation generated for or hosted on NovaCart platforms across India, Singapore, and the United Arab Emirates.
Coverage Includes
* Classification and structural standards for physical product, digital software (CAT-SFT), subscription (CAT-SUB), and service knowledge assets.
* Ownership assignment across Merchandising, Product Management, Quality Assurance, and Knowledge Management teams.
* Product launch knowledge preparation timelines, pre-launch validation, and synchronized Go-Live workflows.
* Change management protocols for price adjustments, feature upgrades, specification updates, and warranty modifications.
* Drift detection, conflict resolution, and emergency documentation correction procedures.
* Product retirement, SKU archive structures, historical document retrieval, and compliance log retention.
Out of Scope
* Master catalog SKU attribute ingestion, taxonomy hierarchy creation, and merchant listing uploads (governed by PROD-001).
* Customer support ticket routing, macro template management, and agent CRM interface navigation (governed by KNOW-001 and SUP-001).
Product & Service Knowledge Overview
In an omnichannel e-commerce environment, accurate, comprehensive, and up-to-date product and service knowledge is vital to commercial success and operational efficiency. Frontline Customer Support Representatives (CSRs) in crm.novacart.internal (SUP-001), automated chatbot engines, field installation technicians (PROD-004), and self-service customers relying on public storefront listings (PROD-001) require a single, synchronized source of truth.
Discrepancies in product dimensions, compatibility parameters, warranty terms (PROD-003), or digital activation workflows (PROD-006) cause customer friction, higher return rates (REF-002), order disputes (LEG-006), and compliance failures (LEG-005).
Core Knowledge Management Principles
1. Single Source of Product Truth: All customer-facing product specifications, internal troubleshooting guides, and service SLAs must synchronize directly from validated NEKB records in know.novacart.internal. Disconnected spreadsheets or local documentation hubs are strictly prohibited.
2. Synchronized Commercial Readiness: No new physical product, digital SKU, or service program may be enabled for customer purchase (STAGE-ACT in PROD-001) until all associated technical and operational knowledge assets are validated and published.
3. Continuous Drift Control: Product and service knowledge assets are audited continuously against manufacturer specification updates, customer return reason trends (REF-004), and warranty claim logs (PROD-003).
4. Immutable Version Traceability: Every modification to product specifications, compatibility matrices, or warranty terms must maintain full semantic version history and audit log logging in audit.novacart.internal (SEC-006).
Knowledge Management Principles
NovaCart's product and service information architecture adheres to four foundational operational mandates:
* Accuracy & Precision: Technical parameters (e.g., operating voltage, dimensions, weight capacity, OS compatibility) must be verified against original manufacturer spec sheets or formal QA lab reports prior to publication.
* Completeness Across the Lifecycle: Knowledge documentation must cover the full product journey: pre-purchase specifications, unboxing guidelines, installation requirements (PROD-004), operational troubleshooting, warranty terms (PROD-003), and end-of-life disposal.
* Role-Gated Accessibility: While customer-facing specifications are published openly on Product Display Pages (PDP), sensitive internal troubleshooting procedures, supplier cost structures, and technical escalation contacts are restricted to authorized internal agent views (KNOW-INT under KNOW-001).
* Proactive Change Management: When a product or service undergoes revision (e.g., firmware update, packaging change, accessory bundle alteration), corresponding knowledge assets must be updated simultaneously across all channels.

	Knowledge Creation Process
Knowledge asset creation follows a structured authoring standard in know.novacart.internal:
1. Initiation & Template Selection: The author selects the appropriate NEKB product knowledge template matching the product vertical (CAT-ELE, CAT-MOB, CAT-APP, CAT-SFT, or CAT-SUB).
2. Metadata Binding: The draft is bound to the specific Master SKU ID, Category Code, Brand Name, and Model Number registered in catalog.novacart.internal (PROD-001).
3. Mandatory Content Sections:
   * Technical Overview: Concise summary of core features and intended usage.
   * Verified Specification Table: Structural grid of technical parameters.
   * Compatibility & Requirements: Explicit list of supported hardware/software/environment requirements.
   * Installation & Setup Overview: Step-by-step unboxing and setup instructions (PROD-004).
   * Warranty & Return Summary: Cross-references to REF-001, REF-003, and PROD-003.
   * Internal Troubleshooting Tree: Gated diagnostic steps for customer support agents.
4. NEKB Policy Cross-Referencing: Every product knowledge document must link explicitly to governing policies (e.g., "Returns handled per REF-001 Grade A guidelines" or "Installation governed by PROD-004 Site Readiness rules").

	Handling Conflicting or Inaccurate Information
1. Emergency Drift Flagging: If a customer, support agent, or warehouse inspector discovers conflicting product information (e.g., PDP states "2-year warranty" while package states "1-year warranty"), the discovering party logs an EMERGENCY_KNOW_DRIFT ticket in know.novacart.internal.
2. Temporary Deprecation Hold: The Knowledge Manager places the affected knowledge section on temporary deprecation hold (STAGE-SPD under KNOW-001) within 1 hour.
3. Corrective Investigation: The Category Manager contacts the OEM or verifies physical inventory in FC storage within 4 business hours.
4. Synchronized Update: Corrected data is published simultaneously across know.novacart.internal and catalog.novacart.internal.

	Business Rules
Rule 1: Mandatory Knowledge Gate for Commercial Launch
No Master SKU may transition to ST-IN_STOCK or ST-PREORDER in catalog.novacart.internal without an approved, published product knowledge document (STAGE-ACT) linked in know.novacart.internal. The catalog management software automatically blocks checkout enablement if the knowledge link is missing or in STAGE-DFT status.
Rule 2: Absolute Specification Priority Rule
In the event of a discrepancy between marketing promotional banners and the verified technical specification table in know.novacart.internal, the verified technical specification table takes absolute operational precedence. Support agents and dispute officers must resolve customer claims based strictly on the verified specification table (LEG-006).
Rule 3: Mandatory Sync upon Specification or Warranty Revisions
Any modification to physical inbox contents, technical compatibility, or warranty duration must be updated simultaneously in know.novacart.internal and catalog.novacart.internal within 2 hours of formal approval. Asynchronous or partial documentation updates are strictly prohibited.
Rule 4: Mandatory 7-Year Retention for Product Knowledge Archives
All product knowledge assets, historical version revisions, QA unboxing logs, and specification approval sign-offs must be retained in immutable WORM storage for a mandatory period of 7 years post-product discontinuation (SEC-003, SEC-006).
Exception Handling
* Emergency Pre-Launch Documentation Bypass: In rare cases where a strategic manufacturer embargo prevents full technical spec release prior to launch day (e.g., major smartphone launch CAT-MOB), the Director of Product Management may authorize an "Embargoed Pre-Launch Knowledge Draft." The core specifications are validated under strict NDA, held in encrypted staging, and published automatically at the exact second the global embargo lifts.
* Supplier Discontinued Part Substitution: If an OEM substitutes a non-critical internal component (e.g., screen panel supplier change) without changing the Master SKU or model number, QA must execute an immediate delta specification audit. The product knowledge asset is updated to Version X.Y+1, logging the serial number range where the component change applies.
Practical Examples
Scenario 1: Knowledge Preparation for a New Smart Home Product Launch (CAT-SMT)
1. Merchandising creates a new Master SKU for a Smart Security Lock in catalog.novacart.internal set for launch in 14 days ($T-14$).
2. Category Manager drafts initial specification and setup documentation in know.novacart.internal.
3. At $T-7$ days, QA receives physical samples, conducts unboxing verification, tests Wi-Fi pairing, and validates door thickness requirements.
4. QA updates setup troubleshooting steps and approves the technical draft at $T-3$ days.
5. Knowledge Manager publishes the complete knowledge asset (STAGE-ACT) at $T-24$ hours. System unlocks the SKU for commercial checkout on launch day.
Scenario 2: Emergency Correction of Inaccurate Compatibility Information
1. A customer contacts support claiming a purchased monitor (CAT-ELE) does not support 120Hz display refresh rates over HDMI as claimed on the PDP, logging a dispute (LEG-006).
2. CSR flags an EMERGENCY_KNOW_DRIFT ticket in know.novacart.internal.
3. Executing Rule 3, Knowledge Manager places the HDMI specification section on temporary deprecation hold (STAGE-SPD) within 30 minutes.
4. QA lab re-tests the monitor and confirms 120Hz is supported exclusively over DisplayPort connections, while HDMI supports max 60Hz.
5. Documentation is corrected to Version 1.1.0 across know.novacart.internal and catalog.novacart.internal within 2 hours. Customer is provided a free DisplayPort adapter or eligible return under REF-001 rules.
Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
Q1: What should I do if I find conflicting product information between the storefront PDP and internal support guides?
Answer: If you discover mismatched information, log an EMERGENCY_KNOW_DRIFT ticket immediately in know.novacart.internal or flag the article in crm.novacart.internal. The Knowledge Management team will place the affected section on a temporary deprecation hold (STAGE-SPD), verify the correct specifications with QA or the manufacturer within 4 hours, and push a synchronized update across all channels (Refer to SUP-001).
Q2: Why can't a new product be enabled for sale before its knowledge documentation is complete?
Answer: In accordance with Rule 1, system software blocks SKU checkout enablement until an approved, validated knowledge document is linked in know.novacart.internal. This ensures that customer support agents, chatbot engines, and installation technicians have verified troubleshooting and setup information available the moment the first order is placed (Refer to PROD-001).
Q3: How long is product documentation kept after an item is discontinued?
Answer: When a product is discontinued (ST-DISCONTINUED), its public PDP displays a read-only "Discontinued" notice, but its internal technical documentation remains active for customer support and warranty servicing (PROD-003). After full end-of-life retirement (ST-ARCHIVED), the complete knowledge record is stored securely in read-only WORM archives for 7 years to support historical disputes, legal inquiries, and regulatory audits (Refer to SEC-003, SEC-006).
