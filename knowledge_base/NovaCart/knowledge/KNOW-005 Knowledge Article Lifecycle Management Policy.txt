Purpose
This document defines the official Knowledge Article Lifecycle Management Policy of NovaCart Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "NovaCart"). It establishes standardized operational procedures, governance principles, asset ownership responsibilities, quality controls, version control standards, review workflows, approval matrices, and business rules governing the complete lifecycle of knowledge articles from initial creation through review, approval, publication, maintenance, revision, archival, and retirement across NovaCart's enterprise ecosystem.
As a core operational document (KNOW-005) in the Knowledge Management category of the NovaCart Enterprise Knowledge Base (NEKB), KNOW-005 unifies the article lifecycle controls across know.novacart.internal, faq.novacart.internal, and sop.novacart.internal, operating in strict operational alignment with COMP-001 through COMP-004, SUP-001 through SUP-005, ORD-001 through ORD-005, SHIP-001 through SHIP-007, PAY-001 through PAY-006, REF-001 through REF-006, PROD-001 through PROD-006, SEC-001 through SEC-006, LEG-001 through LEG-006, and KNOW-001 through KNOW-004.
Scope
This policy applies universally across all customer support articles, FAQ entries, operational Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), product documentation notes, and internal reference guides generated, hosted, or utilized by NovaCart across India, Singapore, and the United Arab Emirates.
Coverage Includes
* Complete end-to-end lifecycle stages for knowledge articles: Ideation, Drafting, Review, Approval, Publication, Maintenance, Archival, and Retirement.
* Classification framework defining internal agent guides versus public customer-facing content.
* Explicit roles, permissions, and accountability frameworks for article authors, cross-functional reviewers, and approvers.
* Semantic version numbering standards distinguishing minor administrative updates from major structural revisions.
* Scheduled review frequencies, drift detection, temporary withdrawal protocols, and permanent archival criteria.
* Quality assurance checklists, audit sampling methodologies, and feedback loop integration for continuous improvement.
Out of Scope
* Technical database architecture, vector search indexing configuration, and knowledge management platform software engineering.
* Broad corporate communications, public relations announcements, and external press collateral.
Knowledge Article Lifecycle Management Overview
A structured lifecycle management framework ensures that knowledge articles remain accurate, reliable, compliant, and accessible throughout their operational existence. Without disciplined lifecycle governance, knowledge repositories suffer from information drift, contradictory guidance, and obsolete instructions that compromise customer support execution (SUP-001) and regulatory compliance (LEG-005).

Core Lifecycle Management Principles
1. End-to-End Accountability: Every knowledge article is managed under a strict chain of custody, linking a designated Author, Department Reviewer, and Executive Approver to its operational state.
2. Immutable Version Control: All modifications, corrections, and structural rewrites are captured through semantic versioning and logged permanently in audit.novacart.internal (SEC-006).
3. Proactive Maintenance & Drift Control: Articles are subjected to mandatory scheduled review intervals and automated feedback triggers to catch and correct inaccuracies before they impact operations.
4. Controlled Archival & Retention: Obsolete documentation is systematically retired from active search and transferred to secure WORM storage, satisfying 7-year audit retention requirements (SEC-003, SEC-006).

	Knowledge Article Classification
Articles are categorized into two primary visibility tiers:
1. Internal Agent Articles (KNOW-INT)
Restricted to authenticated NovaCart staff and tier-assigned support representatives (crm.novacart.internal). Contains detailed operational SOPs (KNOW-003), step-up verification protocols (SEC-001), override rules (REF-005), and internal troubleshooting trees.
2. Public Customer Articles (KNOW-PUB)
Publicly accessible self-service articles written in accessible, plain language. Contains shipping guides (SHIP-001), return eligibility rules (REF-001), product setup manuals (PROD-004), and FAQ entries (KNOW-004).
Article Creation Process
The creation of a new knowledge article follows a structured 4-step workflow:
1. Identification & Scoping: A need for a new article is identified through support ticket volume trends, product launches (PROD-001), or master policy updates (LEG-005).
2. Template Selection: The author selects the appropriate NEKB template matching the asset type (Support SOP, Product Guide, FAQ, or Operational SOP).
3. Metadata Binding: The draft is bound to a unique Document ID (NEKB-XXX-000), Category Code, Department Owner, and Target Audience.
4. Draft Submission: The author completes the draft, incorporating mandatory NEKB cross-references, and submits the file to STAGE-REV.

	Publication & Distribution
Upon receiving final approval, the system executes automated publication workflows:
1. Visibility Assignment: The article is assigned either internal (KNOW-INT) or public (KNOW-PUB) visibility permissions based on its classification.
2. Search Indexing: Content indexes immediately across agent desktop search bars (crm.novacart.internal) and public storefront help center search engines.
3. Audit Log Recording: Publication metadata, author signatures, and approval sign-offs commit permanently to audit.novacart.internal (SEC-006).
Version Control & Change Management
To maintain consistency and traceability across operational teams, articles maintain strict semantic versioning:
Version Numbering Standards
* Major Revision (vX.0.0): Advanced when a structural rewrite, complete workflow redesign, or major master policy replacement occurs (e.g., updating an article from v1.2.0 to v2.0.0). Major revisions require full cross-functional review and executive re-approval.
* Minor Revision (v1.X.0): Advanced when adding new troubleshooting steps, expanding FAQ categories, or updating operational tips that do not alter core procedural intent. Minor revisions require SME review.
* Patch Revision (v1.0.X): Advanced upon minor administrative corrections, typographical fixes, or dead link updates. Patch revisions may be published directly by authors following Knowledge Manager notification.

	Retirement Criteria & Restoration
* Retirement Criteria: An article is permanently archived (STAGE-ARC) when the underlying product is discontinued (PROD-001), the governing policy is retired, or duplicate articles are consolidated.
* Restoration Workflow: Archived articles may be restored to active status (STAGE-ARC) by submitting a formal revision request, updating technical references, and re-completing the full review and approval pipeline.

	Business Rules
Rule 1: Mandatory Policy Alignment Rule
No knowledge article may contradict, expand, or restrict the provisions of a published NEKB master policy. In the event of a conflict between an article and a master policy document (e.g., KNOW-005 vs. REF-001), the master policy document takes absolute precedence.
Rule 2: Single Active Article Rule for Operational Topics
The knowledge repository must maintain exactly one (1) active master article per distinct operational topic. Creating duplicate, overlapping, or conflicting article variants for the same core process is strictly prohibited.
Rule 3: Immediate Deprecation upon Policy Update
When a foundational enterprise policy is revised (e.g., an update to REF-001 or PAY-003), the Knowledge Manager must place all associated knowledge articles on temporary deprecation hold (STAGE-SPD) within 2 hours of policy publication until updated drafts are approved and published.
Rule 4: Mandatory 7-Year Archive Retention
All retired articles (STAGE-ARC), semantic version histories, review sign-off logs, and QA evaluation records must be retained in immutable WORM storage for a mandatory period of 7 years (SEC-003, SEC-006).
Exception Handling
* Emergency Publication Protocol: During critical platform outages, active security incidents (SEC-005), or urgent product recall announcements (PROD-005), the On-Call Knowledge Manager is authorized to bypass standard SME review and publish an Emergency Article instantly. The emergency article holds an expiration timer of 72 hours, during which formal retrospective review and approval must be completed.
* Rejection of Sub-Standard Submissions: Submissions failing the Quality Assurance Checklist or exhibiting incomplete cross-references are rejected with detailed reviewer notes. The author has 3 business days to resubmit corrected drafts.
Practical Examples
Scenario 1: Major Article Revision Following a Policy Change
1. Executive Committee updates the Master Replacement Policy (REF-003).
2. Executing Rule 3, the Knowledge Manager places associated knowledge articles on temporary deprecation hold (STAGE-SPD) within 2 hours.
3. Assigned author drafts a major revision (v2.0.0), updating return shipping fee rules and NEKB cross-references.
4. Legal Reviewer and Knowledge Manager approve the major revision within 48 hours, publishing the updated article to live agent desktops and Help Center channels.
Scenario 2: Merging Duplicate Knowledge Articles
1. Quality Assurance audit identifies two separate articles in know.novacart.internal covering address modification procedures during transit, causing agent confusion.
2. Knowledge Manager initiates a consolidation workflow: designates one master article, integrates unique edge cases from the secondary article, and updates the version to v1.2.0.
3. The secondary duplicate article is transitioned to STAGE-ARC (Archived Vault) and cross-links redirect to the master article.
Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
Q1: What is the difference between a major revision (v2.0.0) and a minor revision (v1.1.0)?
Q1 Answer:
A major revision involves a structural rewrite, workflow redesign, or major master policy replacement (LEG-005), requiring full cross-functional review and executive re-approval. A minor revision involves adding new troubleshooting steps or expanding edge cases that do not alter core procedural intent, requiring SME review only (Refer to Version Control Matrix).
Q2: What happens to an article when it is placed on a deprecation hold (STAGE-SPD)?
Q2 Answer:
When an article is placed on a deprecation hold due to reported drift or an underlying policy update, it is temporarily hidden from active search and agent CRM views to prevent the execution of outdated instructions. The article undergoes rapid revision and re-approval before being restored to active status (STAGE-ACT) (Refer to Article Status Matrix).
Q3: How long are retired knowledge articles retained in the system?
Q3 Answer:
When an article is permanently retired, it transitions to STAGE-ARC (Archived Vault). In accordance with our enterprise governance standards, all retired article version histories, review sign-off logs, and QA evaluations are retained securely in immutable WORM storage for 7 years to support operational audits and legal compliance (Refer to SEC-003, SEC-006).
