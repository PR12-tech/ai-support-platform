Purpose
This document defines the official AI Knowledge Retrieval & Response Governance Policy of NovaCart Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "NovaCart"). It establishes standardized governance principles, business rules, ownership responsibilities, quality standards, compliance requirements, and operational controls governing how artificial intelligence systems retrieve organizational knowledge and generate customer-facing or agent-assist responses using approved enterprise knowledge sources.
As the capstone document (KNOW-006) in the Knowledge Management category of the NovaCart Enterprise Knowledge Base (NEKB), KNOW-006 governs business accountability and knowledge retrieval boundaries for all enterprise AI assistants operating across customer service touchpoints, operating in strict operational alignment with COMP-001 through COMP-004, SUP-001 through SUP-005, ORD-001 through ORD-005, SHIP-001 through SHIP-007, PAY-001 through PAY-006, REF-001 through REF-006, PROD-001 through PROD-006, SEC-001 through SEC-006, LEG-001 through LEG-006, and KNOW-001 through KNOW-005.
Scope
This policy applies universally across all customer-facing AI conversational agents, chatbot widgets, self-service help center search assistants, and internal agent-assist co-pilot tools deployed across NovaCart's operating regions (India, Singapore, and the United Arab Emirates).
Coverage Includes
* Identification, authorization, and tiering of approved enterprise knowledge sources accessible by AI retrieval systems.
* Governance principles and business rules governing AI response generation, factual grounding, and brand tone.
* Mandatory human oversight thresholds, seamless escalation triggers to human Customer Support Representatives (SUP-001), and uncertainty handling.
* Knowledge validation standards, conflict resolution protocols when handling disparate source documents, and drift detection.
* Response quality assurance metrics, helpfulness evaluations, compliance audits, and risk management frameworks.
* Continuous improvement loops, feedback integration from customer interactions, and audit record retention.
Out of Scope
* Technical software architecture, vector database designs, embedding generation, large language model weights, prompt engineering syntax, API protocols, or software engineering code repositories.
* Non-AI automated transactional workflows (e.g., automated email notifications, static payment gateway routers).
AI Knowledge Retrieval & Response Governance Overview
Artificial intelligence assistants represent a powerful extension of NovaCart's customer experience and support infrastructure, providing rapid, 24/7 self-service engagement. However, utilizing AI to assist customers introduces unique operational risks, including potential misinformation, factual drift, unauthorized data disclosure, or unverified policy interpretations.
To safeguard brand trust, protect customer interests, and ensure strict regulatory compliance (LEG-005), NovaCart treats enterprise AI as an official business capability operating under rigorous knowledge governance. AI systems are strictly restricted to retrieving information from vetted, approved enterprise knowledge repositories (know.novacart.internal, faq.novacart.internal, and master NEKB policies) and generating responses that adhere to established enterprise standards.

Core Governance Principles
1. Strict Source Grounding: AI systems must retrieve and generate responses exclusively from validated, approved enterprise knowledge sources. Speculative generation, unverified assumptions, or drawing from external public internet data are strictly prohibited.
2. Transparent Uncertainty Handling: When customer inquiries fall outside approved knowledge boundaries, or when source material is ambiguous or conflicting, the AI system must transparently acknowledge its limitation and seamlessly escalate the customer to a human support agent (SUP-001).
3. Continuous Human Accountability: Department owners and Knowledge Managers remain fully accountable for the accuracy of knowledge sources retrieved by AI, while Customer Experience leads oversee response quality and escalation efficacy.
4. Zero Tolerance for Compliance Drift: AI-generated responses must strictly reflect published master enterprise policies (e.g., REF-001 for returns, PROD-003 for warranties) without modifying financial thresholds, return windows, or legal disclaimers.
Governance Principles
NovaCart's AI operational framework operates under four foundational governance rules:
* Approved Repository Exclusivity: AI retrieval mechanisms are hard-constrained to query solely active (STAGE-ACT) master documents within the NEKB, approved FAQ repositories, and published SOPs. Deprecated (STAGE-SPD) or archived (STAGE-ARC) documents are automatically blocked from retrieval.
* Role-Based Interaction Boundaries: Customer-facing AI assistants are restricted to accessing public-facing content (KNOW-PUB), while internal agent-assist AI tools may access both public and restricted internal operational guides (KNOW-INT), respecting strict data privacy and need-to-know boundaries (SEC-003).
* Immutable Audit Logging: All AI retrieval queries, source documents cited, and generated response outputs must be recorded permanently in immutable audit logs (audit.novacart.internal) to support quality audits and regulatory reviews (SEC-006).
* Proactive Risk Mitigation: High-risk customer inquiries involving financial disputes, account security breaches, physical safety hazards, or regulatory complaints bypass AI generation entirely and route directly to specialized human queues.

	AI Response Generation Principles
AI systems generating customer-facing or agent-assist responses must adhere to five behavioral standards:
1. Factual Grounding: Every factual claim, policy statement, or procedural instruction delivered by the AI must trace directly to an active source document in an approved repository.
2. Plain-Language Clarity: Responses must be formulated in concise, professional, empathetic, and customer-accessible language, avoiding internal database identifiers, system tags, or technical jargon.
3. Prohibition of Unauthorized Commitments: AI systems are strictly prohibited from promising unauthorized financial refunds, overriding store credit limits, granting policy exceptions, or committing to delivery guarantees outside published SLAs (SHIP-001, REF-005).
4. Consistent Tone and Branding: AI persona behavior must remain helpful, respectful, and aligned with NovaCart's brand voice across all digital channels and operating regions.
5. Mandatory Policy Linkage: When answering policy-related questions (e.g., return windows or warranty coverage), the AI must provide a direct reference link to the governing master policy document.

	Knowledge Validation & Trust Requirements
To ensure that AI systems retrieve dependable information, knowledge assets undergo strict pre-retrieval validation:
* Active Status Verification: Automated retrieval engines query exclusively documents flagged with STAGE-ACT status. Drafts (STAGE-DFT), reviews (STAGE-REV), deprecation holds (STAGE-SPD), and archived records (STAGE-ARC) are cryptographically excluded from retrieval indexes.
* Handling Conflicting Information: If retrieval queries return conflicting information across different approved sources (e.g., an FAQ providing a condensed summary that conflicts with a master policy document), the AI retrieval governance rules dictate that Master Policies (Tier 1) take absolute operational precedence over summaries or operational SOPs.
* Drift Detection & Automatic Withdrawal: When a master policy is revised or placed on deprecation hold (STAGE-SPD), the associated retrieval embeddings or indexing links must be purged within 2 hours to prevent the AI from serving outdated guidance.

	Compliance & Regulatory Requirements
AI knowledge retrieval and response generation operate under strict regulatory and legal oversight:
* Data Privacy Protection: AI retrieval systems must never ingest, store, or output Class 3 (Confidential) or Class 4 (Restricted) Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as full credit card numbers, passwords, or government ID scans in chat transcripts or logs (SEC-003).
* Regulatory Alignment: AI-generated responses regarding consumer rights, warranties, and data protection must comply fully with statutory consumer protection mandates across India, Singapore, and the United Arab Emirates (LEG-005).
* Audit Retainability: All interaction logs, AI retrieval traces, and source document citations must be retained in immutable WORM storage for a mandatory period of 7 years (SEC-003, SEC-006).
Risk Management & Exception Handling
* AI Hallucination Containment: If quality audits or customer feedback identify an instance of AI hallucination or inaccurate response generation, the Knowledge Manager must place the affected knowledge topic on temporary retrieval freeze within 1 hour, auditing source articles in know.novacart.internal before re-indexing.
* Emergency System Override: During severe technical outages, security breaches (SEC-005), or product recalls (PROD-005), the Chief Operating Officer or Head of Knowledge Management may execute an AI_EMERGENCY_SHUTDOWN command, immediately disabling public AI chatbot widgets and routing 100% of customer inquiries to human support queues (SUP-001).
Continuous Monitoring & Improvement
NovaCart embeds continuous improvement into its AI knowledge governance framework:
1. Daily Response Sampling: Quality Assurance specialists randomly audit $2\%$ of AI-generated customer conversation transcripts against the Response Quality Checklist.
2. Customer Feedback Integration: Customer helpfulness ratings and post-chat CSAT scores linked to AI interactions are analyzed weekly to identify low-performing knowledge topics.
3. Monthly Governance Review: The Knowledge Management and AI Operations teams review retrieval error rates, escalation frequencies, and audit findings, feeding insights directly back into the Knowledge Article Lifecycle review cycle (KNOW-005).

	Business Rules
Rule 1: Mandatory Approved Source Exclusivity
AI systems may retrieve knowledge exclusively from Tier 1 through Tier 4 approved enterprise sources. Querying external public internet data, unverified third-party forums, or unapproved documents is strictly prohibited.
Rule 2: Absolute Master Policy Precedence in Conflicts
When AI retrieval queries return conflicting information across different knowledge sources, Master Policies (Tier 1 NEKB documents) take absolute operational precedence over operational SOPs or FAQ summaries.
Rule 3: Mandatory Human Escalation upon Uncertainty
If an AI assistant encounters customer inquiries falling outside approved knowledge boundaries, or if retrieval confidence scores fall below acceptable thresholds, the system must immediately initiate a seamless transfer to a human support agent (SUP-001).
Rule 4: Mandatory 7-Year Audit Retention for AI Interactions
All AI conversational transcripts, retrieval telemetry traces, source document citations, and human escalation records must be retained in immutable WORM storage for a mandatory period of 7 years (SEC-003, SEC-006).
Practical Examples
Scenario 1: AI Answering a Customer Question Using Approved Knowledge
1. A customer asks the storefront chatbot: "What is the return window for a defective smartphone?".
2. The AI assistant queries approved Tier 1 knowledge sources and retrieves the Master Refund Policy (REF-001).
3. The AI generates a precise, grounded response: "Defective smartphones are eligible for a full refund or replacement within 7 days of delivery. For more details, review our Master Refund Policy [REF-001]."
4. The interaction is logged successfully in audit.novacart.internal.
Scenario 2: AI Handling Uncertainty and Escalating to Human Support
1. A customer asks an AI chatbot a complex question regarding a disputed international customs fee for an overseas shipment.
2. The AI queries approved knowledge sources but finds no matching specification covering international customs dispute rulings.
3. Recognizing uncertainty, the AI adheres to Rule 3: "I want to ensure you get the exact right answer regarding your customs fee. Let me connect you immediately with a Customer Support Specialist who can assist you."
4. The conversation routes seamlessly to the frontline support queue with full context transfer (SUP-001).
Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
Q1: What sources of information are NovaCart's AI assistants authorized to use when answering customer questions?
Answer: NovaCart's AI assistants are strictly restricted to retrieving information from vetted, approved enterprise knowledge sources (Tiers 1–4). This includes master enterprise policies (NEKB), public storefront FAQs (faq.novacart.internal), verified product catalog specifications (catalog.novacart.internal), and internal operational SOPs (sop.novacart.internal). Querying the public internet or unapproved documents is strictly prohibited (Refer to Approved Knowledge Source Matrix).
Q2: What happens if an AI assistant encounters a question it cannot answer or encounters conflicting information?
Q2 Answer:
In accordance with Rule 2 and Rule 3, when facing conflicting information, Master Policies (Tier 1) take absolute precedence. When facing uncertainty or missing information, the AI is programmed to transparently acknowledge its limitation and immediately transfer the customer to a human Customer Support Representative without guessing or hallucinating answers (Refer to Escalation Responsibility Matrix).
Q3: How long are records of AI customer conversations and knowledge retrievals retained?
Q3 Answer:
All AI conversational transcripts, retrieval telemetry traces, and human escalation records are retained securely in immutable WORM storage for 7 years in accordance with our enterprise security and data retention standards (Refer to SEC-003, SEC-006).
