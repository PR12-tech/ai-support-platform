Purpose
This document defines the official Customer Support Knowledge Base Governance Policy of NovaCart Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "NovaCart"). It establishes standardized governance principles, asset ownership responsibilities, content creation standards, multi-tier approval workflows, review schedules, quality assurance frameworks, and business rules governing the creation, maintenance, publication, review, and retirement of customer support knowledge base content across internal agent tools and public customer Help Center channels.
As the foundational document (KNOW-001) in the Knowledge Management category of the NovaCart Enterprise Knowledge Base (NEKB), KNOW-001 governs the content lifecycle within the Knowledge Management System (know.novacart.internal), operating in strict operational alignment with COMP-001 through COMP-004, SUP-001 through SUP-005, ORD-001 through ORD-005, SHIP-001 through SHIP-007, PAY-001 through PAY-006, REF-001 through REF-006, PROD-001 through PROD-006, SEC-001 through SEC-006, and LEG-001 through LEG-006.
Scope
This policy applies universally across all customer support knowledge assets, internal agent reference guides, public Help Center self-service articles, chatbot response repositories, macro templates, and operational SOP guides utilized by NovaCart across India, Singapore, and the United Arab Emirates.
Coverage Includes
* Structural architecture and categorization of internal and public knowledge assets.
* Explicit roles, permissions, and ownership responsibilities for knowledge content.
* Content authoring, formatting, terminology, and style guidelines.
* Multi-stage review, verification, and publication approval workflows.
* Scheduled review frequencies, drift detection, and content maintenance protocols.
* Content deprecation, archiving, restoration, and permanent retirement workflows.
* Quality assurance sampling, feedback loop integration, and performance metrics.
Out of Scope
* Technical software architecture, database schema design, and vector indexing algorithms of the knowledge platform.
* Broad corporate communications, public relations press releases, and investor relations collateral.
Knowledge Base Governance Overview
An accurate, up-to-date, and well-structured Knowledge Base is critical to operational excellence at NovaCart. It serves as the single source of truth for Customer Support Representatives (CSRs) resolving customer inquiries in crm.novacart.internal (SUP-001) and powers self-service resolution for customers accessing public storefront Help Centers. Uncontrolled, inaccurate, or outdated knowledge content introduces severe operational risk, customer friction, and compliance failures.

Core Governance Principles
1. Single Source of Truth: All support operations, macros, agent scripts, and self-service articles must derive directly from validated, published NEKB documents. Shadow documentation and localized desktop cheatsheets are strictly prohibited.
2. Clear Ownership & Accountability: Every knowledge article must be assigned an explicit Subject Matter Expert (SME) author and an Accountable Department Owner responsible for content accuracy and lifecycle maintenance.
3. Accuracy Before Velocity: Speed of publication must never compromise accuracy, compliance, or structural quality. All content must complete mandatory multi-stage review prior to public or agent-facing release.
4. Continuous Quality & Drift Control: Knowledge content is continuously audited against operational telemetry, policy revisions (LEG-005), customer feedback scores, and product updates to prevent information drift.
Knowledge Base Structure
To ensure rapid searchability and logical navigation, content within know.novacart.internal is organized into two distinct operational access domains:
1. Internal Agent Knowledge Base (KNOW-INT)
Restricted to authenticated NovaCart staff and tier-assigned support representatives (crm.novacart.internal). Contains detailed operational SOPs (SUP-001), step-up verification steps (SEC-001), system override procedures (REF-005), fraud screening flags (SEC-004), and escalation matrices (SUP-003).
2. Public Customer Help Center (KNOW-PUB)
Publicly accessible self-service articles written in customer-facing, accessible language. Contains shipping policies (SHIP-001), return eligibility guides (REF-001), product setup manuals (PROD-004), warranty guides (PROD-003), and platform Terms & Conditions explanations (LEG-001).

	Content Creation Standards
Knowledge authors must adhere to standardized formatting, structural, and language requirements to ensure clarity, consistency, and searchability:
Standardized Authoring Guidelines
* Title Structure: Titles must be explicit, task-oriented, and prefixed by functional category (e.g., [Orders] How to Request a Shipping Address Change or [Refunds] Processing Goodwill Wallet Credits).
* NEKB Cross-Referencing: Articles must explicitly reference foundational policy documents (e.g., "In accordance with REF-001 Section 4..." or "Per SEC-001 Level 2 verification requirements...").
* Structural Template: Articles must follow the standardized NEKB article format:
   1. Header & Metadata: Document ID, Category, Primary Owner, Target Audience (Internal/Public).
   2. Summary / Overview: A concise 2-sentence explanation of the article's purpose.
   3. Step-by-Step Procedure: Numbered, action-oriented workflow steps.
   4. Exceptions & Edge Cases: Bulleted list of non-standard conditions and resolution paths.
   5. Related Policies: Bulleted cross-references to NEKB master policies.
* Tone & Style: Professional, concise, gender-neutral, and free of internal jargon when authoring public Help Center content.

	Knowledge Publication Process
Upon receiving final approval in know.novacart.internal, the system executes automated publication tasks:
1. Versioning & Metadata Binding: The engine assigns a semantic version number (v1.0.0), attaches search taxonomy tags, sets visibility permissions (Internal vs. Public), and binds the author and approver digital signatures.
2. Search Vector Indexing: The article content is indexed immediately across internal agent desktop search bars (crm.novacart.internal) and public Help Center search engines.
3. Change Log Generation: System logs a publication entry in audit.novacart.internal for compliance tracking (SEC-006).

	Retirement Criteria & Restoration
* Retirement Criteria: An article is permanently archived (STAGE-ARC) when the underlying product is discontinued (PROD-001), the governing policy is retired, or duplicate documentation is consolidated.
* Restoration Workflow: Archived articles may be restored to active status (STAGE-ACT) only by submitting a formal revision request in know.novacart.internal, re-completing the full review and approval pipeline.

	Business Rules
Rule 1: Mandatory Policy Alignment Mandate
No knowledge base article, macro template, or agent script may contradict, expand, or restrict the provisions of a published NEKB master policy. In the event of a conflict between a knowledge article and a master policy document (e.g., KNOW-001 vs. REF-001), the master policy document takes absolute precedence.
Rule 2: Single Active Article Rule for Operational Topics
know.novacart.internal must maintain exactly one (1) active master article per distinct operational topic. Creating duplicate, overlapping, or regional variant articles for the same core process is strictly prohibited. Category variations must be handled within designated sections of the master article.
Rule 3: Immediate Deprecation upon Policy Update
When a foundational enterprise policy is revised (e.g., an update to REF-001 or PAY-003), the Knowledge Manager must place all associated knowledge articles on temporary deprecation hold (STAGE-SPD) within 2 hours of policy publication until updated drafts are approved and published.
Rule 4: Mandatory 7-Year Archive Retention
All retired knowledge articles (STAGE-ARC), draft revision histories, approval sign-off logs, and QA evaluation records must be retained in immutable WORM storage for a mandatory period of 7 years (SEC-003, SEC-006).
Exception Handling
* Emergency Publication Protocol: During critical platform outages, active security incidents (SEC-005), or urgent product recall announcements (PROD-005), the On-Call Knowledge Manager is authorized to bypass standard SME review and publish an Emergency Advisory Article instantly. The advisory holds an expiration timer of 72 hours, during which formal retrospective review and approval must be completed.
* Rejection of Sub-Standard Submissions: Submissions failing the Quality Assurance Checklist or exhibiting incomplete policy cross-references are rejected in know.novacart.internal with detailed reviewer notes. The author has 3 business days to resubmit corrected drafts.
Practical Examples
Scenario 1: Updating Knowledge Content Following a Policy Revision
1. Executive Committee approves Version 2.0.0 of the Master Replacement Policy (REF-003).
2. Executing Rule 3, the Knowledge Manager places associated internal agent articles and public Help Center guides on STAGE-SPD deprecation hold within 1 hour.
3. Assigned SME author updates the knowledge drafts in know.novacart.internal, updating procedural steps, refund thresholds, and NEKB cross-references.
4. Legal Reviewer approves the draft within 12 hours. Knowledge Manager publishes updated Version 2.0.0 articles to live agent desktops and Help Center channels.
Scenario 2: Emergency Publication of a Platform Security Advisory
1. CSIRT declares a SEV-1 security incident involving third-party payment gateway maintenance (SEC-005).
2. CSIRT Lead drafts an emergency support advisory explaining temporary checkout alternative routes.
3. Invoking the Emergency Publication Protocol, the On-Call Knowledge Manager verifies the content and executes immediate publication (STAGE-ACT) to agent desktops within 10 minutes.
4. CSIRT and Legal complete retrospective approval within 24 hours.
Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
Q1: How do I report an incorrect or outdated article in the Knowledge Base?
Answer: Customer Support Representatives can click the Flag Article for Review button directly inside crm.novacart.internal while viewing any article. Select the specific reason (e.g., Outdated Policy Reference or Unclear Procedure), enter a brief explanation, and click submit. The article is immediately routed to the SME author and Knowledge Manager for review (Refer to SUP-001).
Q2: What is the difference between an internal knowledge article and a public Help Center article?
Answer: Internal articles (KNOW-INT) are accessible only to authenticated NovaCart staff and contain detailed operational workflows, step-up verification steps (SEC-001), override rules (REF-005), and system navigation guides. Public articles (KNOW-PUB) are customer-facing self-service guides written in accessible language, focusing on general policies, order tracking, returns, and product setup (Refer to LEG-001).
Q3: Can individual support teams create their own localized macro templates or cheatsheets?
Answer: No. Executing Rule 1, all agent macros, scripts, and SOP reference guides must be published centrally within know.novacart.internal. Localized cheatsheets, personal desktop notes, or unapproved macros introduce compliance risks and are strictly prohibited (Refer to SUP-001, LEG-005).
